# Porch Light: toward Caamp

Sean, 2026-11-13: "make something sound like the band Caamp? We may need an actual guitar". An original
indie-folk song (`fiddle/tunes/porch_light.abc`): the new guitar (`fiddle/guitar.py`) strummed under the
banjo flat-picked on modern steel strings (`banjo.arrange_flatpick`), a little tambourine, no fiddle, no
singer (the banjo carries the hook). `song.mp3`, `guitar.mp3`, `banjo.mp3`. Caamp's banjoist plays with a
flat pick, "like a guitar" (Atwood Magazine and The Bluegrass Situation interviews).

Full band (`song_full.mp3`): guitar, banjo, upright bass, drums (`--with=guitar,banjo,bass,drums --levels=banjo:0,guitar:-2,bass:-3,drums:-6`); `bass.mp3`, `drums.mp3` alone.
