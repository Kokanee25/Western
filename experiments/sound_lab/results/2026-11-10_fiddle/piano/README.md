# The saloon piano (#790)

Our own model (`fiddle/piano.py`): `oh_susanna.mp3`, `turkey_in_the_straw.mp3`, at -18 LUFS. Round 1,
not yet against a recording. What was measured on the way (fiddle/README.md "The saloon piano"):
middle C's partials 5-7 / 10-12 under the fundamental: soft -21/-31, ordinary -11/-22, hard -9/-14
(Fletcher's ~-20/-35, -10/-25, -5/-18); the first try was 30-40 dB too dull (a smooth push excites no
upper partials). Keys voiced even (the first pass, one strike a key with random phases, was 13 dB
out at worst); single notes within ~14 cents; above 4 kHz -25 dB; the tune 2.6-4.9 dB over the oom-pah.
