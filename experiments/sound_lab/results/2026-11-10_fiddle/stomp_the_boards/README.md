# Stomp the Boards: the banger

Sean, 2026-11-15: "a real banger - something they would stomp their feet to and dance, up beat and fast, it
doesn't need to fit the era". An original stomp-clap song (`fiddle/tunes/stomp_the_boards.abc`): A, 144 bpm,
intro / verse / chorus / verse / chorus / breakdown / build / last chorus twice / the hit, ~104 s. Arranged by
`fiddle/sections.py` (who plays in which bars: `tunes/stomp_the_boards.sections.json`): fiddle, guitar (drive),
banjo (modern open-back, rolls), bass (every beat), drums (four on the floor, `drums.CLAP` hand claps on two
and four). Loudness by section: intro -20, verses -18, choruses ~-17.3, breakdown -21.8, build -18.6 LUFS.
`song.mp3`, `stomp.mp3` (the drums and claps alone).
