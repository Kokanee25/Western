# Payday Night (#790)

An original reel for Salt Creek's saloon band (Sean, 2026-11-12: "a real banger ... original ... high energy
dance music ... an original song the band plays"). Composed here (`fiddle/tunes/payday_night.abc`), not
from any book: A major, cut time, AABB twice, half note = 120, ~65 s. `payday_night.json` by `abc_tune.py
--tempo=120 --times=2`. `band.mp3` (fiddle, banjo gut take 3, bones, tambourine), `band_piano.mp3` (and the
saloon piano, take 2; `band.py --with=banjo,bones,tambourine,piano`), `fiddle.mp3` alone. The fiddle 98%
of 248 notes within 30 cents; the banjo's strikes a median 8 ms from the fiddle's strokes.
