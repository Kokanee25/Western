# Eastbound Train (#790's follow-on)

An original song for Salt Creek (Sean, 2026-11-13: "an absolute banger as good as Constant Sorrow"): a
lonesome mountain song in that spirit, its own words and tune (`fiddle/tunes/eastbound_train.abc`, words in
its W: lines): verse and chorus three times, G, ~62 half notes a minute, ~94 s. No singer: the fiddle sings
the line. `band_rolls.mp3` (fiddle, modern open-back banjo in three-finger rolls, bones;
`band.py --strings=modern --banjo-style=rolls --with=banjo,bones`), `band_period.mp3` (fiddle, gut banjo
clawhammer, bones, tambourine), `fiddle.mp3`. The fiddle 95% of 213 notes within 30 cents.
