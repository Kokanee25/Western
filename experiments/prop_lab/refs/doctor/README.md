## Notes

- Not found on Commons in the time: a plain white (undecorated) ironstone set, and a separate
  picture of a 19th-century Nélaton probe or bullet forceps (the Met's bullet forceps are 17th-18th
  century). Sharp & Smith's 1889 surgical catalogue (Internet Archive Book Images on Commons) has
  instrument cases and an operating chair if more engravings are wanted.
- The two museum office/bedroom displays are reconstructions; use them for the furniture's form,
  not as proof of an 1882 date.
- Hosts: `api.wellcomecollection.org` and `thumb.wikimedia.org` are refused by this workspace's
  network policy. `upload.wikimedia.org` answers originals and non-standard thumbnail widths with
  HTTP 429 (retry-after 600); the standard thumbnail widths (500, 960 px) download.
