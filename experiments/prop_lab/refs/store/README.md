## What they'd change in G1 (and what G2 does about it)

- **The shelves are full.** Every board in all three photographs is packed end to end with tins and
  boxes standing label-out, two or three high, and more cartons stacked along the shelving's top to
  the ceiling. G1's few jars and bottles a board read as empty shelves: G2 fills each board with rows
  of labelled tins and boxes (one kit prop a row, its goods drawn from one baked model) and stacks
  cartons on the shelving's top.
- **Things hang from the ceiling**: a shaded lamp, tins on wires. G2 hangs the kit's lamps.
- **The coffee mill and the counter showcase** are the store's landmarks at the counter's end (1895).
- **Counter scales with pans** hung from the beam (Georgia, 1899): the store's scales as G2 bakes them.
- **The stove** is a pot-bellied round one in both photographs, where G1 has the saloon's box stove:
  for Sean (the README's "a pot-bellied stove if Sean wants one").
