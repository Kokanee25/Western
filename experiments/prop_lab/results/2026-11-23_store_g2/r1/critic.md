# Blind critic: general store interior, G2 round r1

## 1. Five biggest differences (most important first)

1. **Both renders: the room is empty.** The paintings fill every wall from floor to ceiling with goods, and both counters are covered with jars, tins and stock. In the renders most of the wall area is bare board-and-batten. There is one stocked shelf unit in the far right corner (shop.png) and two on the right (counter.png). The left wall in shop.png has a dark, nearly bare shelf frame, and the left wall in counter.png has none. The room reads as a warehouse or hall, not a crowded store.
2. **Both renders: the plan reads wrong.** The target has two long counters running front to back, one along each side, with a narrow aisle between them and the stove in the middle. In the renders the counters are short, set far back against the side walls, and plain. A forest of white posts with brackets fills a huge open floor. The space looks about twice as wide and as empty as the painted one.
3. **Both renders: the ceiling and the light are wrong.** The target has a dark exposed-joist ceiling, warm amber light and deep shadow. The renders have a pale grey-green beadboard ceiling (vaulted in counter.png), cool grey walls and flat even light. The mood is closer to a modern barn conversion.
4. **counter.png: behind-the-counter details are missing.** The target shows a pigeonhole letter/post wall, a ledger book open on the counter, an inkwell and pen, a cash box, a rolling library ladder and a stair rail. None of these appear. The viewpoint is from the open floor rather than over a counter top.
5. **shop.png: the goods are on the wrong furniture.** The few goods sit on a free-standing trestle table in the middle of the floor (tins and folded cloth) and in loose piles of crates and sacks. They are not on the counters. The left counter in the target, with its candy jars, scales and coffee mill, has no equivalent. The plain flat-front counters have no turned or panelled detail and nothing on top.

## 2. Verdicts

| Item | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Shelf stock (tins, boxes, cases) | CLOSE (sparse) | Coloured tins and boxes fill 2–3 tall units on the right; the rest of the walls are bare |
| Boots and hats | NOT SEEN | None visible in either render |
| Hardware (kegs, tools, rope, oil cans) | DIFFERENT | Small kegs and crocks near the back wall only; no tools, rope or cans hung on walls |
| Tinware | NOT SEEN | No hanging pots, pans or pails |
| Counter showcase | CLOSE | A glass-topped case shows at the right counter's front end (counter.png); it is small and hard to read |
| Coffee mill | CLOSE | A red double-wheel mill sits on the right counter (shop.png); it reads well but is the only thing on that counter |
| Counter scales | CLOSE | A balance scale stands on the left counter (counter.png); it is small and lone |
| Hanging lamps | CLOSE | Thin pendant lamps hang from the ceiling; they are small, unlit and lost against the pale ceiling |
| Counters | DIFFERENT | Short, plain, orange flat-panel boxes against the walls, not long panelled counters flanking an aisle |
| Wall shelving | DIFFERENT | Floor-to-ceiling shelving on only part of one side; the left wall is bare or nearly empty |
| Bolts of cloth | CLOSE (weak) | Folded cloth stacks lie on the trestle table; no upright bolts on shelves |
| Barrels and sacks | CLOSE | Barrels, sacks and crates are present but scattered loosely across the floor |
| Stove and chairs | CLOSE | A small stove and chairs sit at the back (shop.png); the stove is squat and far off, not the tall central pot-belly with its stovepipe |

## 3. Still obviously missing

- Goods on every wall and on top of both counters (density is the main gap)
- Boots, hats, hanging tinware and hung tools or rope
- Candy jars, the post pigeonholes, the ledger and cash box, the library ladder
- The tall stovepipe up through the middle of the room
- The dark joisted ceiling and warm lamp-lit tone
- Long counters set close to each other to make a narrow central aisle
