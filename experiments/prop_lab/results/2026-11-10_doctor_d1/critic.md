# Blind critic: doctor's office props, d1 (2026-11-10)

Judged from the five renders, the two concept panels (surgery, sick room) and the period references
only. The rooms are very dark in the day renders (surgery.png, wash.png, sick_room.png): the
examining table is close to a silhouette at 9:30 am and the washstand's frame can't be seen at all,
so those two are judged mostly from surgery_night.png and the brightened crops.

## 1. Prop by prop

| Prop | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Examining table | **CLOSE** | The right idea: a wooden table with a padded top in two sections and a raised head, matching the target's plain table. But the pad is one smooth orange-tan slab with no buttons, seams, creases or wear, and the base is a plain box apron over an open shelf where the 1880 catalogue and the Remick table have banks of drawers. It reads as **new and clean**, like a modern massage bench in stained pine. |
| Iron cot (x2) | **CLOSE** frame / **DIFFERENT** bedding | The black iron frame with vertical spindles and ball finials matches the target and the 1864-65 wards well. The bedding is the problem: mattress, blanket and pillow are hard rectangular blocks in one flat tan, with no sag, drape over the sides, ticking or rumple, and the "pillow" is a stack of three flat boards. **Toy-like**, like doll's-house bedding. |
| Washstand | **DIFFERENT** (can't be judged well) | All you can see is a dark, low side table with one pull. The target's washstand has a splashback, a towel and a soap dish, and the MOTAT stand has a turned frame, a drawer and a marble or cloth top. Here nothing of the stand reads as a washstand: it's a generic small table. |
| Basin and pitcher | **CLOSE** | The silhouette is right (a tall pitcher with a spout in a wide shallow basin), but the china reads tan or terracotta, not white ironstone, and it's matte with no glaze highlight. The handle is a faceted polygon, the pitcher's surface has visible shading seams, and there's no rim detail. **Clean and slightly toy-like.** |
| Instrument tray | **DIFFERENT** | A pinkish-tan shallow tray holding about four dark, matte, indistinct objects (a black block, a bar, a rod, a ring). Nothing reads as a scalpel, forceps, saw or tenaculum, and there's no steel sheen or ebony handles. It reads as a **desk pen tray**, new and empty. The target has a table full of bright instruments on white cloth. |
| Kerosene reflector lamp | **DIFFERENT** | Both references are wall-hung on a bracket. This is a table lamp with an oversized dark-grey disc behind the chimney, and the disc is not polished tin. The font is a black, low-poly faceted bulb (it reads as ceramic or plastic, not brass or tin). The light glows from the base and foot, not from a flame, and at night (the "one lamp lit") no flame or glowing chimney is visible. The surgery target has a pulley lamp over the table, and the sick room has a plain table lamp. **Reads as a prop placeholder.** |

## 2. The five biggest differences, most important first

1. **The cots' bedding is solid blocks** (sick_room.png, the near cot, lower right, and the far
   cot under the window; also wash.png through the door). The mattress, blanket and pillow are
   one flat tan, with square edges, no drape over the sides and no sag in the middle, and the pillow
   is a stack of slabs like folded lumber. The target's cots have grey wool blankets hanging over
   the rails, plump pillows and visible ticking. It's the most visible fault, and it makes the room
   look like a toy. `critic_crop_1.png`
2. **The lamp is unlit and the wrong kind** (instruments.png, the lamp left of centre;
   surgery_night.png, the side table by the window). There's no flame in the chimney, and a hot
   glow sits at the foot and on the font's side as if the light were placed at the base. The black
   faceted font and the huge dull-grey reflector disc look nothing like the tin wall lamps in the
   references. At night the room's light shows up as round blobs on the wall and floor that don't
   come from a visible flame. `critic_crop_2.png`
3. **The instrument tray holds no recognisable instruments** (instruments.png, the tray right of
   the lamp). It's a pink-beige tray with a few matte dark sticks and a black block. There's no
   blade, saw, forceps, steel glint or ebony handle, nothing like the Weiss or Teufel sets or the
   target's cloth-covered table of shining tools. It reads as stationery. `critic_crop_3.png`
4. **The china isn't white, and the washstand doesn't read** (wash.png, centre). The pitcher and
   basin come out ochre and matte in a near-black corner. The handle is a polygon, the glaze shows
   no highlight and there's no rim band. The stand under them is invisible, with no splashback,
   towel or soap dish as in the target. `critic_crop_4.png`
5. **The examining table is a plain new bench** (surgery_night.png, centre; nearly black in
   surgery.png). Its smooth orange pad has no tufting, buttons, cracking or wear, and its box apron
   has a lower shelf and no drawers. Its edges are crisp and its finish is even. The target's pad is
   worn olive-tan leather with a sagging, creased top, and the 1880 references are buttoned with
   drawer banks. `critic_crop_5.png`

## 3. Overall

The props have the right silhouettes but none of them looks used yet: the iron cot frame and the
table's outline pass, while the bedding, lamp, instrument tray and china read as clean, toy-like
placeholders, a long way from the worn 1882 rooms in the targets.
