# Blind critic: doctor's office props, round r3

What I looked at: the six renders in this folder (downscaled to 800 px, then full-resolution crops),
the two target panels (`building_lab/doctor/sheet/panels/surgery.jpg`, `sick_room.jpg`) and the ten
period references in `prop_lab/refs/doctor/`. The game renders are very dark and the room light
casts everything orange-red. I brightened several crops (x1.3 to x3) so I could see the props, as
noted below. The room's cast affects colour in every view: the white china, the grey blanket and
the walnut all come out the same orange. I've judged colour from the studio preview wherever the
game renders hide it.

## 1. The six props

**Examining table: DIFFERENT.** It reads as a long, low bench with a pad on it. The pad is one hard
slab, and the raised head is a square wedge sitting on top. In the day render the pad is the same
red-brown as the frame, so the leather and the walnut merge into one mass. At night the button
dimples show, but the pad shines like orange vinyl. The target has a dark, worn, olive-brown
leather pad lying soft over a lighter walnut frame with stretchers. The 1880 catalogue table has
sectioned tufting and banks of drawers. Here the drawers are only two small knobs on the apron. It
looks clean and new, and slightly toy-like because every edge is crisp.

**Iron cot (x2): CLOSE in form, DIFFERENT in finish.** The frame is right for 1865-1882: round iron
rails, ball finials, a head end and a foot end (see the studio preview and the cot seen through the
door in `wash.png`). The bedding is what fails. The tick's striped tufts poke through the blanket as
sharp rectangular holes in every game view. The blanket is a stiff board with a hard, straight drop.
The pillow is a smooth orange loaf. The far cot in `sick_room.png` shows no rail at its pillow end,
either because it is hidden in shadow or because it is missing. The target has a sagging grey wool
blanket with soft folds and a dented pillow. Clean and new, with no sag, stain or wear.

**Washstand: CLOSE.** The proportions are right: a top with a low splashback, a drawer, a bottom
shelf and turned-square legs. A plain white pitcher in a basin is also period-correct. Against the
target and the MOTAT stand, it lacks the tall shaped splashback and shelf, and it reads like a plain
modern side table in dark stain. The towel on the side bar is missing from the studio preview: the
bar is empty. In `wash.png` a long cloth hangs from well above the stand down to the bar. That reads
as a curtain, not a folded towel. The china has jagged, stair-stepped outlines that glow orange
(crop 5). Clean and new: no chips, no crazing, no water marks on the top.

**Instrument tray: DIFFERENT.** I see a black tray holding one pair of scissors, a probe, a blade
and a black lidded box. I could not find a napkin, because the instruments lie on bare black. Four
items in a tray this size read as a modern desk or pen tray, not a surgeon's kit. The target shows a
white cloth with many polished steel instruments fanned out. The Weiss and Teufel cases show a saw,
amputation knives with dark handles, forceps and a tourniquet. The scale is unclear too: the tray
looks like a big desk blotter. Very clean, toy-like.

**Kerosene wall bracket lamp: CLOSE in its parts, DIFFERENT in what it shows.** Its parts are right
(a font, a burner collar, a chimney and a disc reflector), but in the game they don't read as those
things. In the studio preview the chimney is an opaque grey vase rather than clear glass. The
reflector is smaller than the chimney and set high behind it, where the references show a large tin
dish behind the flame. By day the lamp is a pale, flat ghost against the window pier, and the
reflector looks like a clear glass ellipse. At night the glow blows out the chimney, and the font
stays a cold blue-grey in a warm room. No bracket arm reads. Clean and new: no soot on the chimney
and no tarnish.

## 2. The five biggest differences a viewer would notice

1. **The cot blankets have holes in them** (`sick_room.png`, the near cot, lower right; also the
   cot seen through the door in `wash.png`). Striped rectangles of tick show through the grey
   blanket in a scattered pattern, like the blanket is torn or the mesh clips the tufts. With the
   rigid slab of a blanket and the loaf pillow, the beds look like painted wooden blocks. The target
   has soft grey wool lying over a sagging tick. Crop: `critic_crop_1.png` (brightened x1.8).

2. **The examining table's pad is not leather** (`surgery_night.png`, centre; same in
   `surgery.png`). The pad and the frame are one red-brown. The pad is a hard slab with a separate
   square wedge, shiny like vinyl at night. The table is a low bench with no drawer bank. The target
   has a dark, worn, olive-brown pad on a lighter walnut frame with stretchers. Crop:
   `critic_crop_2.png` (brightened x1.6).

3. **The instrument tray is nearly empty and has no napkin** (`instruments.png`, the tray on the
   table's right half). A black tray holds four things and a black box, which reads as office
   stationery. The target's white cloth is covered in bright steel instruments. That cloth and steel
   are the only thing that says "doctor" in that view, and it is missing. Crop: `critic_crop_3.png`
   (brightened x1.3).

4. **The wall lamp doesn't read as a tin-reflector lamp** (`surgery_night.png`, top right in the
   window pier). The flame's bloom swallows the chimney. No tin dish shows behind it, and the font
   is cold grey. By day (`surgery.png`, the same spot) it is a pale flat cut-out. A second hot spot
   on the left wall (`surgery_night.png`, top left, about x 380, y 100 at full size) is a glow with
   no lamp drawn at it. Crop: `critic_crop_4.png` (not brightened).

5. **The washstand's towel is missing or wrong, and the china is a cut-out** (`wash.png`, the stand
   at centre-left). There is no towel folded on the side bar. A long cloth drops from above head
   height onto the bar like a curtain. The pitcher and basin have stair-stepped glowing edges and no
   glaze highlight, so they read as flat orange shapes rather than white ironstone. Crop:
   `critic_crop_5.png` (brightened x3).

## 3. Overall verdict

The cot frame and the washstand are the right kind of furniture for 1882. But the bedding,
the table's leather, the empty instrument tray and the lamp all read as clean, new and stand-in, so
none of the six yet looks like the target's worn country doctor's office.
