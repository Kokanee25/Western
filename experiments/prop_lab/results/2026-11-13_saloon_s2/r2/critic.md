# Blind critic, saloon B props S2, round 2

Looked at: the 10 renders in r2/ (5 groups, noon and night), the saloon B panels (bar, dance, gallery, cutaway), saloon-painted.png and saloon-night.png, and the S2 period references in refs/saloon/ (Evans wheel, the two faro pictures, Disston saw patent, Chalfant violin, the two keg photos, the two cookstoves). All viewed downscaled, with crops.

## Verdicts

| Prop | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Wheel of fortune | CLOSE | Spokes, a star hub, pegs round the rim and a pointer at the top are all there, so it reads as a gambling wheel. But the ring is a blank dark navy band with no dice or numbers, and the hub star is small and dull, so from a few steps it looks like a cart wheel in a blue tyre. |
| Faro table | CLOSE | The spade layout, dealing box and case-keeper are right. It is a small four-legged card table with a pale green border round the cloth and not one chip on it, where the references show a long, heavy table covered in stacks. |
| Lookout's high chair | CLOSE | It is taller than the table, which is right. It is a plain ladder-back kitchen chair on long legs, with no footrest, rungs or arms, and at night it turns bright red. |
| Handsaw | CLOSE | Tapered blade with the handle at the wide end reads as a saw. The handle is a flat block with a round knob and no hand hole (the Disston has the closed loop with horns), the teeth do not show, and the blade mirrors the sky blue with a bright white strip along the edge, so it looks like a big kitchen knife. |
| Fiddle | CLOSE | Right outline and colour, with bridge and scroll. The neck is far too long for the body, there are no f-holes, and the strings show as rows of white dots, like frets on a mandolin. At noon it is almost black on the black table. |
| Bow | CLOSE | Long and straight, under the fiddle, as in the Chalfant painting. The stick and the hair sit so far apart they read as two loose rods; there is no frog or tip. |
| Beer kegs | CLOSE | Bulged staves and hoops are right, and the one on the floor is good. They are near-black at noon, all the same size, with hoops the same colour as the wood; the references have pale oak and darker iron hoops. At night they shine glossy red, like varnished furniture. |
| Keg rack | DIFFERENT | The kegs stand upright, and the rack is a low rail in front of two rows of them, the top row seemingly stacked on the bottom. A keg rack holds kegs on their sides in cradles, tap end out, as in the racking room photo. It reads as a fence, not a rack. |
| Cookstove | SAME | Four lids, firebox with a glow, oven door, towel bar, back shelf, cabriole legs and pipe: it reads as an 1880s cookstove at a glance. It is smooth flat black with no cast ornament or nickel trim, where both references are covered in cast panels. |
| Stove board | CLOSE | Right place and size under the stove. It is a plain, even, pale grey slab like concrete or rubber at noon and flat cardboard tan at night, with no metal sheen, rolled edge or embossing. |

## Five biggest differences, most noticeable first

1. **The keg rack is a fence with kegs stood on end.** `kegs_noon.jpg`. The kegs stand upright behind a low rail, the top row on the bottom one. A saloon keg rack lays them on their sides in cradles with the bung and tap facing out. The kegs are also near-black and alike. Crop: `critic_crop_1.png`.
2. **The wheel's ring is blank.** `wheel_noon.jpg`. The ring is plain dark navy with no dice, numbers or bright segments, and the hub star is small and dull. The Evans wheel's whole face is its pale blue dice ring and big gilt star. At night the ring goes black-green and the wheel nearly vanishes into the wall. Crop: `critic_crop_2.png`.
3. **The fiddle is out of proportion.** `saw_fiddle_noon.jpg`. The neck is nearly as long as the body is wide twice over, there are no f-holes, and the strings are rows of white dots that read as frets. It is also almost black at noon, so it disappears on the table. Crop: `critic_crop_3.png`.
4. **The saw looks like a big knife.** `saw_fiddle_noon.jpg`. The handle is a flat block with a knob and no hand hole, the teeth are invisible, and the blade is sky-blue with a white strip along the bottom. The Disston's open loop handle and saw-tooth edge are what make it a saw. Crop: `critic_crop_4.png`.
5. **The faro set looks unplayed and small.** `faro_noon.jpg`. No chips at all, a small card table with a pale green rim, and the lookout's chair is an ordinary kitchen chair on stilts with no footrest. The references' faro tables are long, crowded with chip stacks, with a lookout perched above. Crop: `critic_crop_5.png`.

## Three things that already work

1. The cookstove's shape is right: lids, firebox with a live glow, oven door, towel bar, back shelf, curved legs and pipe on a board. It would sit well in the target's back room.
2. The wheel has the right parts in the right places (spokes, star hub, rim pegs, pointer at the top), so the fixes are colour and markings, not shape.
3. The floor keg and the fiddle's warm brown at night match the worn, lamplit wood of saloon-night.png.

## Clean, new or toy-like

- The cookstove is smooth flat black with no casting, rust or wear; it looks like a modern reproduction.
- The stove board is a spotless even grey slab, like a rubber mat.
- The faro cards are crisp and white, and the table has no chips, so it looks like a shop display.
- The saw blade is a clean flat sheet with a bright white edge strip, like a toy or a knife.
- The fiddle's dotted strings and smooth, hole-less body look like a toy fiddle.
- At night several things shine like varnish: the kegs glossy red, the lookout chair bright red.
