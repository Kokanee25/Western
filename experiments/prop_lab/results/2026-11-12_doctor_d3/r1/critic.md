# Blind critic, doctor's props D3, round 1

Judged from the eight renders in this folder (each viewed once, plus five crops), against the three target panels (front office, sick room, quarters), the Wellcome bag photo and the real 1870s-80s objects. Nothing else was read.

## 1. Prop by prop

| Prop | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Books | **CLOSE** | The right library: tan sheep with red and black labels, dark cloth, a bound journal run, readable period titles. But every book stands dead upright and tight, with no lean, gap or pulled-out volume, and the tops are near level in each row. The journal run is eleven identical clones; the sheep is a blotchy camouflage pattern rather than leather; spines are flat box faces with no rounding or raised bands. The lying piles (shelf, desk, table) are squared up too neatly and the big green book's page block is clean bright white. |
| Bookcase | **DIFFERENT** | Reads as a modern flat-pack shelf unit: plain square boards, no cornice, no moulding, no face frame, no doors, no visible back boards. The wood is one flat saturated orange-brown with almost no grain or wear, far from the target's dark glazed walnut case. It is small and low in the room (front_office.jpg), and there is an odd doubled board under the third shelf. The bottom shelf is nearly empty. |
| Peg rail | **CLOSE** | A believable painted board with turned mushroom pegs, chipped paint, at a sensible height. The board is a flat slab with no chamfer or bead and no visible fixings, and its grey-green paint is dull beside the pegs. Only seen in dim lamp light. |
| Coat | **DIFFERENT** | It hangs like a cardboard cut-out or as if on an invisible hanger: shoulders held square with sharp spiked points, sleeves stiff and parallel, hem a dead straight line, no folds anywhere. A coat hung by its loop on one peg collapses and drapes (see the quarters panel). The cloth is a featureless dark brown with no wool texture, no waist seam and no skirt; it reads as a plain overcoat silhouette, not a frock coat. |
| Hat | **CLOSE** | A recognisable dark felt hat hung on a peg, about the right size. It is a perfectly symmetric lathed shape: a flat circular brim and a smooth low dome, with no crease, no curl, no visible band or ribbon and no felt texture. It reads nearer a bowler disc than a worn hat, and is so dark that it is a silhouette. |
| Doctor's bag | **CLOSE** | Right type (a top-frame Gladstone with straps and a handle) and the leather is nicely mottled. The frame is a bright cream, like bone or paint, where the reference has dark leather-covered or dull metal; it is the brightest thing on the bag and looks wrong. The body is a box with vertical sides and a flat top, not the soft bag that bulges and tapers up to the frame; the handle is small and it looks a little small overall. |
| Chamber pot | **CLOSE** | Good shape: bulbous body, flared rim, lid with knob, loop handle, right size. It is only shown in deep shadow, so it reads flat grey with no glaze highlight; white ironstone cannot be confirmed. The handle is a thin plain loop. In blankets.jpg a white shape pokes out from under the cot's hanging blanket and seems to cut into it (probably this pot; could not be confirmed from one view). |
| Folded blankets | **CLOSE** | Right idea (brown with dark end stripes, white with a blue stripe, grey) and the fold layers are modelled. They read as pressed cotton sheets or card, not wool: thin crisp even layers, perfectly matching footprints, smooth unbroken shading, no nap, no sag or soft corners. The pile is very wide for the cot and sits hard against the foot rail. |

## 2. The five most noticeable faults

1. **Coat**, pegs.jpg, critic_crop_2.png: a stiff flat coat shape with square spiked shoulders and a ruler-straight hem, hanging as if a board were inside it; no drape, no folds.
2. **Bookcase**, bookcase.jpg and front_office.jpg, critic_crop_1.png: a plain orange flat-pack shelf with no cornice, moulding or grain; a viewer sees modern furniture, and it is small beside the desk.
3. **Doctor's bag**, pegs.jpg, critic_crop_3.png: a bright cream top frame on a boxy body; the frame colour jumps out and the bag does not slump or taper like the Wellcome bag.
4. **Books**, bookcase.jpg, critic_crop_1.png: regimented rows, eleven identical journal spines, blotchy "camouflage" sheep and flat spines; it looks stamped out, not a used library.
5. **Folded blankets**, blankets.jpg, critic_crop_5.png: crisp thin identical layers with a smooth surface; they look like folded sheets, not thick wool.

(Smaller: the hat is a featureless symmetric disc and dome in crop 2; the chamber pot in critic_crop_4.png is only seen in shadow, so its white glaze is unproven.)

## 3. What already works

- The book bindings and titles are the right ones for an 1880s American doctor, and the row mix (sheep, cloth, journals) reads correctly at room distance.
- The chamber pot's shape and size, and the bag's type and leather.
- The peg rail with coat, hat and bag under it is a good arrangement and matches the quarters panel's idea.
