# Blind critic, doctor's props D3, round 2

Seen: the eight renders in this folder (each once, at 900 px), the painted panels `front_office.jpg`,
`sick_room.jpg`, `quarters.jpg`, and the Wellcome bag photo. Nothing else was read. Only the eight
props are judged; rooms, light and other furniture are ignored except where the light stops a prop
being read at all.

## 1. Prop by prop

| Prop | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Books (rows and piles) | CLOSE | The best thing here. Tan law-calf spines with red and black labels, legible titles (Gray, Gross, Flint), dark cloth and half-leather beside them: right for an 1880s medical shelf. But every book stands bolt upright, shelves are packed exactly edge to edge with no gap, no leaner, nothing laid across the tops; the third shelf is ten identical clones of one volume at one height. The painting's shelves are ragged: leaning books, gaps, flat stacks. Spines are flat boxes with no rounding and no visible page block at the top. Lying piles (bottom shelf, desk, sitting-room table) are fine, if a little too squarely stacked. |
| Bookcase | DIFFERENT | The panel shows a tall dark walnut glazed bookcase on a cupboard base, taller than the window head. The render is a short (desk-height) open shelf unit in clean orange pine: slab sides, thin plain cornice, a kick board. It reads as a modern flat-pack bookshelf, not a doctor's case. No doors, no glass, no mouldings on the sides, no wear, no dust, one uniform varnish colour. Bottom shelf nearly empty makes it look unfinished. It would pass as a homemade plain shelf, but it is not the target object. |
| Peg rail | SAME | A painted board with turned, mushroom-headed wooden pegs at even spacing, as in the quarters panel. Believable. Only nit: the board is very clean and its edges dead sharp. |
| Coat | DIFFERENT | Reads as a coat standing on an invisible mannequin, not a coat hung on a peg. Shoulders stay full width and square, sleeves stick out from the body in a stiff A with visible polygon kinks at the elbow, the skirt flares out like a bell with a zig-zag hem. A real coat on one peg hangs from its collar: shoulders collapse inward, sleeves drop dead straight, the cloth drapes in long vertical folds (as the painted duster does). It is also near black with no cloth weave, seam or wear visible; in `hall.jpg` it reads as a dark figure against the wall. The lapels, collar and button row are good. |
| Hat | CLOSE | Silhouette of a low-crowned felt hat hung crown-out on a peg is right and matches the panel's placement. But it is a black blob: no hat band, no brim edge binding, no crease or sweat stain, no felt texture, and the brim is too thick and perfectly circular, like a moulded disc. |
| Doctor's bag | CLOSE | Leather surface is good: dark brown, scuffed, mottled. Shape is wrong for the reference: the Wellcome bag is a dome-shouldered Gladstone whose sides lean in to a narrow hinged frame, and it slumps. The render is a rectangular box with vertical sides and a flat top, i.e. a briefcase or satchel. The top frame is a bright tan/orange strip that is the brightest thing on the prop (the reference frame is dark leather-covered with a small lock tab). Handle is a thin wire arch rather than a padded leather grip on two tabs. Looks small on the hall floor. |
| Chamber pot | CLOSE | Form is right: squat round body, wide flat rim, knobbed lid, one ear handle, foot ring. But it never reads as white ironstone: in both shots it is a flat mid-grey with no glaze highlight at all, like unfired clay or grey plastic. The lid sits sunk inside the rim like a casserole lid, and the handle is a thick hook that looks unattached at its lower end. A viewer could take it for a cooking pot or tureen. |
| Folded blankets | CLOSE | Colours and stripes are right (brown wool with dark end stripes, cream with a blue stripe, a grey one below): clearly army/hospital blankets. But they are inflated: each fold is a fat rounded cushion with soft pillow edges, like stacked foam pads or sofa cushions. Wool blankets fold flat with a tight rounded fold on one side and thin loose edges on the other. Surface is smooth as vinyl, no nap or weave. The pile is as wide as the cot and nearly as tall as its foot rail: too big. The grey bottom one merges with the grey bed cover. |

## 2. The five most noticeable faults

1. **The coat hangs like a stiff cut-out on an invisible hanger.** Square full-width shoulders, splayed
   kinked sleeves, bell skirt, near-black with no cloth detail; the hat beside it is the same
   featureless black with no band. Seen in `pegs.jpg` (and as a "figure" in `hall.jpg`). Crop:
   `critic_crop_2.png`.
2. **The bookcase is a short orange open pine shelf, not the tall dark glazed walnut case of the panel,
   and its books are drilled like soldiers.** All upright, no gaps or leaners, a full shelf of ten
   identical volumes, bottom shelf almost empty. Seen in `bookcase.jpg`, `front_office.jpg`,
   `sitting_room.jpg`. Crop: `critic_crop_3.png`.
3. **The blankets are puffed-up cushions.** Fat rounded folds, no wool texture, pile too big for the
   cot. Seen in `blankets.jpg`, `sick_room.jpg`. Crop: `critic_crop_5.png`.
4. **The bag is a box with a bright orange top strip.** Vertical sides and flat top instead of the
   Gladstone's sloped shoulders and narrow dark frame; thin wire handle. Seen in `pegs.jpg`,
   `hall.jpg`. Crop: `critic_crop_1.png`.
5. **The chamber pot is matte grey, not glazed white.** No highlight, sunk casserole lid, hook handle;
   it does not say "ironstone". Seen in `pot.jpg`, `sick_room.jpg`. Crop: `critic_crop_4.png`.

## 3. What already works

- The book spines: label colours, title lettering, the tan calf against dark cloth. Close up they are
  the most convincing prop in the set.
- The peg rail and its turned pegs.
- The coat's collar, lapels and button row as modelling; the hat's placement on the peg.
- The bag's leather surface (scuffs, colour) and its straps.
- The chamber pot's overall silhouette and scale beside the cot.
- The blankets' colours and stripe layout; three different blankets in one pile reads well from across
  the room.
