# Blind critic: the factory's batch 1 (wagons and wheels)

Judged only from the noon and night renders in `build/factory_1/` against the three concept paintings.

## Verdicts

- **Buckboard: CLOSE.** The shape and stance are right, but the box is clean, flat mauve-pink planking and the wheels are pale yellow. It reads as freshly built, not a working rig in a dusty street.
- **Freight wagon: DOESN'T.** It reads as a longer buckboard: the same shallow box, the same light wheels and hubs, no brake, no heavy running gear, no load. Nothing about it says tons.
- **Covered wagon: CLOSE.** The silhouette is right. The canvas is a seamless cream loaf with no bows showing through, no sag between them, no puckered end and no stains. At noon it glows brighter than anything else in the frame.
- **Wagon wheel: CLOSE.** The proportions are believable, but it looks toy-clean. The hub stands up like a grey tin can, and the tyre is speckled like gravel, not iron.
- **Broken wagon wheel: DOESN'T.** The "break" is a neat missing arc with the spokes cut square. There are no splinters, no sprung felloe, no loose pieces on the ground. It reads as a cut-away, not a smashed wheel.

## Five biggest differences (most important first)

1. **Wrong wood colour, too clean.** The paintings' wood is warm, dark, sun-cooked brown with grime in every joint. These boxes are flat lilac-pink with pale tan wheels and no dirt, wear or mud on the lower boards. `buckboard_noon.png` (270,180,610,300); `freight_wagon_noon.png` (240,185,655,300).
2. **Unreadable at night.** The wheels disappear completely, and the wagons are black shapes apart from a grey slab of canvas. In the saloon painting, lamplight catches every rim and edge. `wagon_wheel_night.png` (310,240,650,440); `broken_wagon_wheel_night.png` (320,230,680,450); `covered_wagon_night.png` (220,145,630,420).
3. **Metal doesn't read as metal.** Every tyre is a grey-blue speckle like stone, and the hubs are smooth lilac-grey cans with no hub bands, linch pins or grease. `wagon_wheel_noon.png` (315,250,645,435), hub (455,255,505,325); `buckboard_noon.png` (340,325,390,365).
4. **Missing or wrong construction.** The tongue lies loose on the ground, and nothing connects the wagon to the horse standing inside its outline. The freight wagon has no heft (sides, brake lever, bolsters), and the covered wagon has no bows or drawstring end. `buckboard_noon.png` (600,288,720,310), horse (390,105,545,225); `covered_wagon_noon.png` (220,145,630,285).
5. **The broken wheel's break is too tidy.** It is a clean arc removed and spokes trimmed flat, with no fracture, splinters or debris. The rim is also too thick and blocky for a felloe. `broken_wagon_wheel_noon.png` (520,230,680,420).
