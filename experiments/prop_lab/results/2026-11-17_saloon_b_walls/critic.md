# Saloon B's walls (S5, #1030): blind critic, round 1

## Five biggest differences, ranked

1. **Walls read pale and clean, not dark worked wood.** In back_wall_night, back_bar_night and dressing_night the walls are cream vertical boards with even dark flecks, like painted pine or speckled plaster. The painting and the Seattle photo show dark brown timber or patterned paper with frames, sconces and clutter.
2. **The room is bare and the walls are nearly empty.** In back_wall_night the long back wall holds one small clock, a small deer head and a skull, with metres of plain board between them. The painting crowds every wall with framed pictures, lamps, mounts and a mirror, and the photo has pictures hung edge to edge.
3. **The back bar has no mirror and no depth.** In back_bar_night the arches behind the bottles show flat pale wall. The painting's back bar has a big glowing mirror that doubles the lamps and bottles; this is its main sparkle.
4. **The bottles are identical clones in dead-even rows.** In back_bar_night, right half, every bay holds the same dark bottle at the same spacing, with no labels, no mix of heights or glass colours, and no glasses or decanters. In the painting the shelves are mixed and catch the light.
5. **Light is one flat orange wash with no pools.** back_wall_night is evenly orange from floor to ceiling, and back_wall_day is nearly black indoors. The painting has warm pools around each lamp and darker gaps between them, plus haze.

## The new props

- **Regulator clock:** CLOSE. The case, the dial (Roman numerals, seconds bit, maker's name) and the "REGULATOR" glass read right. But the case is short and squat for a long-case regulator, the top is a plain cap with no carved crest, and in stand_clock_noon a pale paper label floats behind the pendulum glass. In stand_clock_night the hour hand sits on IX at half past.
- **Back-bar bottles:** CLOSE. They are clearly bottles, but all one model and colour, with no labels and no variety (see 4).
- **Rifle on pegs:** CLOSE. In back_bar_night it reads as a long gun, but only as a thin black silhouette high on the cornice. It is too small and dark to see as a rifle, and the pegs can't be seen.
- **Deer head:** CLOSE. It reads as a mounted deer, but it is small for the wall and its antlers are stubby; in clock_night, top right, the neck looks smooth and plasticky.
- **Steer skull:** SAME. In back_wall_night, top left, the long horns and pale skull read at once.
- **Spittoons:** DIFFERENT. The brown lumps on the floor along the bar in back_bar_night and back_wall_night read as rocks or burlap sacks, not brass spittoons. They need a bright brass rim and a flared mouth.
- **Dressing table with tall mirror:** CLOSE. In dressing_night it reads as a small green-legged side table with a dark slot behind it. The mirror is too narrow and doesn't reflect, so it isn't seen as a mirror.
- **Costume rail with dress:** SAME. The red dress on wall pegs reads clearly.
- **Trunk:** SAME. It reads as a banded travelling trunk.
- **Folding screen:** CLOSE. It reads as a three-panel screen, but it is so tall, boxy and flat to the wall that it could be taken for a wardrobe.
