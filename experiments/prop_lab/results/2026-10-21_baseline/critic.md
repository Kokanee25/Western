# Blind critic: card table and chair, A vs B

Two builds can be told apart in every view. I call them **X** (lamp_wide A, lamp_close A, day_wide B, day_close A) and **Y** (lamp_wide B, lamp_close B, day_wide A, day_close B).

**lamp_wide (A=X, B=Y): B better.** B's table shows a wooden rim band round the felt, a heavy dark pedestal and four clear cross feet. A's rim is a thin dark line, its pedestal is lost in shadow and one foot reads as a grey log. A's chairs are bright honey-orange with pale edge highlights. B's are darker brown and closer to the painting's walnut chairs.

**lamp_close (A=X, B=Y): table B, chair A.** B's table looks solid: turned pedestal, square feet with end grain, and felt flecked with warm specks like the painting's cloth. But its outer rim has a pale, glowing extra ring past the edge on the right. A's felt is a flat mottled green and its foot is a blue-grey pipe. A's near chair looks well made (chunky top rail, joints you can read, slatted seat) but too orange for lamplight.

**day_wide (A=Y, B=X): A better.** A's pedestal and feet read as weathered timber. B's pedestal looks like grey stone, and its left foot is a round grey cylinder. B's chairs are a bright yellow-orange that jumps out. A's are dimmer and calmer.

**day_close (A=X, B=Y): B better.** A's table has a pale wood band under the felt on the left, a black blotch on the felt at the right, and grey pipe-like feet. B has a grained wooden pedestal and plank feet. But B's rim looks like two stacked rings with a dark gap and a pale underside, as if the lip floats off the top.

## Overall
- **Table: Y better** (B in lamp_wide, lamp_close and day_close; A in day_wide).
- **Chair: about the same in build.** X is chunkier and more saturated orange. Y is darker and sits better with the painting at night. In daylight X's chairs look garish.

## Three biggest differences a viewer would notice
1. **The pedestal and feet:** Y's are timber, with grain and square feet. X's read as grey stone or metal pipes.
2. **The tabletop rim:** Y has a real wooden rail, but with a floating second ring and a pale halo (a flaw). X has a thin dark edge with a pale underside band and flat felt.
3. **The chairs' colour:** X's are bright honey-orange, glaring in daylight. Y's are darker walnut and closer to the painting's chairs and to the man's dark vest and trousers.
