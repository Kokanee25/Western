# Blind critic: general store interior, round r2

## 1. Five biggest differences (most important first)

1. **The shelving is empty (shop.png, counter.png, clerk.png, stove.png).** The target's walls are floor-to-ceiling shelving crammed with tins, bottles, boxes, folded cloth and hardware. In the renders the side walls are bare plank. The few shelf units that exist (dark green, back wall and right edge of counter.png; the back wall of stove.png) are almost empty, with a few dark specks. This is the single biggest gap: the room reads as a barn, not a store.
2. **The room is a vast, empty hall (shop.png, counter.png).** The target is a narrow shop with two long counters down the middle, shelving both sides and a stove on the centre axis, so it feels packed and intimate. Here it is a wide open floor with four or five white posts and a lot of bare boards. The ceiling is flat white beadboard with no exposed beams or hanging lamps, and the walls are pale grey-green planks, so it feels cold and modern. The target is warm, dark timber with amber light.
3. **The counters are thin plain orange boxes (counter.png, clerk.png).** The target's are long, heavy, weathered counters with glass jars, candy, display cases, a ledger and a till on top. These are smooth, flat-coloured cabinets pushed against the side walls instead of running down the room. They carry only three white bottles and a scale, and read as modern cabinetry.
4. **Furniture and stock are sparse and don't read (all four).** A few barrels, three or four crates, a couple of grain sacks, a trestle table with a handful of boxes or books, and two spindle chairs. They are scattered on the open floor and not grouped. The target has barrels with produce, sacks stacked at the counter, crates against the walls, cloth bolts, a stool and a clerk's desk. Nothing is hanging from the ceiling or walls (no lamps, pots, tools, boots, hats).
5. **The stove is tiny and misplaced (stove.png, shop.png).** The target has a big pot-bellied stove on the centre axis with a stovepipe to the ceiling and a chair circle. Here it is a small dark box with a thin pipe, pushed off to one side, with two chairs close to it. In shop.png it is almost lost against the back wall.

## 3. Still obviously missing

- Stock on every shelf: tins, jars, bottles, boxes, boots, hats, folded cloth, rope, tools.
- Pigeonhole cabinet and clerk's desk with open ledger.
- Candy jars, display cases, a till or coffee mill on the counters.
- Hanging lamps (kerosene), hanging goods (pots, lanterns, harnesses, hams) and exposed ceiling beams.
- Wainscot or painted dado and a warmer wall colour; the walls feel flat and cold.
- Shop-window displays (the stacked tins and goods the facade shows).
- Stairs up and down (cellar and loft), the storeroom door detail, and the cellar hatch.
- A lived-in layer: dust, wear, spills and clutter on the floor.
- The long counters down the middle of the room with the aisle between counter and shelving.
- Warm, golden light; the render is flat and grey by comparison.
