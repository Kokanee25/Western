# Blind critic: general store interior, round 1

Verdict: it does not yet read as a real 1882 general store. It reads as an empty, freshly painted hall with a few props dropped in. The target is a crammed, warm, cluttered shop. The renders are a bare room.

## Five biggest differences (most important first)

1. **The shelves are empty or almost empty.** The tall wall shelving (stove.png left, counter.png right edge) is bare teal grids with no tins, boxes, bottles or cloth. The target's shelves are packed floor to ceiling. This is the single biggest failure: a store without stock is not a store.
2. **The room is huge, bare and open.** Shop.png shows an empty plank floor with a few things pushed against the walls and an enormous gap between the counters. The target is a narrow room with two long counters and a dense middle. The scale is more like a dance hall.
3. **The counters are bare teal boxes with a flat orange top.** They are in every image. The target has weathered pale wood, with glass jars of candy, a ledger, a display case and scales on top. The teal paint looks modern and flat, and nothing sits on them except a few bottles and the scales (clerk.png).
4. **The look is clean and cold.** The walls are pale grey-green beadboard, the ceiling is uniform, and the posts are clean white. The target is amber, with exposed dark joists, hanging lamps, a dark green wainscot, and sun and dust. The lighting is flat grey-white, not warm.
5. **The set pieces are too few and too small for the room.** The stove and chairs (stove.png) are tiny and isolated, with no pipe run visible to speak of. The barrels are scattered in pairs along the wall. The sacks are a few lumps by the back door. Nothing clusters into the cracker-barrel, stove-side loafing area the target shows.

## Verdicts

- **Counters:** CLOSE in layout (two long runs, as on the plan), DIFFERENT in finish. They are teal and clean, with no goods, no glass case and no ledger. (shop, counter, clerk)
- **Wall shelving and ladder:** DIFFERENT. The shelves are the right shape and height, and the ladder is visible on the left in stove.png, but they are empty. In shop.png the shelves barely register. In counter.png they are dark empty cabinets.
- **Stove and chairs:** CLOSE. A box stove with a pipe and two rough chairs by it match the target's idea (stove.png). It is small and cold, with no pipe elbow or hearth clutter, and it is not the target's big round pot-belly.
- **Barrels:** CLOSE. The barrel shapes, hoops and staves are good (stove.png, counter.png). They are too evenly spaced and too empty-looking, with no open tops, no pickles or crackers visible, and no scoops or lids.
- **Sacks:** CLOSE/DIFFERENT. A few tied sacks sit against the back wall (shop, counter, stove) but they read as lumpy jugs or urns, not stacked grain sacks.
- **Counter scales:** CLOSE. The brass-beam balance on the clerk's counter (clerk.png) is recognisable. It is oversized and the texture is noisy, and it sits on a dark slab like a book. There is no stove-side floor scale.
- **Bolts of cloth:** DIFFERENT. I could not find any in the renders. The target shows folded cloth on shelves and hanging fabric.
- **Window goods:** CLOSE. Barrels, jars and an urn sit on tables in the show windows (counter.png), and they read as a display. They are sparse and tiny against big empty windows, with no tins stacked in pyramids as the target's front exterior has.

## Still missing

- Stock on the shelves: tinned goods, boxed goods, bottles, hardware, lamps, tools.
- Candy jars, a glass display case, a coffee grinder and a cash register or till on the counters.
- A ledger, a wall clock, posters and notices, and a mirror.
- Hanging lamps and lanterns, hanging items (hams, tools, rope, pans, boots) and hats.
- Hanging cloth, boots and shoes, and a mannequin or hat rack.
- A pigeonhole or cubby wall behind the counter.
- Crates, kegs and open goods in front of the counter.
- Warm light, dust shafts, and the dark wainscot and exposed rafters of the target.
- Wear and dirt on floor and walls, such as worn paths, scuffed counter fronts and stained boards.
- Stairs to the loft and cellar, and a cellar hatch.
- A spittoon and a cat or dog (period colour).
