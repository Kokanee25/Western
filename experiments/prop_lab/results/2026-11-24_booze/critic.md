# Blind critic: back-bar bottles and labels, night (2026-11-24)

## 1. Legibility at 1 m: PASS
Words I can read in the full 1 m frame: **CUTHBERT'S**, **BURNT HOLLOW** (x2), **PURE RYE** (x2), **BELLE OF LAR..** (cut off by the label edge; "OF LARK"?), **VAN DORST** only with effort (letters break up, reads as "V N / DO RST"). Secondary lines are half-readable at best: "Merrimack Rum" (Cuthbert's), "Old Sour Mash" (Pure Rye), "Bourbon Whisky" (Burnt Hollow). All small print (makers, towns, "OLD FASHIONED HAND MADE" banners) is mush, which is fine.
Verdict: brand names readable on 5 of 6 distinct labels; Van Dorst is the weak one.

## 2. At 3 m: PASS (just)
Mostly colour: cream rectangles with a red/black smudge. But the top-shelf "ASHBY'S" is almost readable, and the labels are the brightest, most uniform things on the back bar: a row of identical pale cream squares at the same height on every bottle. Not noise or flicker, but repetitive.

## 3. Period: CLOSE
Good: engraved vignettes (ship, distillery, jug), serif display faces, printer's ruled borders, red/black two-colour printing. Off: labels are clean, square-cut, evenly cream and perfectly centred (no foxing, tears, glue stains, peeled corners, drips); "amber" quarts read as one deep maroon-red glass, all the same shape and size, like modern repro; layout of every label is the same template; the gin bottle doesn't read as a square case gin from the front.

## 4. Five biggest problems (most important first)
1. **Every bottle is the same bottle.** 1 m: bottles at x 140-750, y 190-425 are one tall round quart in one red-brown glass; only the gin differs in colour. 3 m: x 80-1220, y 160-390 the shelves read as a row of clones. Needs flasks, squat bitters, pumpkinseeds, true amber/olive/aqua glass, varied heights and fill levels.
2. **Labels too clean and too bright / identical template.** 1 m x 140-750, y 290-380; 3 m every label. Same cream paper, same tone, same centred position, same layout (banner, oval vignette, name, small print). No ageing, stains, tears, slight skew or differing paper colours. At 3 m they become a dotted line of pale squares, the brightest thing after the lamps.
3. **"VAN DORST" lettering breaks up.** 1 m x 295-355, y 320-345: the display face falls apart into "V N / DO RST"; the one label the player squints at looks like a texture error.
4. **Brand names clipped at the label edge.** "BELLE OF LAR.." (x 700-750, y 325-350) and "PURE RYE" at x 1055-1110 (E cut) run off the label width; looks like a layout bug, not wrap-around.
5. **Labels look printed on, not stuck on.** 1 m all labels: no paper thickness, edge shadow or curve-off at the sides; glass highlights run straight over the paper; reads as a decal on the glass rather than a paper label. The repeated brands (two Pure Rye, two Burnt Hollow side by side at 1 m) add to the stamped feel.
