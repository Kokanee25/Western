# Blind critic: the Baron's coach (#782)

Judged only from the six renders against the street painting and period Concord coaches.

## Verdicts
- **The coach: DOESN'T.** It reads as a black iron boxcar or hearse on a farm wagon's running gear, not a Concord: a flat-sided, flat-topped rectangle sitting rigid on a frame, with no rounded body, no visible thoroughbraces, no slits and a low crate for a driver's box. Only the wheels, the roof rail and the riveted plating say "armoured coach".
- **The strongbox: CLOSE.** It has the right parts (iron straps, end handles, hasp and padlock, hinged lid, papers inside), but the body is untextured matte black with no plate, rivets, edge wear or thickness, so it looks like an unfinished primitive next to its rusty straps.

## Five biggest differences (most important first)
1. **The silhouette is a box, not a Concord.** A Concord body is egg-shaped: sides that bow out and tuck in to a curved bottom, a rounded roof edge, a body that hangs below the line of the frame. This one is a straight-sided rectangle with a flat top and square corners, more like a hearse or a railway car. Even armoured (the "Monitor" kept the coach's shape), the shape must be a coach's first. `coach_noon.png` (382,166,624,314); `coach_door_open.png` (300,60,770,350).
2. **The iron is a dead, flat black.** Every plate is the same near-black with no iron sheen, rust, scratched paint, dust or road dirt; it is the darkest thing in every shot, flatter than the wheels and timber around it, and against the painting's warm, coloured surfaces it reads as a hole in the picture. At night it vanishes into a silhouette with no edge light. `coach_noon.png` (382,166,624,314); `coach_night.png` (382,162,660,336).
3. **No rifle slits, no windows, nothing on the sides.** The brief's defining feature, narrow vertical slits, is not visible in any view; the sides and door are blank riveted panels, so nothing tells you men inside can shoot out (or see out). `coach_noon.png` (382,166,624,314); `coach_door_open.png` (440,60,770,360).
4. **It isn't hung on thoroughbraces.** The body sits square and rigid on a flat timber frame with a dark gap under it; there are no leather braces swinging from curved jacks, no perch, so it looks like a wagon bed, not a coach that rocks. `coach_noon.png` (340,280,660,345); `coach_front.png` (380,330,620,420).
5. **The driver's box and boot are plain crates.** The driver sits low on a stepped wooden box in front of the body, below the roofline, instead of high on a seat at the front of the roof with a footboard and dash; the boot at the back is a square plank crate rather than a leather-covered boot with a hinged flap. `coach_front.png` (415,195,560,340); `coach_door_open.png` (708,168,876,444); `coach_noon.png` (334,240,414,306).
