# The Baron's coach (#782): sheet and blind critic

`python3 experiments/prop_lab/coach_sheet.py --out=<this folder>` (the lab's bridge `prop`, `prop_open`):
the coach before the store at noon and 21:30, from the front, its left door open; the strongbox shut and
open (keyed). `round1/` is the first look (the plates in the props' iron).

- **It stands** in the town: 1,507.8 kg, 186 members; the strongbox 44.3 kg, 10 members, on the coach's
  floor; the door and the lid open. `tests/test_coach.gd` (6).
- **Round 1** (`round1/critic.md`): coach DOESN'T, strongbox CLOSE. The plates read as camouflage (the
  props' stove iron on a metre of plate), the slits unseen, no braces, the driver low, the boot a crate,
  the body a box, not a Concord.
- **Round 2** (`critic_round2.md`): the plates in their own iron (`prop:plate_iron`), framed slits 5 cm
  wide, the jacks' iron C and the braces' leather drawn past the body's ends, the driver up at the roof's
  line, the boot's leather apron, rivets on the strongbox. Coach still DOESN'T (the body a box: a Concord's
  is egg-shaped, sides tucked in, the bottom rounding up; it doesn't look hung), strongbox CLOSE (clean,
  the papers a blank block).

Two fixes made for the look; what's left (the Concord's curved body) is a design call for Sean, in the
next step's issue: curved plates drawn on the members (and dented as curves), or an armoured van's box.
