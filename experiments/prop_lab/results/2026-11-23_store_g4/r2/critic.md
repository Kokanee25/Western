# Blind critic: general store interior, r2
Views: shop (from the door), clerk (east side), post (post office corner), counter_top (down onto the back counter), against the target sheet (shop, behind the counter, stock loft, goods cellar panels).

## Biggest differences (your order)
1. shop: the room is a bare, pale-green-grey striped box with a big empty floor; the target is a warm, packed, golden-lit room with shelving walls, counters down both sides and a stove in the middle.
2. shop: the hung goods are oversized and read badly. Grey pails, clay-orange teardrops (hams?), tan "boots" and lantern clusters crowd the ceiling in the foreground at huge scale; the target has a few small hams and lanterns, tastefully placed.
3. shop/clerk: no long counters with jars, candy or glass cases, and the shelving is thin and far away (only one dark wall of shelves); the target has floor-to-ceiling shelving and counters running the length of the room.
4. All views: lighting is flat and cold on the walls, with hard light stripes on the floor; the target has warm low sun, haze and wood glow. The floor reads as plain orange planks.
5. counter_top: the back counter is nearly black and underexposed; the ledger, ink and cash box are only just legible. The target's open ledger, quill and strongbox are lit and are the hero.

## Items
(a) Post office pigeonhole case: CLOSE. In post and shop it is a dark wood case with a "POST OFFICE" sign and a grid of small slots with letters, which reads right. It is small, sits low and is dim; the target's is a tall cubby wall dominating the left.

(b) Goods hung from the ceiling: DIFFERENT. The pails are readable as pails and the lanterns as lanterns, but the clay-coloured teardrops do not read clearly as hams (they look like gourds or pots) and the tan boot shapes look like cardboard slabs. In the shop view they are huge and cut off at the frame edge and look like clutter. In the clerk and post views at distance they read better. A period store would have these, but the scale and density are wrong.

(c) Books, ink and cash on the back counter: CLOSE. The counter_top view shows an open ledger, a closed ledger labelled LEDGER, an ink pot with pen, and a cash box and a small tray, all clearly identifiable. They are very dark and flat, and the target has an open book in warm light as the focal point.

(d) The room as a whole from the door: DIFFERENT. The shop view is an empty, cool, striped hall with a few crates, barrels and a table. It has no sense of the packed shelved shop of the target, no warm light from the windows and no counters flanking the aisle. The stove and doorway at the back do give the right layout, but it feels like a warehouse.
