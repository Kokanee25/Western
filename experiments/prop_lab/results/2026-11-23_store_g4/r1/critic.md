## Biggest differences (your order)

1. Palette and light: the renders are cold grey-green wall and ceiling against a flat orange-tan floor, with desaturated, washed-out light (shop, clerk). The reference is warm amber, honey-lit timber, deep brown rafters and a teal-green wainscot with cream above. The renders have no golden low sun, no window glow, no atmosphere (shop.jpg whole frame).
2. Ceiling: the renders have a pale grey beadboard ceiling with fat white posts and corbels, so the room feels like a modern porch or hall (shop.jpg top half; clerk.jpg top). The reference has exposed dark joists and rafters over a plank ceiling. No dark wood warmth overhead.
3. Hung goods read as odd clusters, not stock. Black boot-like blocks at top right of shop.jpg (approx x900-1220, y0-130) are unreadable lumps. Pails hang as stiff grey buckets on a trapeze frame (x300-490, y60-240). Hams are uniform orange-pink ovals on strings (x740-910, y190-260). Lanterns top-left are cut off by the frame. They look like a mobile, not hams, pails and boots hung on hooks from beams.
4. Emptiness and lack of density: the shop room is a big bare plank floor with a few crates and a lone table (shop.jpg x110-420, y450-580). The reference is packed with floor-to-ceiling shelving, counters with candy jars, a stove as centrepiece, ladders, barrels, signs. Only the right-hand shelves (shop.jpg x1050-1180, y250-400) and the post.jpg shelves have density.
5. Surfaces are too clean and uniform: counters are flat plain orange-tan boxes (clerk.jpg bottom right, x0-1280 y490-720) with no wear, no zinc or marble, no goods on top except a scale and three jars. Walls are uniform vertical board with no dado, paper, posters or tools. Counter_top is very dark, with the lower half a black-brown slab.
