# The blind critic on #1304's dressed street (2026-11-27)

A fresh agent shown only the street painting (`docs/concept/street-golden-hour-painted.png`) and the ten
sheets here (A as it was, D as it is with `ValleyStreetDressing`), told to ignore the placeholder blocks.

**Busier:** D, clearly, but only up close. Wagons, teams and tied horses give across, mid_east, mid_west and
saloon_door life and depth; nearly all of it is animals and vehicles, not people. From west_entry A and D
look much the same (the dressing is a smudge at the vanishing point).

**Still too wide or empty:** yes. Worst in `mid_east` (the near half bare rutted dirt, one cart to the
right), then `across` (big empty foreground, the dressing a thin band in the middle distance) and
`west_entry`. `mid_west` is better.

**Five things still wrong (D against the painting):**
1. Almost no people: 1-2 a view against the painting's ~10 on porches and in the street.
2. Bare foreground ground (mid_east, across): flat dirt where the painting has sage, rocks, a walk's edge.
3. Little porch clutter at the fronts: a few barrels; no benches or loungers.
4. Nothing tall on the skyline in town: no water tower, poles, wires, windmill or trees.
5. No life at the vanishing point: no distant walkers or a wagon in dust and haze.

**Broken or odd in D:** the same horse model and pose in a row (across, saloon_door); the saloon's tied
horses fill the view from its door; mid_west's team faces the camera, parked mid-street; mid_east's cart has
its tongue down and no horse; the livery's hay a neat block against a blank wall; a "stalled over 5 s"
overlay on three renders (lavapipe's slowness, not the game's).

**Gaps that show most:** saloon_door (open prairie between the jail and the grey block to its east: the
worst), across (between the hotel and the next block), mid_west (the long run past the jail), west_entry.
