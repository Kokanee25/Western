## B. Kick runs

**Run 1 (sheets 1-2): 4/10**
- The kick is almost invisible. The kicker stands rigid with his arms at his sides, and from -0.17 to +0.13 s his foot barely moves, so the chair seems to break on its own. His body doesn't shift its weight and his arms don't swing.
- Before the kick he stands right against the sitter's back (side view, -0.30 s), and their bodies look like they overlap.
- The fall is the good part: the chair tips back and he goes over onto his back with his legs on the seat (0.5-1.2 s).
- At +0.80 s the kicker takes a stride straight toward the falling man, and in the side view (2-3 s) his feet look as if they stand on or in the man lying behind them.
- The get-up is up, then down again (kneel at 7 s), then up, and he leaves his hat on the ground.
- The kicker is a statue for 2-9 s. He never looks down, steps back or reacts.
- The man ends standing shoulder to shoulder with the kicker, both in A-pose.

**Run 2 (sheets 3-4): 3/10**
- The kick does show: a foot behind the chair at +0.10 s, and the back legs fly out.
- But the man then sits level and unmoved on a chair missing its back legs for about 0.4 s (0.10-0.48 s), as if it were still holding him.
- He then raises one arm straight up with a flat palm (0.78 s), a posed gesture.
- The chair sinks slowly instead of dropping.
- He folds forward head to knees and stays still there for about 1 s (1.98-2.98 s).
- At 3.98 s he appears huge, on all fours right in front of both cameras, then is back at the chair standing at 4.98 s. It reads as a pop or teleport.
- He ends with his feet inside the broken chair frame (4.98-8.98 s), pressed against the kicker so that their bodies overlap (three view, 4.98-5.98 s).
- Both men freeze in A-pose facing the camera.
- Small credit: his hat stays on.

## The five biggest faults (most important first)

1. **Bodies pass through things.** At the end of a get-up his shins stand inside the broken chair's frame: rise/pair_002 Y (6-9 s), rise/pair_003 X (5.98-8.98 s), kick/sheet_4 (4.98-8.98 s, both views). The two men overlap too: kick/sheet_4 three view (4.98-5.98 s), the kicker against the sitter's back in kick/sheet_1 (-0.30 s), and the kicker's feet in the fallen man in kick/sheet_2 side view (2-3 s).
2. **The kick doesn't read and the kicker is a mannequin.** The kicker stands rigid, arms down, with barely any foot movement: kick/sheet_1 (-0.17 to +0.13 s) and kick/sheet_3 (+0.10 s). He takes a stride straight into the falling man in kick/sheet_1 (+0.80 s), and then stands frozen without reacting in kick/sheet_2 and kick/sheet_4 (2-9 s).
3. **He folds forward when the chair drops back.** His head goes to his knees as the seat goes out behind him, before he finally goes over backwards: fall/pair_002 X (0.43-0.60 s), fall/pair_003 Y (0.30-0.58 s), kick/sheet_4 (1.98-2.98 s). In fall/pair_001 (both, 0.13-0.43 s) he lands in a perfectly balanced deep squat with his arms reaching forward.
4. **Posed, stiff arms all through.** He sits with both forearms held out palm-up, as if holding a tray (the first frame of every sheet). The flat "stop" hand or T-arms appear during falls: fall/pair_002 Y (0.30-0.43 s), fall/pair_003 X (0.28-0.45 s), kick/sheet_3 (0.78 s). The sit-up has one arm locked straight out: rise/pair_002 Y and rise/pair_003 X (4-5 s). Every run ends standing dead still in an A-pose (rise pairs, 7-9 s).
5. **The chair doesn't act like wood carrying a man, and the get-up bobs.** In kick/sheet_3 (0.10-0.48 s) the chair holds him level on two legs. Broken legs fly far and spin, and one stands upright on its end (fall/pair_003 Y three view 0.28-0.98 s; fall/pair_001 0.43 s). Get-ups go up and then back down to a kneel (rise/pair_002 X 6 s to 7 s, rise/pair_003 Y 5.98 s to 6.98 s, kick/sheet_2 6 s to 7 s), and the hat is usually left on the ground. There is also the pop in kick/sheet_4 (3.98 s).
