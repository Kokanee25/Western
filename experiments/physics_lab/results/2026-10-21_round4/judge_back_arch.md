# Blind verdict: arch pairs (shot in the back)

| pair | better | how sure (1-3) | why, in one line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 001 open ground | X | 2 | X pitches forward from the hips and settles out flat and limp; Y drops straight down on its knees and freezes in a tight, posed-looking kneeling curl |
| 002 walking | X | 1 | Nearly the same fall; X's torso and head go forward and limp, while Y keeps upright with its head thrown back and then planks forward with its head held up |
| 003 balcony rail | even | 1 | Both fold over the rail, then drop backwards onto their back on the floor; Y doubles over the rail more naturally, but X's sprawl at rest reads as just as dead |
| 004 bar | X | 1 | X slumps chest-first into the bar and slides down it; Y buckles and bends away from the bar, which reads as falling back from the shot. Both end on their backs |
| 005 crates | X | 1 | Same as 004: X sags into the crates the way a man pushed into them would, Y folds back away from them. The resting poses are almost identical |
| 006 top of stair | even | 1 | Both crouch at the edge for over a second, then slide head-first down to the foot. I couldn't separate them |
| 007 hitching rail | X | 2 | Same as 001: X folds forward and lies out prone with his legs straightening; Y sinks onto its knees and stays in a child's-pose curl with the hat in front |
| 008 porch edge | X | 2 | X goes over the edge and lies prone with its feet against the porch. Y hangs face-down over the edge and creeps off it for six seconds, still moving at the end |
| 009 card table | even | 1 | Both slump over the barrel and turn round. X ends propped sitting against it, cross-legged (stiff); Y rolls off onto its side. Each is plausible, each has one oddity |
| 010 oak stair | Y | 2 | Y pitches onto the stairs, slides a step or two and stops dead, draped head-down. X keeps speeding up, cartwheels its legs over at the foot and leaves the frame |
| 011 railed walkway / landing stair | Y | 2 | Y goes head-first onto the steps and stops near the top. X tumbles the whole flight, flies in the air at 4 s and is still moving fast at 6 s, which looks like a launched ragdoll |
| 012 railed walkway | X | 1 | X bows forward over the rail before sitting down against it; Y sinks on buckling knees with its chin on its chest. They end almost the same, slumped against the rail |

**What runs through the pairs.** On flat ground and at waist-high objects, one version folds forward from the hips, so the torso and head lead the fall. The other drops straight down on buckling knees with the torso upright and the chin on the chest. The knee-drop version usually ends in a tight kneeling curl that looks posed. The forward fold reads more like a man hit from behind, except where it bends him away from the object he faces (004, 005). Where there is a drop (porch, stairs), the versions differ in how far he carries on after going down. In one he keeps tumbling or sliding: the whole flight of stairs, a cartwheel at the bottom, or a slow creep off the porch lasting several seconds. In the other he stops within a step or two. Stopping quickly looked more real on the stairs, and going cleanly off the porch looked more real than hanging there and creeping.

**Faults common to both.** There is almost no first reaction: at 0.18 s and 0.38 s he is still standing upright, with no jolt, no arch of the back and no stagger. Then he collapses vertically on the spot. He never takes a step forward or throws his arms out, and his feet stay planted. At objects he faces (bar, crates, barrel, balcony rail, walkway) he turns round and ends sitting or lying on his back, facing the shooter, head toward the shot. That reads backwards for a ball in the back. At the top of the stair he crouches at the edge for about 1.5 s before going over, instead of pitching straight down. The resting poses he sits in have crossed legs, which looks stiff (009, 012). The hat always comes off cleanly and lands well clear of him.
