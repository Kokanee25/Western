# Blind verdicts: furniture falls (yblind)

| Pair | Verdict | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 001 | Y | He slumps back onto the chair and it tilts and slides under his weight; in X the chair stays rigid while he sits into it. |
| 002 | Y | The chair gives a little (tilts back, shifts) as he collapses against it; in X it doesn't move at all. |
| 003 | Y | His head and shoulders hit the chair hard and it is shoved and turned; in X the chair is fixed and his head seems to sink into the seat. |
| 004 | X | A face-first fall into the chair pushes and rotates it; in Y the chair barely moves under the same hit. |
| 005 | Y | He stays slumped in the chair he fell into, legs out; in X he sits bolt upright again after landing (puppet-like), then pitches forward. |
| 006 | Y | The chair tips over under him and both hit the floor together; in X the chair's back breaks into pieces but he stays hung arched over it in mid-air. |
| 007 | X | The light pedestal table tips over as his weight slides to its edge; in Y it stays perfectly still under a full body charge. (X's tip comes about 1 s late and the table ends propped steeply.) |
| 008 | EVEN | Practically identical: he sits down and rolls against the barrel, and nothing is hit hard enough to move. |
| 009 | Y | Brushing past, the chair only turns slightly; in X the chair's back slats snap and fly off from what looks like a light graze. |
| 010 | EVEN | Identical: he's charged face-down onto the table and the table doesn't budge in either. |

**Tally:** X 2, Y 6, EVEN 2

**Most common faults (either version):**
1. Furniture stays rigid under a hard body hit: chairs that don't shift when a man's full weight lands on them (X001, X002, X003, Y004), and the round table unmoved under a charge (Y007, both 010).
2. The body is held in an unnatural pose: he hangs arched over a broken chair (X006), sits back up after collapsing (X005), or lies stiffly draped over the table edge.
3. Breakage and contact are out of proportion: chair spindles snap from a light brush (X009), a chair breaks but still holds him up (X006), the head clips into the chair seat (X003, Y004), and a table tips late, about a second after he has settled (X007).
