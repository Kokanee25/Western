**Tally:** X 6, Y 3, EVEN 1.

**The 3 most common faults (in either version):**
1. **Passive arms:** in the losing version of almost every pair, the hands stay in the lap or at the sides, with no reach, grab or brace, so he falls like a sack or a doll. (Railing X in 1 and 3; chair Y in 5, 6, 8, 9 and 10.)
2. **Head-first pile-driver off the railing:** in every railing fall, in both versions, he goes over and drops nearly vertical with his legs straight up, landing on his head or shoulders. A surprised man would twist, grab the rail or post, or at least get his arms under him. One variant is the stiff horizontal plank (2 Y).
3. **Limp head and "unconscious" posture:** the head drops onto his knees or chest, or lolls far back (8 Y), while the body sits still for a beat. Sometimes a springy sit-up rebound follows (9/10 X, 4 X), and some falls end curled with his face planted in the dirt (5 Y, 7 X).
