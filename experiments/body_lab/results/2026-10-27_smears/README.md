# The drag smears, 2026-10-27

Sean's picture from the spec saloon: rows of thin pink slashes where a bleeding man slid.

- `before.jpg`: main, a man bleeding 3 ml/s from the chest dragged 2.4 m over the saloon floor
  (`body_drag`, the README's lab commands). The diagonal `smear.png` squeezed into a 16 x 40 cm box
  every 18 cm from each of chest, belly and pelvis.
- `after.jpg`: the same floor, dragged twice with a pause: one swipe a pull, heavy where he lay,
  streaked the way he went, kinking where his body swung.
- `pieces.png`: start, five band pieces (three variants), end, laid end to end
  (`tools/textures/smear_band.py`).

half-metre the smear went see-through and vanished on the saloon's dark floor; now a thinning
smear narrows and keeps its colour. Shot after it: `saloon_along.jpg`, `saloon_above.jpg` (2.3 m at
