# Where to hide from a stick on the kitchen's back wall (issue #537)

`farm_hide` (experiments/farmhouse/bridge.gd): a crouched man (eyes 1.15 m up, his body 0.9) at each
spot of the yard, looked for from every window and door of the house by the gang's own sight line
(`Sight.clear`): a man 0.3 and 0.9 m inside each opening, at its sides and middle, crouched and
standing. And the stick's harm there (`docs/briefs/blast-surfaces.md`'s radii): an eardrum within
5 m in the open, within 3.2 m with a standing wall between (`Blast.shielding`).

The house's own space (FORMAT.md): x along the front from its west corner as built (0) to 9, z back
from the front wall; the lean-to's back wall (the kitchen's) is z = 7.6, its window at x 1.2-2.0,
its door at 4.0-4.9; the outhouse at (7.6, 15.5). Rows from the far yard down to the house:
`#` solid, `H` the house, `.` seen from an opening, `o` hidden but in the blast's harm, `+` hidden
and clear.

- **The outhouse** hides you (behind it, x 7.5-8.5, z 16.5-18) and is clear of the blast (10.3 m,
  shielded), but it's 10.3 m off: 7.4 s at a crouch (1.4 m/s) against the planted fuse's 5 s.
  Running there (6 m/s, ~2 s) is heard through the wall (running steps 13 m, half through a wall)
  by Hollis in the kitchen 1.5 m away: that's what sent him to the back window in round 13.
- **Round the lean-to's west corner** (x -0.5 to -1.5, z 5-7.5): out of sight of every opening (no
  window on the lean-to's west end; the front room's west window is 4 m down the wall and looks
  out, not along it), the lean-to between it and the stick (3.4-3.8 m, shielded), and 4.6 m from
  the stick's spot along the wall: 3.3 s at a crouch, inside the fuse. Crouched steps carry 2 m
  (1 through a wall): unheard. The way there runs under the kitchen's window; crouched against the
  wall (z 7.9) only a man crouched at the window looking down along the wall sees you (`.` at x 2.0
  in the close map below). **This is the hiding spot**: no prop needed.
