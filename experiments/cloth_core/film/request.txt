# #973's film (tools/lab_film.py on a runner, .github/workflows/lab_film.yml). In a row, left to right:
# our sheet, the Jolt rag, our glove (the long glove's own mesh from sean_s3_talking's skin), Jolt's
# glove; behind, our sheet hung by two corners. One frame a tick (60 a second).
@start --size=960x540 --fps=60 --game-args=--real
time 16.2
clock off
goto -3 0 6 180
cc_set -3 0 -3
cc_cam front
cc_film build/cc/front 7
cc_set -3 0 -3
cc_cam low
cc_film build/cc/low 7
@stop
@encode build/cc/front experiments/cloth_core/film/out/front.mp4 60
@encode build/cc/low experiments/cloth_core/film/out/low_half_speed.mp4 30
@still build/cc/front 20 experiments/cloth_core/film/out/front_start.jpg
@still build/cc/front 400 experiments/cloth_core/film/out/front_end.jpg
@still build/cc/low 400 experiments/cloth_core/film/out/low_end.jpg
