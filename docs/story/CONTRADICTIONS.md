### C1. The payroll is saved: does the Baron's outrage still happen, and how?

**Sean's pick (2026-11-11): a.** "Two barrels: the Baron planned the wreck and the floor for the same
day. Saving the pay changes nothing for him." D (Sean, 2026-11-11, #830). It opens s23 (the saw is
bought before the train's outcome is known).

Left to this bible by Sean (#744, 2026-11-11): "show 2–3 versions in context". The versions are
written out in `OUTLINE.md`, Act III. Since he asked, he has also decided that the floor falls on
Payday Night whatever happened on the train (#809, 2026-11-11), which answers half of it.

- **a. Two barrels:** the Baron planned the wreck and the floor for the same day. Saving the pay
  changes nothing for him.
- **b. "The deputy stole it":** the pay is saved but comes up short, and the Baron's men say the
  deputy took it (Sean's own twist). The floor still falls.
- **c. It really worked:** saving the pay beat him, and the floor is him trying something else in a
  hurry that afternoon, which is why it leaves traces.

Leans c: it's the only one where the player's best mission changes anything, and the hurry explains
Sean's "one or two hard-to-find ways" to learn of it. b can be added to either later.

### C2. The payroll is lost, or the train is wrecked: what is Payday Night then?

- **c. The wreck was the outrage:** no dance. Rourke comes for the wreck's wounded with the same
  letter, and the sawn girder waits, found or not, for another crowd.
