Sean, 2026-11-17: "we need to condense all the mission related ideas into one place and we can organize and then
trim the fat from there". This is that place. It was gathered on 2026-11-17 from every source: the mission cards
(still on PR #743's branch, not on `main`), Sean's own files (`docs/missions/sources/`: his No Justice card, his No
Law draft, his mission inventory, the ChatGPT cards he sent), the proposals and their versions, the story bible
(`OUTLINE.md`, `SETUPS.md`, `CONTRADICTIONS.md`), `DESIGN.md`, and what is built in the game.

**How to read it**
- **The table below is the whole game on one screen**: every mission in play order, in one line, with how much is
  still unsettled.
- **Under it, a block per mission**: what Harlan walks in with (gun, badge, horse), the beats in order, and the
  choices. Every beat says whose it is: **D** = decided by Sean (with the date), **P** = proposed (by a session, the
  bible or ChatGPT; never his until he says), **O** = open, **built** = in the game now.
- **The full record** is in [`missions/`](missions/), one file a stretch of the story: Sean's own words about each
  mission, word for word; every line of dialogue anyone wrote; what each mission plants and pays off; every other
  idea, including old versions and rejected ones (the fat); where the sources disagree; the open questions.

**What this is not.** Nothing was decided here and nothing was trimmed. It was gathered by five reader sub-agents
working from the sources. The story session has read this page end to end and all of Sean's quoted words (its
findings are the next section); it has not read the rest of the full record. Where this file and `OUTLINE.md` or `CONTRADICTIONS.md` disagree, that is a
finding, not a ruling. "This session" in a source note means Sean's words to the story session on 2026-11-17 (#1056,
#1059).

**Next:** Sean goes down the table and says keep, cut or change; each answer is written back here and into the
bible, and the cards (#743, #959) follow this file.

## Six new mission ideas (proposed 2026-11-17)

Proposed by the story session when Sean asked for more missions with No Justice's weight: a choice where both answers
are defensible, both cost, the game doesn't say which is right, and the world answers back. Sean, 2026-11-17: "Yes add
them to the mission file as proposals they're all great". So each is **P (the story session's; Sean likes them)**:
not decided, no beats, no card. Each is aimed at a gap named above.

1. **The foreman on the East Road (the mid-game reveal).** Harlan catches the foreman alone, nobody behind him, at
   the spot where the sheriff was shot. The arrogance goes and he begs (the coward, S51). Then the widow walks up
   with her husband's Winchester and asks Harlan what Joe asked: are you going to stop me? No Justice turned over:
   this time the law probably would hang the man, so stopping her is right on paper, and she watched her husband
   die. Pays off the coward, the Winchester (S14), the sheriff's last words (S40) and No Justice's question in one
   scene; the widow acts, not only is protected. Needs: where it sits against the farmhouse and the raid (item 10:
   the foreman's fate), and what each answer does to the witness and the case.
2. **Cody's arrest, as night one's mirror.** Same saloon, same table, same barkeep. Harlan is armed now and Cody
   won't draw: he sits and dares him, because nothing happened last time. The room that sat still for Earl watches
   the deputy. The mission is not the arrest: it is getting one of those twelve men to stand up and swear to what he
   saw. Fills item 6 (Cody's arrest has one sentence). A clean arrest should be worth more than a body (DESIGN §10).
3. **Rourke's supper.** Before Payday Night, Rourke has his old steward to the Baron's table at the hotel. Courteous,
   and about the war: something Harlan did under his orders, or refused to. Gives the two a face-to-face before the
   letter and the trestle (S38's plants are an object, a line and a look; none is a scene).
4. **Dry Creek, with teeth.** The Baron dams the creek upstream. The town's troughs go dry, the widow's stock starts
   dying, Joe's people are downstream too. Harlan can blow the dam, but the flood goes somewhere, and where is his
   to judge. Makes "water is the stakes" something the player sees. Gives the undeveloped `dry-creek` a shape; needs
   dynamite, so after Buried Alive.
5. **A wounded Colter.** The bounty is a Colter holed up and bleeding out. Take him dead, or treat the man he came to
   arrest and bring him in breathing (the steward's hands, S5). Saved, the Colters owe the deputy, which matters
   when the Baron cheats them on the train. Gives `the-bounty` a shape and the Colters' line a hinge.
6. **Earl's burial.** Not a mission: a small act in the world. A pauper's grave the day after the sheriff's funeral,
   and nobody comes. If Harlan carried him (S50), he can be the only one there; the undertaker counts the mourners
   in his book. Costs almost nothing to build.
