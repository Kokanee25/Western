# Setups and payoffs

The story bible, part 2 of 5 (#809). Planning only. Every piece of foreshadowing: what it is, where
it's planted, where it pays off, which thread. Status words as in `OUTLINE.md`: **D** Sean's, **P**
proposed by Sean or an earlier session, **P·bible** this bible's own (Sean hasn't seen it), **O** open.
A setup's status is the status of its weakest end: a decided plant with a proposed payoff is P.

## The rules

1. **The order is open, so a setup that matters is planted where every player passes, or in two or
   three places** (#809). Every player passes: the sheriff on the road, day 2 at the doctor's (he's
   sworn in either way), No Justice's cabin, Hell on Wheels into Payday Night into I Am the Law, the
   trestle, the raid. Nothing else is safe: a player can skip the saloon, the funeral, the widow.
2. **Nothing is paid off that wasn't set up, and every setup pays off.** The ones that fail this are
   in "Missing" at the end.
3. **A plant is something in the world** (a line, an object, a man's habit, a place), never a
   caption. A second plant is a different kind of thing from the first: if the first is a line, the
   second is an object or a witness.
4. **A script is checked against this file** (#807): each line that plants or pays something names
   its S-number in the script's notes.

## A. The spine: these must reach every player

**S1. The faded red shirt** · T1 → T2 · D
- Planted: the sheriff on the road. "Man in a faded red shirt. Rode up easy, like he wanted
  directions. Then he shot me." (built; weaker when he's weak: "...red...").
- Again: day 2, his last words, clear or fading by day 1's care (D). Every player is there.
- Again (P·bible): if Harlan left him, the freighter who hauled him in repeats what he kept saying.
- Pays off: Joe's camp, where the survivors name the killer "by one thing: a faded red shirt" (D); the
  cabin, where the shirt is "plain to see" as he runs (D); Main Street, in the arrest ending.
- Note: the old payoff, "the player recognizes him from the saloon", is gone since Kett isn't in the
  saloon (D, 2026-11-11). The man the player recognises from the saloon is Cody (S2).

**S2. "Ain't no law left in this valley to save you now."** · T1, T7 · plant built, payoff P·bible
- Planted: Cody over Earl's body (built line). A player who stayed away hears it retold in the
  morning, each teller a little different (No Law's "inconsistent versions", P).
- Pays off: the jail night. Cody is held in the depot's freight room under the tank (C7 c), his own
  side is coming to free him or kill him, and
  the only thing that can save him now is the law: Harlan. He says so. The mission after that is
  called I Am the Law (D).
- Why it's the strongest in the game: the three titles are one sentence. No Law, No Justice, I Am
  the Law.

**S3. The Baron's offer** · T4 · D
- Planted: the funeral. He offers the widow a price for her claim, quietly, at the grave (D). A
  player who skips the funeral hears of it from her at `widows-place`, and sees his stakes at her
  ditch (D).
- Pays off: the farmhouse (the foreman walks up the lane with a deed: "his answer to her refusal", D);
  the coach's papers; the raid on his house.
- Gap: a player who skips both the funeral and the visit meets the Baron's want for the first time
  at the farmhouse. The third plant this bible proposed (the stranger at the table, if he's the
  Baron) fell with C10 b: the stranger is the foreman, which plants the Baron's **man** on night one
  (S39), not his want. A third plant for the want is still needed (O).

**S4. Cody, the witness** · T1 → the ending · D
- Planted: No Law. A whole room saw him kill, and the barman knows him by name ("Jesus, Cody, my
  chair!", D).
- Pays off: he's the man the law **can** hold; alive and safe after the jail night he may talk, and
  his word is the second kind of evidence (D, 2026-11-11).
- Arrested (D, Sean 2026-11-11, #830, C6 b): after the funeral he walks back into the saloon, bold
  as before, and Harlan, with the sheriff's revolver now, arrests him there. Same room, same barman:
  the answer to night one. The cabin no longer decides whether there's a witness. Whether Cody is
  also one of the men at the cabin, and what happens if he dies there first, is O (s24).
- The jail night is the Water Tower, one side (D, Sean 2026-11-11, #830, C7 c): the tank meant to
  drop on the freight room, and nobody can tell if it frees him or crushes him. He's a witness if he
  lives through it (D: "if he dies, Harlan loses the witness").

**S5. Harlan is a steward** · T8, T1 · D

## B. From one mission to the next

**S10. Which way he rode** · T2 · D. Plant: follow Cody out of the saloon and see him ride for the
foothills. Payoff: at the camp, when the survivors say which way the raiders fled, that player
already knows. A player who didn't follow loses nothing.

**S11. The doctor's warning** · T7 · D. Plant: the doctor (and the bold cowboy) tell Harlan the killers
won't be punished for a Ute woman. Payoff: the release on the train; the mission's title.

**S12. Joe's disguise** · T2 → T4, T5 · D, the last step P·bible. Plant: Joe changes into a cowboy's
hat and neckerchief and says why: so the gang's friends won't take it out on his people. Payoff 1
(D): if it holds, the kin come for Joe; if anyone saw his face, they go to the camp. Payoff 2 (D): I
Am the Law. Ask him to hide for the day and he remembers that "the deputy hid him the way the law
hid his wife's killer". Payoff 3 (P·bible): the Shipment is "a disguised robbery" (Sean's inventory)
where being seen feeds the Baron's story (D, 2026-11-11). Joe already taught him why a man hides his
face. Payoff 4 (D, Sean 2026-11-11, #830, C4 a: "Keep the costume"): the raid was the Baron's
provocation, and a cowboy taking revenge is no Ute "outbreak": the costume is what spoils it (S37).

**S13. The sheriff's horse** · T1, T4 · D. Plant: Harlan leads it into town with the sheriff across
the saddle; it stands at the livery; the livery man complains of the oats. Payoff: `widows-place`
(he rides it home to her; maybe it becomes his, P). After that the town knows whose horse he's on.

**S14. The sheriff's rifle** · T1 → T2 · D. Plant: in its scabbard on that horse from day 1 (P); the
widow gives it to him (D). Payoff: the rope shot. The dead sheriff's rifle saves Joe.

**S15. "That's not the way this works, son."** · T1 · plant D, payoff P·bible. Plant: the dying sheriff,

**S16. The boys' game** · T4 · D. Plant: at `widows-place` the boys play sheriff and outlaws with sticks

**S17. The ways in** · T4 · D. Plant: coffee in her kitchen (the back door), the yard, the outbuildings.

**S18. The place on the road** · T1 · D. Plant: day 1's spot on the East Road. Payoff: the ride to the

**S23. The sawn girder** · T4, T5 · D that it happens; the placing is P·bible. Sean (2026-11-11): "1

- Pays off: the floor; the sawn girder lying in the rubble for anyone to see; the captain walked to

## C. Things the world keeps

**S31. Rubble stays** · the ending · D. Plant: every mission leaves its mark: the hole in the widow's
wall, the saloon's floor, the depot's tank, the coach in the cut, the trestle, the Baron's wall.
Payoff: the last walk reads them (D, 2026-11-11: "it reuses the world").

**S32. Graves** · the ending · D/P. Plant: the sheriff's grave; Earl's burial as a recorded death (P,
Sean's draft); Joe's wife. Payoff: the walk shows "who's alive and who's buried" (D); Joe's grave at
the camp if he died (D). Also plants Dig Him Up (S33).

**S33. The boot** · T1 · P (not picked). Plant: the sheriff's last word, if day 1 went badly: "...boot".
Payoff: Dig Him Up, at his grave, by stealth. What's in it is O (s9).

**S34. The chair** · T7 · D. Plant: the barman's first words over a dead man are about his chair ("owners
care about their own things first"). Payoff: the broken chair is kept, and he's the witness who'll
swear to Cody. Echo (P·bible): Payday Night, and his floor.

**S35. First to use it owns it** · T4 · D (v2). Plant: the stakes at her ditch; her husband dug it first.
Payoff: why the Baron needs her signature and can't simply take the creek; what her signing costs.

**S36. Clocks** · T5, T8 · D that they exist (#789), the uses P. Plant: Harlan's watch; the depot's clock
on railroad time, the town's on its own. Payoff (P): Hell on Wheels' windows are read off the depot
clock; the watch's inscription, now that Harlan's past is kept (C15 a; the inscription is S38's,
P·bible).
