  Reproduced here on two cores under lavapipe (cap on: 10 of 15 runs hang at quit; cap off: 0 of 3).
  The hang is in Godot's worker pool at exit (`exit_languages_threads`): when a low-priority task is
  running and a second is queued behind it at the runlevel switch, the idle workers see the queue,
  don't count themselves idle, and are never woken again (a breakpoint on the switch shows exactly
  that state; engine code, the same in 4.7.2 and the 4.7 branch). The cap makes the whole game crawl
  on a slow frame, so its work (shadow merges, the sound warm-up, the engine's pipeline-cache save)
  is still in flight at quit. Posting the game's own tasks high priority isn't enough: the engine's
  own pair hangs too. So #386's two lines are reverted; the stopgap's idea waits for a quit that
  can't hang (the more-cores brief's step 5). Evidence and options in #392. Then #384 (step 1) and
  #390 (the brief's F3 additions) merged behind it.
