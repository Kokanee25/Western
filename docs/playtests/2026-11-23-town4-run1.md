- t 517.5: pressed E again, facing the storekeeper at 1.3 m. It counted as a second knock (the store wife heard it). No shove.
- t 520 to 591: waited. The storekeeper said "Directly. I'm in the middle of something.", then offered calico; the townsman answered him. The storekeeper then went to carry freight.
- t 596: stopped the game.

## What broke

- At t 0.1, before I'd done anything, the general store's dressing crates and the undertaker's embalming table "went loose (fell whole)": 20+ pieces broken, no shot. A store that wrecks itself at load.
- The storekeeper appeared at the door the instant I knocked. No sign of him walking to it; he stood 0.6 m from me. My own position got nudged 0.7 m back when he arrived.
- The second E press, facing the storekeeper at the open door, counted as another knock ("bang on the door" still offered). Banging on a door a man's standing in is odd. It did not shove him, and nothing broke.
- The storekeeper said "Come on in", then a minute later "Directly. I'm in the middle of something." The two don't fit together.
- The townsman sat at the store bench with "seated: false" for over a minute.

## What was flat

- The saloon was shut and empty at 7 (fair for the hour), and five of the seven shops showed no light or smoke. At most six people were outside at once on a long street.
- People go between two spots and back again, several times in ten minutes. It reads as pacing, not a day.
- The smith and undertaker stand dead still for minutes at their posts. No work in their hands that the read-out shows.
- Nobody spoke to me unless I knocked. Walking right up to the liveryman got one line; standing 0.6 m from the drifter got one line. Then nothing.
- No laugh, no argument, no news all run ("read news" empty).

## What was fun

- The overheard pairs: drifter asks for work, liveryman says "Might be, might be." Storekeeper offers calico, townsman says "I heard different." Short, dry, funny.
- The lines are good: "Sun's warm. Wall's warm. I'm not moving." "Something you're wanting, or is it just the shade?"
- People came to the store on their own: the townsman to sit on the bench, the undertaker on an errand across the whole town. The store became the place to be.
- The knock worked and got a fitting answer.

## Top three to fix

1. The general store's crates and the undertaker's table break themselves at load (t 0.1, 20+ pieces).
2. The storekeeper appears at the door the instant you knock, and then answers the knock twice ("Come on in" then "Directly. I'm in the middle of something."). He should be heard coming, and not contradict himself.
3. Folk pace between the same two spots and stand still at posts for minutes. Give the street more people, and more to do between the loops.
