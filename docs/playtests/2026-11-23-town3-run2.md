- Walked to the liveryman at the livery door (t 22) and stood by him for about a minute. He said two lines.
- Walked to "saloon". That place put me outside a building at x 8.9; the barkeep was at x 25. `walk barkeep` stopped outside the wall twice. I found the way in by walking to `saloon_annex` and then a spot just inside (t 225). Stood at the bar about 2.3 m from the barkeep for two and a half minutes. The drifter came in, drank, laughed, and left.
- Walked to the townsman and the loudmouth on the general store benches (t 442) and stood by them 40 s.
- `walk undertaker` took me 151 m east to his empty post at the undertaker's; he was 190 m away at the other end of town by then. Stood there 40 s, then stopped the game.

## Lines wrong for the hour or place

- The liveryman's "Wind's got the horses jumpy." is filed as his **afternoon** line; it was 12:00.
- "Dry as a bone. We could use a rain." at noon is fine.
- The loudmouth asking for a beer and then sitting at the general store is a small mismatch, not a wrong line.

## What broke

- **Crates broke on their own at the start**: at t 0.1, a crate stack at the general store "went loose (fell whole)", 30 members broken, "no shot". Nobody touched it.
- **`walk saloon` doesn't take you to the bar.** It left me outside a wall; `walk barkeep` stopped at the wall twice. I had to find a way in by hand.
- **`walk undertaker` walked to his post, not to him**: 151 m the wrong way while he was at the far end of town.
- **`look undertaker` from 138 m pointed me 72 degrees down at the boards**, and `look townsman` from 1.8 m away pointed me 45 degrees down; my height read 1.62 m while he sat at 0.54, so I seem to have ended up standing on something by the store bench.
- The saloon marked the drifter's laugh "not seen" with me standing next to him.

## Flat

- A very empty noon: five townsfolk up and about, most of the town at dinner indoors. The street felt deserted.
- Seven lines in ten minutes, almost all said to the air. The bench pair never spoke.
- The town clock moved 9 minutes in 10 real minutes, so the "quiet noon" never changed into anything.
- Regard never moved off "wary". Standing next to people for minutes earned nothing, good or bad.

## Fun

- The barkeep's two lines have character ("it isn't worth it"; "holding my bar up?").
- The drifter drifting in, drinking, laughing with the barkeep and drifting out to lean on the porch felt like a real man's afternoon.

## Top three to fix

1. **People talk to the air, not to each other.** Only one exchange in ten minutes, and its reply landed after an unrelated line so it reads backwards. Two men sat together on a bench for four minutes in silence.
2. **Getting to people is broken.** `walk saloon` stops outside, `walk barkeep` stops at a wall, `walk undertaker` goes to his empty post 150 m from him, and `look` from a distance pitched me into the floor.
3. **The store's crates fall apart on their own at the start** (30 members, no shot): the town begins with broken scenery.
