## What was flat

- The shove got one line from the miner and nothing from anyone else. The rancher at his elbow didn't react at all,
  and said nothing and changed nothing.
- No one ever mentioned the shot or the shove to me later, or at the table.
- The card game: nobody greeted me when I sat down, nobody remarked when I went bust, and I just stayed in the chair.
  The only choices are check/call or fold, so I couldn't raise or bluff. The game folds you if you're slow.
- The cheating flag went up twice and nothing happened that a player could see or hear.
- The card table's lines repeat a lot (see above). Nobody else in the room talked in 13 minutes except right after
  the shot, plus one toast and one preacher chat.
- People's words after the shot don't match what they saw: "whoever it was", "Is he gone?" with me in the room,
  "take that iron off him" after it was away, and the barkeep reporting "down by the saloon" from inside it.

## What was fun

- Drawing the gun got three distinct warnings at once, before I'd fired. That felt right.
- The panic: shouts of "Get down!" and "Get in and shut it", the whole room spilling into the street, the pianist
  frozen at her keys, then people coming back asking "Is it over?" and grumbling "That's my supper ruined."
  It was ordinary again in about 2.5 minutes, which felt about right.
- Earl and the drifter keep up a believable all-night game with a running rivalry ("You're a lucky old man, Earl").
- "Let's make it worth the lamp oil." Good line.

## Top three to fix

1. **What people say should match what they saw.** "Whoever it was" and "Is he gone?" from people looking at me, a
   warning about a holstered gun, and the barkeep reporting a shot "down by the saloon" from inside it.
2. **A shove in a crowded bar should get more than one line.** Bystanders should react, and the rancher right
   beside him (and Earl, who's never in regard) should think worse of me. The town should bring it up again.
3. **Get people home and make the table feel like a table.** Strays stood minutes in the street while listed at
   their posts. At cards: a greeting, a comment when you bust, a raise, fewer repeated closers, and a visible cheat.
   Also make `walk saloon` and `walk PERSON` end where they say.
