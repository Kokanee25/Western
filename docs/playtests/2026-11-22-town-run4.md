## What broke / was flat or confusing / was fun

**Broke or confusing**
- I can't find how to knock. The screen prompt says "E: bang on the door", but no command did it. I shoved the door's glass pane instead and it broke and cut me four times ("Glass cut your chest."). A player pressing E at a shut shop door should not get that. Please check whether E really means "knock" when the door is shut.
- `shove` at 1.9 m missed; at 0.5 m it hit. The prompt said "bang on the door" at 1.9 m, so the prompt shows from further than the reach.
- `walk saloon` ends on the porch at x 8.9 and `walk barkeep` stops at the front corner, "arrived": false, at x 24. The way in is somewhere I never found; I walked to coordinates instead. A player on the porch has the saloon 17 m to the east of the porch.

1. **The saloon doesn't change.** Nobody moves for ten minutes. At least a few should get up, go to the bar, step out, come in. A town that only talks isn't a town.
2. **Knocking at a shut door.** The prompt says "E: bang on the door", but I could only break the glass. Make E knock, and stop the glass pane taking a shove at all, or make it a clear break-in.
3. **Getting in the saloon and being noticed.** `walk saloon` leaves you on the porch 17 m from the people; `walk barkeep` stalls at the front wall; and once in, sitting at their card table doesn't change anyone's view of me (`wary`, `look_away`, all zeros, from start to end).
