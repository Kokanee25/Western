# Playtest 2026-11-14: No Justice, the hideout

Three runs of the mission scene (`--world=valley --mission=no_justice_hideout`), played through the
dev bridge by a tester who hasn't read the code. Run 1 from the Ute camp, creeping in. Run 2 from the
slope (`--arrive`), opening fire with the rifle. Run 3, a short one, to test the trail. About 25
minutes of game time in all. On this machine the sky draws black with blue-lit clouds, and the
ground is a flat brown. I haven't held either against the game.

## What I did

**Run 1: the trail, then creeping in.** I start at the Ute camp with Joe 2 m off. The screen says
*"Their trail goes on south-east, toward the rimrock: follow it with Joe."* I see no camp (no lodges,
no fire, nobody but Joe) and no trail on the ground ([start](2026-11-14/hideout_start.png)). Joe is a
faint, ghostly shape the colour of the dirt ([Joe at the camp](2026-11-14/hideout_joe_camp.png)). He
didn't move or speak. I faced south-east and walked. After about 20 m the scene cut, with no fade:
I was suddenly ~800 m away on a slope with Joe behind me. Joe: *"Fresh. We are close."* The screen
said *"The hideout's over the brow below you. Go up quiet."* Six seconds later, Joe: *"When I find him,
you will not like what I do. Better you know it now."* (A good line.)

From the slope (~100 m out) I could see a rock outcrop and, past it, the roof of a shack
([arrival](2026-11-14/hideout_arrival.png)). I couldn't see any men. I crouched and crept forward. At
~50 m Joe counted them for me: *"Five. Six. There. By the fire. The red shirt."* Then: *"How do you want
it? Quiet, or now?"*, and the screen said *"Creep in, or open fire."* Even then, from the brow and
standing, the camp was hidden behind the ground in every screenshot
([from the brow](2026-11-14/hideout_over_brow.png), [standing](2026-11-14/hideout_standing_view.png)),
yet the game told me I had a clear line to all of each man. I never saw the fire and never saw a red
shirt with my own eyes. I only knew him from Joe and the readouts (he is "Kett", the only unarmed one).

I crept, crouched, to ~28 m. They spotted me: Boyd, *"Riders! On the hill!"* (we were on foot),
*"There's only two of 'em!"*, and then *"Red! Where you going? Red, you yellow son of a bitch!"*. Joe:
*"The red shirt. He runs."* The screen: *"The red shirt's barred himself in the shack."* At 25 m the
camp finally showed: a plank shack, two horses, a covered wagon
([spotted at 25 m](2026-11-14/hideout_creep_25m.png)). The men were tiny dark smudges.

The fight was good. I shot from a crouch with the revolver, through trim and around an oak board. Men
called out to each other: covering, flanking, reloading, dragging a hit man to cover, giving up.
Joe kept a running commentary (*"That one is empty." "He is loading." "He is down."*). I took a ball
in the thigh, then the hip (*"Your hip's broken."*). I tended it (*"Packed and bound it with your
bandana."*) and fought on. Dutch and Boyd died, Lew went down, and Amos and then Pete gave up. Joe shot
Amos *after* Amos had surrendered (he went out cold and later bled to death). That may be Joe's
promise coming true, but nothing marked it.

With a broken hip I limped down to the shack (very slow: ~5–9 m a minute). Close up, the shack's
walls drew see-through, with distant hills and rock painted over the planks
([at the wall](2026-11-14/hideout_shack_door.png),
[through the hole](2026-11-14/hideout_kett_through_wall.png)). I kicked a board out (and hurt my hip
doing it, a nice touch). I could then "see" Kett's chest 2.5 m away, but nothing showed on screen. I
shouted "Drop it!" twice: no answer. I waited over a minute: Joe stayed 30 m back, calm, and didn't
come for his man. I fired twice at Kett's chest and head from 2.5 m: no hit, no sound from him, no
wound. **Run 1 ended stuck**: everyone down but the red shirt, who couldn't be reached, hit or spoken
to, and Joe idle.

**Run 2: open fire from up the slope.** With `--arrive` I walked to ~50 m upright, rifle drawn, and
wasn't noticed. Joe gave the same count and question. Aiming at the red shirt showed only the brow
and a floating "Outlaw hideout" label ([rifle on the red shirt](2026-11-14/hideout_rifle_on_red.png)).
I fired anyway and hit Kett in the chest. The others went for me: *"You son of a bitch! Come on!"*,
they dragged Kett, and Boyd still shouted *"Red, you yellow son of a bitch!"* at a man being dragged
off shot. Then *"The red shirt's barred himself in the shack."* I worked the rifle from a crouch. Dutch
went down and called *"That's it, boys. Throw 'em down."*, and Lew, Boyd and Pete all gave up in the
same second. Amos fought on alone (*"Hold still..."*) until Joe shot him in the head. Kett went out
cold in the shack and died of his chest wound.

Then nothing. No message, no word from Joe about the man who killed his wife. Two surrendered men
just stood there. I waited over a minute ([after the fight](2026-11-14/hideout_after_fight.png)), walked
down to Kett's body (same see-through walls, [shack](2026-11-14/hideout_run2_shack.png)) and back to
Joe. His only line: *"Harlan. Not at me."* (my rifle was pointing his way, a good line). **Run 2
ended with no ending.**

**Run 3: the trail.** I stood still at the camp for 90 s: nothing, and Joe never set off. Then I walked
*north-west*, away from the trail: after a few seconds the scene jumped to the hideout all the same.

## What broke

- **No ending, either way.** With every outlaw dead, down or surrendered and the red shirt dead (run 2)
  or barred in (run 1), nothing happened: no screen line, no word from Joe, no next step. Over a minute
  of waiting each time.
- **The red shirt can't be hit or reached once he's in the shack** (run 1). At 2.5 m, with a hole kicked
  in the wall and the readout saying his chest was in clear view, two shots did nothing and he didn't
  react to "Drop it!". His readout never shows a mood or what he's doing, alive or dead.
- **You can't see the hideout from above.** From the slope and the brow (100 m, 50 m) the ground hides
  the whole camp in every screenshot, while the game says the men are in plain sight (and they could
  be shot). It first showed at ~25 m, too late to plan anything. The scene's whole point (look down,
  pick out the red shirt, choose) doesn't work.
- **The shack's walls draw see-through up close**: far hills and rock show over the planks. It might be
  this machine's renderer, but the rest of the scene drew fine.
- **The trail doesn't care where you go.** Any first walk, even the wrong way, cuts straight to the
  hideout. It cuts with no fade or word, from a camp with nothing in it, and Joe doesn't lead or move.
- **Internal names said aloud** in subtitles and shouts: "Crew Pete:", *"They got Crew Dutch!"*,
  *"Crew Amos's giving up!"*.
- A floating "Outlaw hideout" label hangs over the shack in the 3D view.

## What was flat or confusing

- I never saw the red shirt, or anyone's clothes. Joe says "the red shirt", but at the distances the
  scene is played at, the men are dots or hidden. I knew who he was only from the readout (the only
  unarmed man).
- "Quiet, or now?" didn't seem to change anything. Creeping in crouched got me spotted at ~28 m, and
  walking upright with a rifle at 50 m wasn't noticed. Either way the red shirt runs to the shack at
  the first alarm, so there's no way to get at him "quietly".
- Joe's promise (*"you will not like what I do"*) never paid off where I could see it. He shot a
  surrendered man in run 1, but with no line or moment to it, and he never went near the red shirt.
- *"Riders! On the hill!"* when we're on foot. *"Red, you yellow son of a bitch!"* at a man who'd just
  been shot and was being dragged.
- Five men said the same line in the same second, more than once (*"Where'd he go?"* ×5, *"Covering!"*
  ×3–4, three men giving up at once).
- Surrendered men just stand there; there's nothing to do with them. One surrendered man (Pete,
  run 2) was hit again (by me), and one (Amos, run 1) was shot by Joe. The game gives no pushback
  either way.
- With a broken hip, crouch won't toggle off. Fair, but the screen doesn't say so.

## What was fun

- Joe's voice is spare and right: *"Fresh. We are close."*, *"Five. Six. There. By the fire. The red
  shirt."*, *"How do you want it? Quiet, or now?"*, *"Harlan. Not at me."*
- The gunfight's chatter is alive: flanking, covering, dragging the wounded, a hat shot off (*"That was
  a good hat, you son of a—"*), *"My leg... damn you..."*, a group surrender called by the hurt man.
- Wounds feel real: the thigh, the broken hip that slows you to a crawl, the bandana, and kicking a
  wall with a broken hip hurting it.
- Shooting through thin boards and trim while oak stops a ball makes the shack and wagon real cover.

## Top three things to fix

1. **Give the scene an ending.** When the red shirt is dead (or taken) and the gang is broken, say so
   and say what's next, with Joe's moment (his promise) in it.
2. **Let the player see the hideout from above**, and see the red shirt as a red shirt. The view from
   the slope shows nothing while the game says everyone's in sight.
3. **Make the barred-in red shirt reachable**: hittable, answering a shout, with a door or a way in. Fix
   the shack walls drawing see-through up close, so the close fight can be seen.

(Also: the trail jumps to the hideout on any first step, in any direction, and the "Crew …" names
are said aloud.)
