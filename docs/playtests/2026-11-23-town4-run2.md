## What broke

- **The start breaks things.** At t 0.1, with no shot, the general store's crates and the undertaker's embalming table "went loose (fell whole)": 39 members broken before I did anything.
- **`walk saloon` leaves you on the porch**, not in the bar room.
- **`read room` never thinks I'm in**: `you_in: false` all run, even standing inside facing the bar at 4 m.
- **The bar is somewhere you can't go.** The barkeep, and later the drifter drinking with him, stood outside the saloon's red east wall board, below the floor height. `walk barkeep`, `walk drifter` and `walk saloon_annex` all stopped short (arrived: false) at about x 21. I could hear them but never stand with them.
- **People blink out of the street list and back**: the drifter (t 240-285) and the liveryman (t 240-285), the liveryman back on the exact same spot.
- **Sit posts that never sit**: townsman's "store_bench sit" with `seated: false` the whole time.
- **Things I was beside were marked unseen**: the barkeep's cough at t 377 and the laugh at t 567 were "seen: false" with me 4-8 m away (perhaps because of the wall between us).

## What was flat

- The saloon at noon is almost empty: only the barkeep, and the drifter for a minute or so. Five minutes of silence in the bar.
- Only five people in the whole town at noon; everyone else at dinner indoors. I couldn't find the doctor, storekeeper, smith or landlady eating anywhere I could see.
- Nobody's trade shows except the barkeep's. The townsman and loudmouth just move between benches and walks.
- The town's regard is all zeros and "wary": I can't tell any one person from another by how they treat me.
- "That's not how I heard it" as an answer to "Beer's warm. Ice wagon never came." doesn't quite follow.

## What was fun

- The porch chatter: a reply ("Might be, might be") that answered the drifter's line, then the loudmouth's grumbles. People coming and going on the walks felt alive.
- The lines themselves are good: short, dry, period. "Hot as the hinges out here" and "Whiskey's two bits, and it isn't worth it" both raised a smile.
- A laugh and a grin between the drifter and the barkeep: two people sharing a moment without me.

## Top three to fix

1. **Let the player stand at the bar with the barkeep.** Right now he and his customers are behind the saloon's east wall and lower than the floor, and `walk saloon` / `walk barkeep` can't get you there. `read room` never counts you in.
2. **Nothing should break on load:** the store's crates and the undertaker's table fall apart at t 0.1.
3. **A noon saloon with something going on**, and people who don't blink out of the list: more folk about at midday, and the drifter and liveryman staying visible while they're in town.
