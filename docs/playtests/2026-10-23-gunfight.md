# Playtest: ten gunfights in the new saloon (2026-10-23)

Played blind (no code read), through the dev bridge, headless. One fight a game, a fresh game for
each. Revolver, five rounds. Times are game seconds from the moment they went for their guns.

A note on how I played: every bridge command takes a second or more of game time while it runs,
and the game runs on while I think. So I'm slower than a player at the keys. They aimed and fired
much faster than I could turn and shoot. Count my deaths with that in mind.

## The ten fights

1. **spec_hall, seed 1. I waited.** (I meant to shoot first, but the six seconds ran out while I
   read the room.) All three called "There he is!" within a second. Brody fired first. I drew late,
   crouched and aimed. I hit Lyle in the pelvis. Lyle and the Kid traded "Cover me, I'll get round
   him!" / "Go on, I got you!" and the Kid flanked. I ran dry and was down while reloading. **~40 s,
   I lost.**
2. **spec_hall, seed 2. I shot first.** My first shot hit Brody in the chest. He went down and
   later died. Lyle shouted "Hold on, I got you!" and went to drag him, then gave it up and took
   cover behind the bar. The Kid kept firing from the back and called a flank. Lyle broke my right
   arm. My gun went away and wouldn't come out again. Then he gut-shot me. **~38 s, I lost.**
3. **spec_stair, seed 1. I waited, crouched, aiming.** The Kid on the stair fired first and hit me
   in the chest. I hit the Kid in the chest. He gave up at once ("Enough! It's yours!"). Brody and
   Lyle yelled at him ("Get up, you yellow dog!", "Kid! Pick up that gun!"). Brody shot me in the
   chest four seconds later. **~15 s, I lost.**
4. **spec_stair, seed 2. I shot first,** then tried to run for the front door. I hit Lyle. He went
   down. Brody went to rescue him. The Kid and Brody kept firing, hit my arm and gut, and I was
   down before I got to the door. **~10 s, I lost.**
5. **spec_gallery, seed 1. I waited outside.** I walked out of the front door and crouched beside
   it on the boardwalk. Brody, behind the bar, kept hitting me through the doorway (chest, left
   arm, pelvis, hand). My gun went away after the arm hit and would not come out. Lyle and the Kid
   on the gallery hardly fired. At ~40 s they said "Damn it. Lost him." / "...Gone." and gave up
   the fight. The Kid strolled down to 4 m from me, calm. Brody kept shooting alone and put me
   down. **~58 s, I lost.**
6. **spec_gallery, seed 2. I shot first.** Brody took one in the chest and went down. **The Kid
   gave up 1.5 s later, unhurt, before firing a shot**, while Lyle was still up and unhurt. Lyle
   started round the gallery to drag Brody. I shot him in the chest. Then I shot the Kid in the
   chest: I couldn't tell he'd given up. The gang never fired once. **~10 s, I won unhurt.**
7. **spec_back, seed 1. I waited, then backed off** out the front door and crouched beside it
   outside. After a "Where'd he go?" they came looking: Lyle and Brody walked to the front, Lyle
   went out and round the west side, and shot me twice from beside the door before I saw him.
   The Kid stayed on the gallery. **~22 s, I lost, never fired.** Good fight from them.
8. **spec_back, seed 2. I shot first.** Brody, 2.7 m away, took a chest hit, said "That's how it
   is? Fine!" and stayed up shooting. Lyle's shot went close by Brody ("Watch where you're
   shooting!"). The Kid hit me in the chest from the gallery. **~6 s, I lost.**
9. **spec_front, seed 1. I waited in the street, crouched, no cover.** The Kid fired first from the
   doorway (neck, "Hurt, but steady"). I put three into the Kid. After the first two he called
   "He's got me pinned!" and Brody told him "Hold, damn it! Stay in it!". After the third he gave
   up. I hit Lyle in the chest. He went down. Brody, upstairs, called "Hold on, I got you!" to
   drag Lyle three times over 40 s but never came down. He paced the gallery and hardly fired. Lyle
   gave up when I walked in beside him. The Kid died of his wounds. I shot Brody from under the
   gallery. **~100 s, I won, hurt.**
10. **spec_front, seed 2. I shot first,** then ran to the east end of the street and crouched. Lyle
    went down from my first shot and bled to death. Brody, on the balcony, called a drag but stayed
    up there and shot me in the thigh. The Kid fired from the doorway. I hit the Kid, then Brody.
    The Kid quit at once when Brody fell. **~17 s, I won, hurt.**

Score: I won 3 (6, 9, 10), lost 7. Shooting first won three of the six fights I opened. Waiting won
only fight 9.

## Did they fight?

Yes, mostly. They shot back fast and accurately. Most of my losses came within 10–40 s. They
called to each other the whole time: spotted, covering, reloading, flanking, "Cover me, I'll get
round him!". They acted on it: the Kid flanked in fights 1 and 2, and in fight 7 Lyle came out of
the saloon and round the side to find me. They took cover behind the bar and peeked. When one fell,
a friend went to drag him.

But it often fell apart once someone was down:
- The **Kid folds very easily.** In fight 6 he quit unhurt within two seconds of Brody falling.
- A man who goes to **drag a friend gets stuck doing it.** Brody (fights 9 and 10) and Lyle
  (fight 6) stayed upstairs "rescuing" a man on the floor below and never got to him. Brody spent
  40 s pacing the gallery in fight 9 and stopped fighting.
- In fight 5 two of them **gave up the fight ("...Gone.") while their friend was still shooting at
  me**, and one walked up beside me, calm.

## Who quit, and was it earned?

- **Kid, fight 3**: gave up on his first chest hit. Borderline: a chest hit is serious, but both
  friends were up and he had been winning.
- **Kid, fight 6**: gave up **unhurt**, 1.5 s after Brody fell, with Lyle still up. **Not earned.**
- **Kid, fight 9**: gave up after three chest hits. Earned.
- **Kid, fight 10**: gave up hit once, with both friends down. Earned.
- **Lyle, fight 9**: gave up wounded, down and alone, when I walked up to him. Earned.
- **Lyle and the Kid "broke off", fight 5**: they lost sight of me, said "...Gone." and went calm
  while Brody was still shooting at me from the same room. Not earned. It looked like they forgot
  the fight.
- **Brody** never quit, not even alone with both friends down (fight 9). That fits him.

## What broke

- **A hit to either arm puts my gun away, and I can't draw it again.** `weapon revolver` answers
  `ok: false` with no reason. `shoot` then clicks and fires nothing. Fights 2 and 5 ended like this.
  Maybe it's meant, but nothing tells the player why.
- **After I black out I "come round" in the street**, at about (-23.9, 6.2), 40 m from the saloon.
  The men then say "Easy, friend.", "Help you?", "Go on, then.", as if nothing had happened. Every
  lost fight ended this way.
- **The wrong wound line:** Lyle (fight 9) and Brody (fight 10), both hit in the chest, said "My
  leg... damn you...".
- **"Hey! That was me!"** comes from a man when his friend's shot passes near him (fights 3, 5, 7,
  9). It reads as if *I* shot him. "Watch where you're shooting!" (also said) reads better.
- **`gf_result` gives the wrong state for men who die later:** in fight 9 the Kid died of his wounds
  but stays "surrendered". In fight 10 Lyle bled out but stays "down". An unconscious Brody showed
  as "surrendered" in fight 6.
- **No sign that a man has given up.** `read` still showed "armed" and "fight" for men who had
  surrendered, so in fight 6 I shot a man who had given up.
- **`press shout` ("Drop it!") did nothing I could see**: no line in the events, no reaction
  (fight 9, against Brody alone).
- **Long hitches:** some single commands took 10–20 game seconds (a `shoot 2` in fight 5, a
  `shoot 1` in fight 8 that ran on until I blacked out). The first frame read showed physics at
  ~31 ms.
- After a reload the hammer is left at half cock. The next `shoot` "clicks" once before firing.
  Not a bug, but worth knowing.
- One shot from under the gallery hit Brody in both the chest and the head (fight 9).

## What was fun

- The talk. "Kid's quit on us!" / "Get up, you yellow dog!", and "He's got me pinned!" / "Hold, damn
  it! Stay in it!". You can follow the fight by ear. It makes the gang feel like three people.
- Being hunted in fight 7: they lost me, said so, came looking, and one went round the outside.
  That was the best fight.
- A wounded man calling for help and a friend going to him is a good beat.
- Fight 9 had a proper arc: the Kid wavering, then breaking, Lyle down, Brody alone upstairs.

## What was flat

- The fights are very short and the first shot decides most of them. One chest hit usually drops a
  man, me included. There's little middle to a fight.
- The rescue turns into standing about. A man stuck "rescuing" upstairs is just a target.
- The Kid quitting at the first sign of trouble makes some fights end with no fight.
- They rarely came at me through the room. Most of the time they held their spots and shot.

## The top three things to fix

1. **The rescue that never arrives.** A man going to drag a friend should get there (down the
   stairs) or give it up and fight. Brody spent 40 s pacing the gallery in fight 9.
2. **Quitting and breaking off need a reason.** The Kid gave up unhurt with a friend still
   standing (fight 6). Two men said "...Gone." and went calm while Brody was still shooting at me
   (fight 5).
3. **Tell the player what happened to his gun and to him.** An arm hit silently puts the gun away
   for good. After blacking out you wake in the street with the gang friendly. And a man who has
   given up looks the same as one who hasn't. (Fix the chest wound's "My leg" line while there.)

**Verdict:** they fought: fast, loud and together, and they hunted me when they lost me. But once
one man fell the gang often came apart: the Kid folded too early, and the rescuer got stuck.
