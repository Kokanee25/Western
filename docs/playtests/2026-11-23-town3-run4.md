# Town playtest 2026-11-23, gate 3 run 4 (seed 54, 21:00, headless, the three deeds)

Headless run: nothing was drawn, so everything below comes from the text read-outs (`read people`, `read folk`, `read room`, `read regard`, `read news`, `events`, `cards`). I can't say how anything looked: faces, gestures, whether a laugh or a flinch showed on screen. Times are the game's seconds since start (t). Real time 15:48:52 to 15:58:30 (about 9.5 minutes). The town clock was set to 21:00 at speed 0.03, so it stayed close to 21:00 all run.

## What I did

- Started at the street's middle. `walk saloon` took me to x 8.9, a doorway 17 m west of the room where everyone actually was (x 25-33, behind the "saloon annex" place). I walked on and stood in the room by the bar and card table (t 36).
- Eleven people inside: barkeep at the counter, rancher, miner and loudmouth drinking at the bar, Earl and the drifter at cards, fiddler and pianist playing, liveryman, storekeeper and the saloon girl talking in the back. Room "lively" (0.69).
- Watched about a minute. Then the shot, then a walk east down the street (to x 70) and back, then the shove, then cards, then stood up and watched 40 s more. Stopped the game.

## The deeds

**Regard at the start (t 2):** every one of 19 townsfolk "wary", all numbers 0. Town: wary.

**(a) A shot into the air, t 107, inside the saloon.** I drew the revolver at t 105. Three people spoke at once at the draw: barkeep "Hey now. There's no call for that.", drifter "Whoa, now. Easy with that iron.", fiddler "Put that away, mister."; then barkeep "Put that up or get out." Fired once, aimed, straight up. At once: Earl "Get down! Everybody down!", loudmouth "Lord almighty!", saloon girl "Get in. Get in and shut it.", undertaker (out in the street) "Up the street. Was that up the street?" Everybody but the drifter and pianist ran out. Regard after: all 11 in the room still "wary", but fear up 0.15-0.2, trust down 0.12-0.18, respect down 0.08.

**(b) A shove, t 298, the loudmouth at the bar.** Took three tries: twice `walk loudmouth` / `walk` to his side left me 1 m away and the shove "shoved at nothing". From the other side it hit: "staggered back". The loudmouth said nothing. No one said anything about it. The room went "hushed" (0.05). The pianist ducked and hid, and said 27 s later "Well. That's my supper ruined." Regard after: loudmouth, barkeep, drifter, fiddler, saloon girl went to "cold" (liking -0.2 to -0.3). Rancher and miner, a metre from him, did not change.

**(c) Cards, t 343-426.** `sit s` sat me at the table. Two hands. First: checked, drew 3, drifter bet 2, I folded (the nod timed out). Second: a pair of fives, checked, drew 3, checked, and took the pot with two pair, fives and fours. Nobody said a word about my win. Regard after: barkeep, drifter, fiddler, saloon girl came back from "cold" to "wary" (respect back near 0); Earl and the liveryman went slightly positive. The loudmouth stayed "cold". I stood up in the middle of the next deal ("You're dealt in: a pair of twos") and no one reacted.

## Lines and repeats

- **98 spoken lines** in the run (t 0 to 488, about 8 minutes of game time), most at the card table.
- **20 lines were said word-for-word more than once**; the worst were the three after-the-shot news lines (4, 3 and 3 times), told by people standing together who had all seen it.
- About half the card table's lines repeated within 8 minutes.

## What broke

- `walk saloon` goes to x 8.9, 17 m away from the room where everybody drinks (x 25-33). The people who fled ran to that same spot.
- After the shot the storekeeper wandered 68 m down the street and stayed there about two minutes, while `read folk` still said "in".
- `walk loudmouth` stopped 1 m short ("walked 0 m") twice, so `shove` hit "nothing within reach". I had to pick a spot round the other side.

## What was flat

- No one ever points at me. Eleven people saw the shot from a few metres; they ask "Who was it?" and gossip "somebody was shooting". The news record knows it was me.
- The shove gets no words at all, from him or anyone; the pianist's "That's my supper ruined" is the only line, and it doesn't fit.
- Winning a pot gets no line from the losers.
- The card game rolls straight on, with its usual banter, while the room is "hushed" after the shove.
- Small talk ("Beer's warm. Ice wagon never came.") 50 s after a gunshot, while the room is still tense.

## What was fun

- The draw and shot reaction: fast, loud, and every person did something (run, hide, stay put).
- The room's life before the trouble: laughs that spread (loudmouth, then rancher and miner), a toast ("To the last one. And the next."), "Ain't no sheriff tonight, boys!".
- Cards work: sitting down, the prompts, being asked "You in or out?", winning a pot. Regard moved with it.

## Top three to fix

1. **Repeated lines after the shot.** Nine people saying the same three sentences to each other ten times, about a shot they all saw. Give each a line of his own, or let one tell it and the others answer.
2. **People who saw the shooter don't know who it was.** "Who was it? Anybody see?" from a man 4 m away. They should look at me, name me or talk about "the stranger".
3. **The card table repeats itself:** about half its banter came round again within 8 minutes (Earl's "Two pair... Don't look at me like that." three times). Plus: no word at all about the shove or my winning pot.
