## What else broke

- **Kicking the shack door is a lottery.** Same spot, same stance: one run the door gave on the 3rd kick, one on the 5th and
  one on the 13th, with `It doesn't give. Your foot does: bruised.` between. Standing a step to the side, my kicks landed
  on the shack's side wall (`hideout_shack/left/siding`) 9 times in a row and I got no hint that I was at the wrong wall.
  By the end my foot was bruised and one run said `Your leg won't take a kick` and I couldn't force the door at all (I shot Kett through the wall instead: the `outside` end).
- **Joe goes down and the scene carries on without him.** In the arrest run `joe went down` at the hideout (`I cannot stand on it`) 36 m from the shack. The
  question `Are you going to stop me?` was still asked and Joe, held down, never moved. It ended well, but it reads odd.
- **Silence answers Joe.** In the murder run I didn't nod or shake; after about 10 s Joe said `Then take him` and the state went to `stop`, same as a nod.
  I shot Kett afterwards anyway and got `murder`.
- **Friendly fire on Joe.** Shooting Kett at the shack, `hit joe hit in the hand_r` and `Harlan. Not at me.` Joe was 1 m from me.
- The gang is hard to hit at 30+ m with `look X; shoot`: about two in three shots wasted, and on two occasions the line crossed men who'd already surrendered (`lew hit in the chest`, `boyd hit in the abdomen`). Needs closing in.
- The No Justice start leaves the doctor in the front room for good. Being let in is by walking up to the door (no knock); the knock prompt at that door is a different thing (see 1-2).
