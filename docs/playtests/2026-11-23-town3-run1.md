## Who stood still

## What broke

- The general store's crates fell apart on their own at t=0.1: 30 members broke, with "no shot".
- The townsman stood still for 2.5 minutes while the game said he was on his way somewhere.
- After the store had been opened, a second E gave "Directly. I'm in the middle of something." It doesn't fit.
- I was moved about 0.9 m sideways at the store door without walking or any shove event.
- The town clock barely moved: every block still read 7.0 after 10 minutes.

## What was flat

- The saloon was shut and the town was thin at 7 am: about 7 people on a long street, often 100 m or more apart.
- The store wife "heard" the knock but never came.
- People vanished and reappeared at the livery for no visible reason.

## What was fun

- The knock got a quick, natural answer, and a follow-up when I lingered ("or is it just the shade?").
- The drifter asking the liveryman for work felt like a real town.
