# The town's gate: five blind runs (townsfolk step 11, #1162)

Build: `main` at PR #1161 (build 5116) plus the bridge's `read folk`. Valley, `--no-murder`, no mission.
Five fresh agents that had not read the code played 10 real minutes each (clock at 0.03: 10 real
minutes = 10 game minutes): run 1 at 07:00, run 2 at 12:00, run 3 at 17:30, run 4 at 21:00, run 5 at
12:00 with one shot in the air at 3:00. Each run's own report: `2026-11-22-town-run1.md` … `run5.md`;
raw logs in `build/town/runN/` (not kept in the repo).

**No screen.** Lavapipe crashed in its shader compiler (libgallium) the moment the camera turned into
the yard, as it did in step 10, so every run was headless on the bridge's read-outs
(`read people|folk|room|regard|news|fetch`, `events`). Questions that need eyes or ears are marked
"can't answer from text"; the looks, the laughs' sound and the frame are not judged here.

**A run thrown away.** The first run 1 used `clock on` (a 45-minute day), so its 10 minutes ran
07:00 to 12:22. My setup fault, not the game's; redone with `clock 0.03` (two blind rounds on one
thing is the rule, and this is the same round: nothing about the game had changed).

## Verdict: the gate is not passed

The gate asks for ordinary life, believable talk and sound, something changing, and one unscripted
moment worth telling. Seen on read-outs alone:

- **Ordinary life: no, by day.** At 07:00 and 12:00 three men are outdoors (07:00: smith, townsman,
  undertaker; 12:00: loudmouth, townsman, undertaker); at 17:30 eight. The other ~22 are "out" or
  indoors. Each of those men held one position to the centimetre for all 10 minutes (distinct
  positions in the logs: 1 each; `from_post` 0.0). Whether they animate in place (lean, work) can't be
  seen from text.
- **Doors and the saloon: shut.** The saloon is `open: false`, empty and "hushed" at 07:00, 12:00
  and 17:30 (the barkeep alone at 17:30); the store never answers a knock. Only 21:00 has a room.
- **The night room: yes.** At 21:00 11 people are in, mood "lively" 0.55-0.70; coughs, grins, lifts,
  toasts and laughs are logged with a named source (run 4).
- **Regard never moves.** "wary", `look_away`, every dial 0.0 in all five runs, including after
  sitting at the card table (run 4) and after a shot (run 5).

## The repetition row (the four no-mission runs, plus the shot run)

Fails. Each run starts the same world from the same state, so the same hour plays out the same:

- **Runs 2 and 5 (both noon) are identical until the shot.** Loudmouth "Hot enough to curl a board." at
  13-15 s, townsman the same line at 73-78 s; same posts, same positions. The bark's wording is by
  the man's seed (intended); its time and the order of who speaks are not varied between runs.
- **Run 3 (17:30):** one bark (storekeeper, `day.store_last`, 71 s), one cough at 7 s, one at 377 s.
- **Run 4 (21:00):** the card table repeats lines inside the run (every deal, "Check.", "Ante up, boys").
  The toast "To the last one. And the next." is said by three different men (rancher 143 s, miner 241 s,
  loudmouth 420 s). Room bits: cough ×14, lift ×8, toast ×4, laugh ×4 (three men), grin ×3, stretch ×3.
- **Paths:** no man walks anywhere in 10 minutes by day except when a shot moves him (run 5: loudmouth 7
  positions, townsman 6).

## Faults found (not fixed here; each is bigger than a line)

1. **The day streets are empty and static** (07:00, 12:00, 17:30): 3 / 3 / 8 outdoors, no moves. By the
   roster's data (`config/valley_folk.json` day blocks) this is what's written; it needs the roster
   given more outdoor errands and walks (a Phase 2 data job, with the spots' animations checked on screen).
2. **The saloon is shut by day and the store shut at noon** (`open: false`; the storekeeper "dinner").
   By design, but a blind player finds a dead town.
3. **Regard never moves, not even after a shot or a shove.** (`TownRegard` bands stay at "wary", 0.0.)
4. **Same hour, same run:** nothing in the town's timing varies between sessions (see the repetition row).
5. **A shot in the air drew invented casualties** ("Is he dead?", "Give him air") with nobody hurt.
6. **The undertaker spoke at 180.0, before the shot at 180.5.** A half second early on the agent's clock
   (event times are the bridge's, rounded): not reproduced as a leak (I shot from three positions and
   facings by hand: the undertaker's line came after each shot).
7. **Run 5 logged "Walk_undertaker: 214 members broke"** after the shot and `members_broken: 214`, and
   the loudmouth stood at y = -0.36 (under the street) afterwards. **Not reproduced** from 100,0,-2
   (facing west, pitch ±80), 105,0,-8 (east) or 105,0,-2 (north); `members_broken` stayed 0. Needs
   the exact position and facing; recorded in the lane file.
8. **Bridge/tool gaps for a blind player:** `walk saloon_porch` "no way there" (run 3); `walk barkeep`
   stalls at the building's corner (run 4); `sit` chose a chair outside the saloon in the old fast-clock
   run; a `shove` at 0.5 m broke the store's glass pane and cut the player four times (run 4).
   Knocking is `key E` (now in `tools/playtest.md`).
