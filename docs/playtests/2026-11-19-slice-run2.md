# Playtest, 2026-11-19, slice run 2

**Played without pictures.** The sandbox's software renderer runs out of memory, so I played the
game headless, by its text answers only (`read all`, `read people`, `read look`, `events`, `story`,
`cards`, `moment_result`). I never saw a frame. I can't say how anything looked, only what the

One play-through. About 20 real minutes, about 1,180 game seconds. I did not crash.

## How the game was started, and a big finding

- `--mission=sheriff` gives only the prologue (dawn, the wounded sheriff, the doctor's door). It ends on a
  card: "The doctor takes him in... (Enter: start again)". Enter restarts the prologue. It does not
  lead to the town at night, the saloon or the next morning.
- To reach No Justice I restarted with `--mission=no_justice`. It starts the next morning, with
  the sheriff dead, "the funeral due tomorrow" and a revolver already "given". So the story from the prologue to
  No Justice isn't joined up in anything I could start. I had to cut between them.
- The saloon night, the card game and the morning after were played in the free valley, not as scripted beats.
  I used `time N` (several times), `goto` (to the saloon, bank steps and saloon steps) and `walk`.

## What broke

- **The story doesn't join.** The sheriff mission ends on "start again". No Justice starts from a built morning with
  the sheriff already dead. I found no way from one to the other.
- **The doctor's door.** In the saloon shooting, I carried the wounded man to the door, knocked, and nobody came.
  The "doctor" was in the saloon yard, 40 m off. "doc" stood still inside at the same spot all game.
  Two different men are called doctor ("doctor" and "doc"). The wounded man died on the step. In No Justice
  the door also took 43 s and many knocks to open. The prompt "E: bang on the door" keeps showing after you are inside
  the doorway (seen at 46, 48 and 50 s).
- **Hideout fight.** I was out cold after five shots at a distance of 30 m, on `blood_ml 4977` (23 ml lost),
  "Bone broken". I can't tell what hit me. No one told me.
- **Camp.** "Joe talks with the survivors" but there are no survivors in the people list.
- **`walk hideout`: "no way there"**, and `walk storekeeper` took 215 s for 154 m and stopped short ("arrived false").
- **Pressing the wound.** The game says "Hold E on his wound". I found no bridge key for it (`press interact`, `use`,
  `grab` don't exist; `press shove` picked him up instead). So I could not test packing. The result says "poor".
- **"20 pieces of timber broke"** appears for a single revolver shot in the saloon yard, and at the start of the ride.
  I don't know what broke.
- **Earl.** Joe says Earl was shot, the story says "earl: undertaker", but "earl" is also alive and playing cards.

## What was flat or confusing

- The long sheriff carry is 8 minutes of nothing but the same four mutters.
- A nod answers everything, which is neat, but there is no way to say anything else. I could not "talk".
- In the saloon the card game ran fine and nothing came of it. No stranger, no accusation, no cheat.
- A man died on the street, and a day later he's still lying there. The undertaker walked past. The town did not clear him.
- The prompt "E: bang on the door" shows at the saloon too, which has no door to bang on.
- "Wait here" in the camp: I did not know when I could move. The scene ended on its own at about 130 s.

## What was fun

- The sheriff's first lines are good. "Rode up easy, like he wanted directions. Called me friend. Then he shot me."
- The card game is a real game: ante, check, call, draw, a showdown ("A pair of jacks. Thank you kindly."), and nod/shake to
  answer. I liked folding by shaking my head.
- Joe: "A hat and a rag at my neck, and I am nobody's Indian." Good character.
- The gang's shouting ("Covering!", "There's only two of 'em!", "Cover me, I'll get round him!") sounds like a fight.
  They flank.
- Drawing a gun at a card table: instant, believable talk-down lines.

## Answers

**1) The saloon scene.** I can't say how it looked. It read natural until I drew: the barkeep, Earl and
the drifter each told me to put it away before I fired. The room had no script of its own. When I fired once into
the drifter's chest at 1.7 m, he went down and "Take it! Take whatever you want!" came a moment before the shot. People ran,
cowered or called out, and the barkeep ran for the doctor. Nobody drew a gun back, nobody tried to stop me.
The timber line was odd (20 pieces broke).

**2) The shot man and the doctor's.** I carried him by tapping E, 44 m to the doctor's steps, in about 50 s.
I knocked. Nobody opened. The doctor was still in the saloon yard. He bled out about 77 s after being shot, on the step.
A player would feel the game had no answer for that. In the sheriff prologue the doctor opened in about 10 s.

**3) One shot in the air, street, noon.** In order (game seconds after the shot): at 0 s everyone near went "alarmed";
at 1 s the townsman: "Get down! Everybody down!" and the loudmouth: "Lord almighty!"; at 3 s they were "cowering" or
"running", the doctor "watching"; at 14 s, still cowering and watching; at 30 s the same; by 70 s most were "calm" again,
the storekeeper "shaken", and they said "...They gone?" at 57 s. So about a minute to an ordinary street.
Nobody came to me, nobody was armed, nobody asked what I'd done. The dead drifter lay 1 m from me the
whole time and no one remarked on him except "Is he dead?" at 14 s.

**4) Did the town remember the next morning?** Not that I could see. At 06:30 everyone was "calm". Their opinion
of me (`toward_you`) was blank. The dead man still lay in the street. The only line was the storekeeper's "...They gone?" from the night.
The doc never opened. The gang (still at the hideout) logged "notice" on me from 2 km away on the first morning check.
I wasn't told of any consequence of killing a man at the card table.

**5) Three worst problems:**
1. The wounded man's doctor doesn't come. The door stayed shut, the "doctor" stood in the saloon, and a man bled to death on the
   step. The "bang on the door" prompt also repeats once you are in.
2. The story doesn't join up. The sheriff mission ends on "start again", No Justice starts after it with the sheriff already
   dead, and the saloon night has no scripted event, so a new player gets three separate things.
3. The town doesn't answer a killing. A body stays in the street into the next day, nobody refers to the shot man, and
   the street is calm again in about a minute.
