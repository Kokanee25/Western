# Playtest 2026-11-12: Joe, the man at your side

Blind playtest through the dev bridge, about 70 minutes. I read only the playtest brief. Headless, so
everything here comes from `read all`, `joe state`, `events` and `gf_result`, not from pictures.

## What I did

1. Started the town with `--joe`. Walked Main Street west 25 m, ran 26 m, turned round and walked
   back, stopped and stood.
2. Told Joe to wait (look at him, shout), walked off about 36 m east, looked back and shouted to call
   him. It didn't reach him. Walked closer and tried at 21 m, then 16 m. Neither worked. At about 9 m
   it did. Told him to wait once more, walked off, called him from about 7 m, and walked off east with
   him following.
3. Four fights (fresh game each time): `spec_front` seed 1 (I shot first), `spec_front` seed 2 (I
   crouched and waited for them to draw), `spec_hall` seed 1 (I shot first at close range), `spec_back`
   seed 1 (I shot first). I was down inside 15 s in the back fight, so I played `spec_back` seed 1 a
   second time: crouched, drew and fired at the man behind me first.

## Joe on the walk

- **Where he keeps:** behind me and a little to the side (about 2 m off my line), settling at almost
  exactly 6 m every time I stopped. Walking, he dropped back to 7–9 m. When I ran, he fell to 15 m
  but closed back to 6 m within about 3 s of my stopping. Fine and sensible.
- **In my way?** No. When I turned round and walked back through where he was, he never blocked me
  and never ended up in front of me on the walk. `in_your_line` stayed false the whole time.
- **Wait:** works from close up. He says "I will wait here." and stays put. The second time I told him
  to wait he said nothing.
- **Come:** only works from close up. From 36 m, 21 m and 16 m, the shout with Joe dead centre in my
  view did **not** call him. Each time it came out as "You! Step out here and face me!" ("you shouted
  a man out"), aimed at somebody else. The storekeeper answered "Please, mister..." and "Hey—!
  There's no call for that." At about 9 m and 7 m the same shout gave "Come on." and he came. So you
  can send him off, but you can't call him back from any real distance. That's the reason you'd want
  the call at all.
- When called he says nothing back. A word ("Coming.") would tell you it worked.
- Odd: Brody, Lyle and the Kid walked right up to me in the street (4–7 m) and then wandered off to
  drink. Joe paid them no attention (he was armed, they were armed, nobody reacted).

## Joe in the fights

**1. `spec_front` seed 1 (I shot first): about 190 s, I lost (bled out from a chest hit).**
- Joe fired first, before me, and his round passed 1.8 m from me.
- He then moved about 8 m *forward*, towards the gunmen, and took cover there.
- He spent about 12 s of the fight in front of my gun. Once, `look kid` reported "in the way: joe at
  5.0 m" just as I fired twice. I didn't hit him, but only by luck.
- **He shot the Kid**, the man I had been shooting at for a minute: chest and arm. The Kid gave up and
  later bled to death. This is exactly what he's not meant to do.
- Then he kept going forward, past the gunmen, to about 21 m from me. He said "Where did he go?" and
  "lost sight of you", and when I went down he "stood down: lost you for good". I was lying 11 m from
  him with Brody and Lyle still there. Everybody went calm.
- Calls: "You are hit. Stay low." (good, but stiff), "He is going round on the front." (three times;
  odd English), "One goes round. front." (broken), "Above us. Up there." (useful), "That one is down."

**2. `spec_front` seed 2 (I crouched and let them draw): about 210 s, I won (Brody dead, Kid shot
and later dead, Lyle down).**
- Joe was **hit in the neck within 4 s of the draw** and went out cold, "can't feel his legs", for
  about 34 s. He stood in the open the whole time ("no cover he can reach").
- He got up ("I am still here.") and walked straight into the space in front of me, 6 m ahead and 8°
  off my aim, while I was firing at Lyle.
- Then for over a minute he said **"Where did he go?" about every 5 seconds (11 times)** while I sat
  still, crouched, 6–7 m from him. Then "stand down: lost you for good". He never found me again.
- He fired no shots that I saw after going down. He did nothing to help in this fight.

**3. `spec_hall` seed 1 (close quarters, I shot first): about 285 s, unfinished. Lyle and the Kid
dead, Brody standing, I ran out of ammunition.**
- Joe hid behind me for the first ~60 s ("pinned down") with five rounds and didn't fire.
- Hit in the pelvis at 74 s. After that his state read **"tend his wound" for over three minutes**,
  sitting in the open with the same text frozen ("3 s since your last shot (208 s ago)"), firing about
  twice in that time.
- Once I was empty, Joe was the only gun left on our side. Brody fired at least ten more shots, and
  the fight never ended.
- Shouting at Joe in a fight gives "Drop it! Hands where I can see 'em!", so you can't order him about
  once the shooting starts.
- Good calls: "He is loading.", "He is down.", "That one is empty.", "Right side. A man."

**4. `spec_back` seed 1, first try (I shot first at Brody): about 15 s, I lost.**
- Joe called "Behind you!" just after the draw. That was the right call (Lyle was behind me and shot
  me three times), and also "Harlan! Get down." and "One on your right." Best callouts of the session.
- Joe was hit twice in the chest and went out cold, "bleeding inside 95.8 ml/s". A minute later he
  was still out cold but alive ("a wound that would have killed him: out cold while it's on"). So the
  can't-die rule held.

**4b. `spec_back` seed 1, second try (crouched, shot the man behind first): about 125 s, unfinished
when I stopped, nobody down.**
- This was Joe at his best. He took cover on the right, faced Lyle (the man behind me), hit him in the
  belly, called "He is loading.", "Right side. A man.", "Above us. Up there.", "Harlan. Behind." He
  wasn't in my line once (0 s).
- Even so, he shot a man I'd been shooting at a moment before (I'd hit Lyle's hat and head first), but
  I had turned away from Lyle by then, so that felt fair.

## What broke

- **The call doesn't carry.** Look at Joe, shout, from 16 m or more: the game treats it as calling a
  man out to fight instead of calling Joe. It happened three times (36 m, 21 m, 16 m).
- **Joe loses you while you're close by.** In fights 1 and 2 he said "Where did he go?" over and over
  and then "lost you for good" while I was 6–11 m away and hadn't moved (crouched in both).
- **Joe stuck in "tend his wound"** for 3+ minutes (fight 3), with the status text frozen, in the
  open, mostly not shooting.
- **The fight doesn't end** when one side can do no more: fight 3 went on with me empty and Brody
  shooting at the walls. In fight 2 it went on for ~90 s with Lyle "down" until I shot him again.
- Not Joe, but it cost a lot of time: most of my shots went into walls and framing. `look` turned me
  to men whose path was blocked ("framing stud ... stops a ball"), and `shoot N` fired all N rounds
  even after the target was dead (I put six more into the dead Kid and emptied my belt).

## What was flat or confusing

- Joe's English. "I will wait here.", "I am still here.", "He is going round on the front.", "One goes
  round. front.", "That one is down." He sounds like a phrasebook next to the gang's "Keep him busy,
  I'm going round!". Two of the lines don't parse.
- "There." as his first call in every fight tells you nothing.
- "Where did he go?" repeated every 5 s for a minute is noise, and it's about *me*.
- No reply to "Come on."
- You can't steer him in a fight at all (the shout becomes "Drop it!").

## What was fun

- When it works, his callouts are really useful: "Behind you!", "Harlan. Behind.", "He is loading.",
  "That one is empty.", "Above us. Up there." I'd have played the back fight better if I'd listened to
  the first one.
- On the walk he's easy company: never in the way, keeps a sensible gap, catches up after a run.
- Fight 4b felt like having a partner: he took the man behind me while I faced the front.
- He goes down hard and gets up again, and it reads as tough rather than immortal.

## The top three things to fix about Joe

1. **He must leave your target and your line of fire alone.** In fight 1 he went forward in front of
   my gun (about 12 s in my line, "joe in the way at 5 m" as I fired) and shot the Kid, whom I was
   working on. In fight 2 he got up and walked into the gap in front of me. He should stay level with
   you or behind, and take the other men.
2. **He mustn't lose you, or sit out the fight.** "Where did he go?" ×11 and "lost you for good" with me
   6–11 m away, crouched; and three minutes in "tend his wound" in fight 3 while a gunman was still
   shooting. In both cases he stopped being any use.
3. **The call has to reach him.** Wait and come only work from about 10 m. Beyond that, the shout
   picks a fight with a stranger instead. Also give him a spoken reply to "come", and plainer English
   in his callouts ("I'll wait here", "One's going round the front").
