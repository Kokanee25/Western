**Nobody, in either try.** `read fetch` stayed empty the whole time (`calls: [], events: [],
log: []`) for both men: 197 s after the townsman went down, 140 s after the undertaker did. Nobody
walked toward the doctor's (x 74.6) or anywhere else. Only `harlan_seen` changed (false to true,
around the first shot).

### 2. How long until the doctor came out, how did he come, what did he do?

**He never came.** No doctor showed up in `read people`, before or after. The townsman lay at x 46,
28 m from the doctor's door. He sat cowering and wounded for about 107 s, collapsed again at +107 s
("went down" a second time, t 176.7), was unconscious by +136 s, and **died of blood loss at +181 s**
(t 250.3). Nobody touched him.

### 4. What looked wrong, odd or unrealistic

1. **No doctor, nobody fetches him.** A man bled out for three minutes, 28 m from the doctor's
   door, at noon, with two witnesses. Nobody went to him or ran for help.
2. **"Went down" didn't mean down.** After the third leg hit the event said "townsman went down",
   but his mood stayed "cowering" and "shaken". Then a second "went down" came 107 s later,
   and only then did his mood read "down". Two "fell" events for one fall.
3. **Legs that don't give.** The townsman took three thigh hits and the undertaker four (one pelvis)
   and both stayed standing. The undertaker even walked about 8 m on two shot thighs and a shot
   pelvis.
4. **Damage with no shooter.** The undertaker took seven "hit" wounds (thigh, forearm, abdomen) at
   t 393-397 while my gun was holstered. It looked like him falling into or through his walk's rail
   (which broke) and counting as wounds. His wound count stayed at 4.
5. **One pistol ball broke 214 pieces of timber** (the shot through the undertaker's balcony baluster).
   After that, a loose piece (`@RigidBody3D@79331`) hung in my line of fire and ate the next shot.
6. **Witnesses go calm in about a minute.** The loudmouth and the undertaker were both "calm" and back
   at their posts 65 s after a man was shot in the street, with him still lying there.
7. **"Stand back, give him air."** was said 60-90 m from either victim, with nobody near either one.
   The line fits the scene, but the speaker wasn't there and did nothing after.
8. **The street is nearly empty at noon:** three townsfolk in about 240 m of Main Street.
9. `look NAME thigh_r` landed one ball in the left thigh. At 12 m that's fine, just noted.
