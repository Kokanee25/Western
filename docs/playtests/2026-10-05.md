# Playtest, 2026-10-05

About 50 minutes of play over two games (the first town burnt down, so I started a second one).
Played blind through the bridge at about one frame a second. Note that this is slow: every
command takes a second or more, so anything decided in under two seconds (a duel, being shot
first) was out of my hands. I've said where that matters.

## What I did

**First game.** I started in the street at 17:00, facing west into a low sun. The street looked
superb.

![The street at the start](2026-10-05/01_start.png)

I turned round and saw an armed man standing out at the range behind me, minding his own business.

![The man at the range](2026-10-05/02_outlaw_at_range.png)

I walked to the general store (the storekeeper stood behind his counter), then to the saloon.
The saloon at dusk is the best-looking place in the game.

![The saloon bar](2026-10-05/05_saloon.png)

I drew on the barkeep and held my sights on him. He did nothing until I shouted. Then he put his
hands up: "Take it! Take whatever you want!" Just then the gang walked in behind me.

![Sights on the barkeep](2026-10-05/07_sights_on_barkeep.png)
![Lyle walks in behind me](2026-10-05/08_shout_barkeep.png)

They warned me ("Keep that iron where it is", "Don't you do it") and I turned to face Brody, half
a metre away, gun still in my hand.

![Brody, face to face](2026-10-05/09_brody_close.png)

I tried to holster to back down. Brody shot me in the chest before I'd finished. I blacked out and
came round four seconds later in the back corner of the general store, completely unhurt.

![Coming round in the store](2026-10-05/10_came_round.png)

The gang left town. I drew on a man on the porch: he put his hands up and begged. I fired into
the air: everyone in town shouted "Get down!" or "Lord almighty!" at the same instant and
cowered where they stood.

![The porch loafer begs](2026-10-05/12_aim_loafer.png)

I brought the gang back in and followed them to the bar. I walked up to Lyle with my gun
holstered and called him out. He shot me in the chest on the spot, in the bar. Out again, back in
the store, unhurt again.

Then I called out the man at the range from 10 m. He said "Whenever you're ready", then "Your
funeral, friend", and shot me in the chest less than a second later. I tried again, drawing the
moment I shouted: he shot me in the head before my first shot left.

![Shot in the duel at the range](2026-10-05/18_call_out_outlaw.png)

Next I set up in the street, gun out, crouched, and shot the Kid first as the gang walked up. He
went down gut-shot ("Oh God, not the belly...") and died about 40 seconds later. Brody shouted
"Easy, I got you!" to rescue him but got stuck in the saloon's doorway, where I shot him in the
chest. Lyle then stood behind the open saloon door for nearly two minutes calling "Hold on, I got
you!" without moving. When I came round the door he shot me twice and I blacked out again.

![The Kid goes down](2026-10-05/21_shoot_kid.png)
![Brody stuck in the doorway](2026-10-05/23_brody_in_door.png)
![Lyle fires through the smoke](2026-10-05/31_lyle_behind_door.png)

I threw a stick of dynamite at the livery (it went off at the hitching rail in front, 5 m from
me), then set a lit stick inside the livery door. It went off before I'd got clear and peppered my
legs. The livery caught fire and burnt to its frame in about a minute and a half, and the fire
spread down the street.

![The stick goes off at the rail](2026-10-05/35_stick_thrown.png)
![The livery alight](2026-10-05/41_livery_fire_2.png)
![The livery's sign floating over the ashes](2026-10-05/44_fire_4.png)

I set the general store's door alight with `ignite` and watched from the street. The whole south
side burnt down. The storekeeper, the porch loafer and both men on the bench burnt to death at
their posts, all within five seconds of each other, without a word. The man by the jail had
already died the same way.

![The store's front burning](2026-10-05/49_store_burning_2.png)
![The south side, a burning skeleton](2026-10-05/50_store_burning_3.png)

Last in that game I found the man from the range leaning on the saloon's side wall, put the
shotgun on him, and he surrendered ("Enough! It's yours!"). I shot him anyway. He shouted "I'm
unarmed!" five times at once and went down. The barkeep only cowered.

![He surrenders to the shotgun](2026-10-05/53_drop_it.png)

**Second game.** I let the gang come in and followed them without drawing. The Kid passed so
close I could see his face under the hat. They drank, then Lyle and the Kid went to lean on the
storekeeper. Lyle drew on him: "Open the drawer. Slow." That was a great scene.

![The Kid walks past](2026-10-05/58_gang_passes.png)
![The gang at the bar](2026-10-05/59_kid_at_bar.png)
![Lyle robbing the storekeeper](2026-10-05/60_harassing_storekeeper.png)

I drew on Lyle and shouted. Lyle and the Kid shot me twice in the chest at once. I came round in
the same store, a few metres from them, unhurt. I called the Kid out in the street ("Suits me.")
and drew: from 18 m he hit me three times out of three in three seconds. A shot that missed me
went on down the street and dropped the man at the range, 50 m behind me. When the gang rode in
again, they were already in a fight with me and put four rounds in me before my shotgun fired.

Last, I shot a townsman's hat off from 2 m. "He shot my hat clean off!" He was also hit in the
head, and stood there shaken.

![After the hat shot](2026-10-05/70_after_hat.png)

## What broke

- **Blacking out heals you completely.** Six times I was shot (chest, head, gut, a broken bone,
  twelve wounds once) and four seconds later I woke in the same corner of the general store with
  "Unhurt." and 5000 ml of blood. Nothing is said about it. Once that corner was where Lyle and
  the Kid had just shot me, a few metres off.
- **Calling a man out in the saloon started a gunfight, not a duel.** I faced Lyle at the bar,
  gun holstered, and shouted. He shot me in the chest at once, saying "Something I can do for
  you?" at the same moment.
- **The man at the range talks to you from anywhere.** He stood 20–30 m away, out of sight, and
  his lines ("Keep that iron where it is", "One more move...", "Go on, then.") came up as
  subtitles during the fights in the saloon and store, which had nothing to do with him.
- **The LIVERY sign and its lantern hang in the air after the building has burnt away.** The lit
  lantern stays lit on nothing.
  ![The floating sign](2026-10-05/43_sign_and_horse.png)
- **Nobody escapes a fire.** The storekeeper behind his counter, the porch loafer, both bench men
  and the man at the jail all burnt to death standing at their posts. None of them said anything
  or moved. While the livery burnt next to them, the nearby townsfolk were listed as "calm".
- **The horses don't react to anything.** One stood calmly at the rail through a dynamite blast
  and while the livery burnt down around it.
- **One stick and one lit door burnt the whole town in about eight minutes** (over 5,000 pieces of
  timber), both sides of the street.
- **A dynamite blast "hits" everyone in town in the chest.** The event log said the barkeep
  (inside the saloon, 30 m off), every man on the porches and the dead Kid were all "hit in the
  chest" by a stick at the livery. Mine said "you hit in the chest" and I stayed "Unhurt.".
- **Wrong words for the wound.** Brody, hit in the chest, cried "My leg... damn you...".
- **The surrendered man said "I'm unarmed!" five times in the same instant** (once for each
  buckshot pellet) and "I give up, you bastard!" twice. He was still listed as armed.
- **The man at the range went down with no wounds** after a stray round hit his thigh.
- **A downed man's position is off.** The game put Brody on the porch outside the saloon door,
  but his body lay inside, about a metre and a half away.
- **The revolver goes back in its holster cocked.** After a blackout and after reloading, the
  gun was "put away... hammer cocked".
- **`shoot` with four rounds loaded fired once and clicked once** straight after a reload left
  the hammer at half cock.
- **Grass grows up through the saloon's porch boards.**
  ![Grass on the porch](2026-10-05/27_lyle_corner.png)
- **The backdrop's mountains have a hard vertical edge** at the right of the view east past the
  saloon, with sky beyond it.
  ![The edge of the mountains](2026-10-05/55_shot_surrendered_2.png)
- **Stars show in the sky at golden hour** (17:00–17:30), white dots over the sunset.

## What was flat or confusing

- **The gang wins every fight they see coming.** Every time they knew I was there, they fired
  first and put me down with one or two hits. The Kid, who I'd guess is meant to be the nervous
  one, hit me three out of three at 18 m. The only men I hit were ones who didn't know I was
  there or were stuck. At my speed I couldn't have won a duel. A player with a keyboard might,
  but with no hit to stagger me and blacking out in a second, it feels like there's no room to
  fight back.
- **Downed men stay down forever and silent.** Brody lay "down" for over ten minutes after a
  chest hit, said nothing, and didn't react when I put a gun on him and shouted.
- **Rescuers get stuck.** Brody stood in the saloon's door frame for a minute saying he'd
  drag the Kid out. Lyle stood behind the open door for two minutes shouting "Hold on, I got
  you!" and never moved.
- **The gang leaves after every fight** and doesn't always come back. In the first game, after
  the Kid died and Brody went down, `gang` named Brody and the Kid and nobody came. In the first
  game the gang had forgotten Brody shooting me ("ignore" when they came back); in the second
  they came back already fighting me.
- **Everyone says the same thing at the same moment.** Every shot in town brought eight people
  shouting "Get down! Everybody down!" or "Lord almighty!" in the same tenth of a second, then
  "...They gone?" all together, even when it was me who'd fired and I was standing right there.
- **Townsfolk only cower.** Nobody runs from gunfire, a blast or a fire.
- **Shooting a surrendered man changed nothing** except the barkeep cowering.
- **The day goes very fast.** Golden hour lasts a minute or two of real time and it's full night
  three or four minutes after the start, so most of my fighting was in the dark. I kept setting
  the clock back.
- **Flames look like paper lanterns.** A burning building is covered in small cream teardrops;
  there's little smoke and the sky isn't lit by it.
  ![Flames as teardrops](2026-10-05/50_store_burning_3.png)
- **The fuse is short for setting a stick down.** Lighting, setting it down and walking off took
  me about three seconds and it went off at my feet. (My slowness counts here.)
- **A thrown stick went off 5 m in front of me** at the hitching rail, in the air, instead of at
  the livery 12 m off.
- **Two saloons.** The big painted SALOON front with men on its porch isn't where the gang drink;
  they go into a plainer, unpainted-looking box with SALOON on it, which stands in grass with no
  boardwalk. I couldn't tell which was which.
- **The gang "ride" in on foot.**
- **Some subtitle names are labels**: "Porch Loafer", "Far Man A".
- **Pressing "shout" didn't show as anything I said**, so I never knew whether I'd shouted
  "Drop it!" or called a man out until someone answered.

## What was fun

- **The look.** The street at golden hour, the saloon's bar with its bottles and lamps, the
  livery front: these are beautiful.
  ![The gang riding in](2026-10-05/57_riders_come_in.png)
- **The gang's robbery of the store** was a proper Western scene, unprompted: "You got a problem
  with my money?", "Hurry it up, old man", "Open the drawer. Slow.", the storekeeper's hands up.
- **The gang's talk in a fight**: "Oh God, not the belly...", "You damn fool!", "Easy, I got you.
  Come on!", "Oh God, Kid...", "Damn it. Lost him.", "Come on out!". It sounded like men.
- **The Kid dying slowly of a gut shot** while his friends tried to reach him.
- **A missed shot carrying on down the street** and dropping a man 50 m away.
- **Shooting a man's hat off**: "He shot my hat clean off!"
- **Gun smoke in the saloon** after Lyle fired, with him a ghost in it.
- **A whole street burning down** to its frames from one stick: frightening and spectacular,
  even if too fast.
- The man at the range giving himself up to the shotgun: "Enough! It's yours!"

## The top three things to fix

1. **Blacking out costs nothing.** You wake in four seconds, fully healed, in the same corner of
   the store, sometimes next to the men who shot you. Nothing in a fight matters while this is so.
2. **Fights against the gang are over before you can act.** They draw and hit first, at any range,
   two or three times in a second or two, and one or two hits put you out. Calling a man out in
   the saloon gets you shot rather than a duel. Rescuers who stand stuck in doorways make the
   rest of a fight dead time.
3. **People don't save themselves from fire.** Five townsfolk burnt to death standing at their
   posts without a word, horses stood in the flames, and one stick and one lit door took the
   whole town in eight minutes. The livery's sign and lantern were left hanging in the air.
