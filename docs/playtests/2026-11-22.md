# Playtest 2026-11-22: sleeping in a bed saves the game

**In short:**

- **Sleeping worked, but only with the `sleep bed` shortcut.** It faded to dawn and saved. The message read "You sleep till dawn. Day 2, 06:00. Saved." I woke by the bed in the jail's back room.
- **I could not reach a bed on foot.** In the valley the jail's bed is behind a solid stone wall. In the old town there are no beds at all.
- **Continue brought back the same world.** The dead man was where he fell, with the same blood loss. The clock read day 2, 06:00, the other townsfolk stood where they had, and my gun was still empty. I found nothing missing and nothing new.
- **Sleeping was never refused after shooting.** I slept 7 seconds after shooting a man, and again 3 seconds after firing a shot. Both times it just slept.

## Top problems

1. **No refusal after violence.** I shot the loudmouth in the thigh. He was on the ground bleeding and cowering. Seven seconds later `sleep bed` slept and saved. No "you can't sleep now" appeared on screen or in the events. On a fresh game I fired once and ran `sleep bed` 3 seconds later, and the same thing happened.
2. **The bed can't be reached by walking.** The bed is at the back of the valley jail, behind the front office. The office's back wall is solid stone at every point I checked. I found no door into the back room, and walking round the outside of the jail got stuck at its front corner. In the old town I went through the doctor's, the jail and the saloon floor and got "no bed here" every time. `sleep bed` there said "no bed in the world". I couldn't get up the saloon stairs.
3. **The start card didn't appear with `--continue-card` alone.** With a save present, starting with only `--continue-card` put me in the old town at noon, and the bridge said `card_up: false`. Typing `continue` still loaded the save into the valley. With `--world=valley,--continue-card` the card showed properly ([the card](2026-11-22/n1_start_card_valley.png)). The plain playtest start also opens in the old town, not the valley.
4. **The wounded man died during the sleep, which may be right.** He was hit in the thigh at t=33.9 and still had 4.9 litres of blood at t=41. He "died (blood_loss)" at t=44.3, just as the sleep began. In the morning his body had 2.5 litres. If the night simulated his bleeding out, that's correct. But the game never told me he died in the night.
5. **Nobody reacts to the body in the morning.** There was a dead man on the saloon porch at dawn, and everyone was "calm". The townsman who fled was gone and a new set of people was about. That's probably the day's shift change.

## What broke

- The refusal after shooting never happened, in two tries (problem 1).
- I couldn't reach any bed on foot (problem 2).
- The `--continue-card` start without `--world=valley` gave no card and the wrong world (problem 3).
- The game crashed once in the software graphics driver. Afterwards a yellow line, "The game stalled over 5 s just now (it came back). The report: …", sat on screen in almost every valley shot. On the next start it said "Salt Creek froze last time; the watchdog ended it after 60 s". The stall line covers the subtitles.

## What was flat or confusing

- Nothing told me a man had died while I slept. Waking to "Saved." with a corpse outside and a calm town feels off.
- The clock read 12:00 for 5 minutes of play in the old town and 06:00 after a 20 s wait in the valley. The playtest may stop the clock, but as a player I couldn't tell whether time was passing.
- In the valley, the ground and floors draw as a flat brown or black sheet covering the lower half of the screen. The noon sky is black, and stone walls look blotched purple and blue ([valley start](2026-11-22/v01_start.png)). The old town drew normally on the same machine ([town start](2026-11-22/s01_start.png)). It may be this machine's driver.
- In the old town, `look store_front` from the street's middle pointed at the barber's window.

## What was fun

- The townsfolk's reactions to gunfire were good: "Whoa, now. Easy with that iron." "Get down! Everybody down!" "Please, mister..." "Don't shoot! I got a family!"
- In the old town the gang was running a shakedown on the store ("Nice store. Shame if something happened to it.") while I hunted for a bed.
- Continue is solid. The world came back exactly as I left it, dead man included, and quickly.

## The top three things to fix

1. Refuse sleep after a fight, with the reason on screen. Today you can shoot a man and sleep 7 seconds later.
2. Make the valley jail's bed reachable on foot, or put a reachable bed in the doctor's or the saloon's rooms. Say on screen where beds are.
3. Show the Continue card whenever a save exists, and start the playtest in the valley. Tell the player what happened overnight (who died).
