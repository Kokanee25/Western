# Playtest, town run 3: ten minutes, no mission, no shot (valley, 17:30)

Headless run, so no pictures: I read the game's text read-outs only. Logs are in `build/town/run3/log.txt` (16 snapshots of `read people`, `read folk`, `events 40`) and `build/town/run3/bits.txt` (three `read room / regard / news / fetch` sets).

## What I did

Game clock 17:30 at the start, 17:39 at the end (616 s of game time; container clock about 23:56 to 00:06).

- 17:30. Stood at the middle of Main Street. Read the folk list: seven people "in" (out on the street), the rest "out" (indoors or away).
- 17:31. Tried `walk saloon_porch`: "no way there". Walked to the general store steps instead (46 m).
- 17:31-32. At the store door the screen said "E: bang on the door". The storekeeper was already at the door. He said "Directly. There's others before you." I had no knock command, so I used `shove` to try the door. It hit him instead. I shoved him twice by mistake. He said "Easy! Easy..." and then "Hey—! There's no call for that." He did not move from his post, and he did not leave or fetch anyone.
- 17:32. Tried to walk further into the store. I stayed in the doorway. The room never opened up for me.
- 17:33-34. Walked the street east to the townsman at the bank, then to the barkeep in the saloon. Tried `sit`: "nothing: no free chair by you". Stood in the saloon about a minute (`you_in: true`).
- 17:35-36. Walked to the saloon girl and the loudmouth on the saloon porch. Both walks ended short ("arrived: false", about 2 m away).
- 17:36-38. Walked east to x=100, then to the undertaker at x=117. Waited there until the end.
- Never fired or drew a weapon. I used none of `gang`, `fight`, `spawn`, `ignite`, `dynamite`, `time` or `clock` after the setup.

   - Barkeep: behind the counter in the saloon (`bar_back counter`). I knew from the folk list's post name and from seeing him alone in the saloon's `in` list.
   - Smith: at `smith_door`, "work", in front of the blacksmith's. Post name only.
   - Storekeeper: "stand" at `store_walk`, by the general store door. He spoke to me there. His day-book says "general_store: dinner" while he stood out front.

2. **Anything unexpected?** Yes, and it was a fault more than a surprise. Nobody did anything: not one person moved in ten minutes (see 3). The one surprise was that the storekeeper answered before I did anything: "Directly. There's others before you." No one else was in the store that I could see.

Their positions did not change by even a metre, and every `from_post` read 0.0 (the storekeeper 0.3). 6. **Saloon laugh.** None. `read room` showed only the barkeep in the room, mood "calm", "music": 0.0, and no one else. The only sound is the barkeep's cough. `sit` found no free chair. No laugh, no talk, no band.

## What broke / was flat or confusing / was fun

**Broke**
- `walk saloon_porch`: "no way there". Other porch names did not exist either (`read places` lists no `saloon...` place).
- Walks to the saloon girl and the loudmouth stopped about 2 m short ("arrived: false", "legs": 2). I was on the saloon porch side but could not reach them.
- At the store door I stayed in the doorway (`walked_m` 1.2 and 0.1). I could not get into the store at all.
- There is no `knock` command in the bridge. "E: bang on the door" is shown, but `shove` fell on the storekeeper instead. Nothing opened.
- `read room` keeps showing the single old cough as four separate entries with the same time stamp. `next_bit_in` kept counting (47.6, 38.7, 73.9), but no new bit appeared.

**Flat or confusing**
- The town stood still. All seven people on the street kept the same spot for ten minutes. The roster showed 14 more people "out" indoors or away whose day-books said "work", "home", "away". I never saw any of them.
- It was quiet. One line of unprompted speech in ten minutes, and none between townsfolk.
- The storekeeper's day-book said "dinner" (indoors) but he stood by the door "at store_walk" at 17.5. The roster says he is "out" in the book and "in" at his post. That is confusing.
- No reaction to the shoves beyond two lines. No one else on the street noticed.

**Fun**
- The storekeeper's answer through the door ("Directly. There's others before you.") has a good Western voice. So does his "Easy! Easy...".
- The `read folk` list is a clear window into who is meant to be doing what, and was what I leaned on.

## Top three things to fix

1. **People don't move.** Every man stood on his post for the whole ten minutes (17:30 to 17:39). No walking, no chatting, no change of job. The town reads as a set of statues.
2. **The saloon is dead.** One barkeep, one old cough, no laughs, no music, no free chair. A player who walks in has nothing to see or hear.
3. **I couldn't get indoors or reach people.** The store door never opened, there is no way to knock, and two walks stopped short of the saloon girl and the loudmouth. A shove at a door lands on the man behind it.
