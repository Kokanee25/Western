## 3. The Baron's coach cut (the heist)

On the coach's real route: the South Road is the Baron's own road between his headquarters and town (his town house, the bank, the depot), and this is its loneliest stretch, 0.9 km from the town bridge and 1.0 km from his headquarters, out of sight of both. The road climbs out of the creek bottoms at about 7 % into a cut through a low hogback: the team is slowest on the grade and the cut's 8 m banks won't let the coach swerve. The log deck sits on the south-west bank's lip, chained and wedged; its fall line meets the road at the top of the grade. The rimrock trail leaves the road 40 m on: the way out.
