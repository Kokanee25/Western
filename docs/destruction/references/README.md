# The reference library: real masonry failures

Photos and video of real masonry and concrete failures (Sean's from his concrete work first), to
tune the rules and the look against: how a rubble wall holes, how big the pieces are, where cracks
run, how much dust, how a brick wall comes apart in clumps.

**Where it lives is Sean's call** (the lab brief's open question; `docs/briefs/masonry-lab.md`).
The proposal:

- **Sean's own photos and short clips**: in the private Character Studio repo
  (`Kokanee25/saltcreek-characters`, `references/masonry/`), the way its AAA screenshots are kept
  for the art judge: sessions read them in place, they never enter this public repo, and git
  doesn't carry this repo's history of large videos.
- **Public pictures we only link to** (papers, museum and survey photos): listed below with their
  source and licence, never copied in.
- Anything Sean is happy to make public and that's small (a few hundred KB a picture) may sit in
  this folder.

## What's useful to shoot

- A rubble or block wall holed by a blast or a machine: the hole's outline, what hangs over it.
- A brick wall's pieces on the ground: clump sizes, how many bricks stay together.
- Adobe or mud walls crumbling: the dust, the crumbs.
- Cracks in a loaded wall before it failed: where they ran (along joints, through units).
- Anything with a tape measure or a known object in frame for scale.
