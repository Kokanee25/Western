## The situation

- DECIDED (DESIGN.md §5): "the sheriff is found **gut-shot** on the road. By the wound rules he has
  hours, not minutes. He's conscious, knows who shot him, is frightened and unsure who to trust."
- DECIDED (lock, 2026-11-03): "He was murdered over his family's land and its water rights, and he dies
  of it; Harlan gets his revolver". The widow is his wife.
- OPEN (lock): "who shot him and who ordered it are open".
- PROPOSED (DESIGN.md "Story shape", 2026-10-15): "the ball the doctor digs out may match the
  stranger's gun, or clear him."
- PROPOSED (DESIGN.md §5 cast): "**The doctor** — knows more about the sheriff's wound than he says."

## The ways to do it

- DECIDED (DESIGN.md §5): "Saving him teaches first aid under pressure (the hospital-steward past pays
  off in minute one)."
- DECIDED (`valley.json`): "he can bring him to the doctor", about 480 m.
- PROPOSED (showcase moments): "**The doctor's table**: You carry the sheriff in; the doctor probes for
  the ball while you hold the lamp; the ball matches a gun you've seen". "**The gut shot who talks**:
  Dying slowly: get him to the doctor, or let him say where the money is first".
- PROPOSED (DESIGN.md §5): "He might tell you, pin his badge on you".
- DECIDED (Sean, 2026-11-09): "you haven't been appointed to anything yet the first night - it's not

- DECIDED (Sean, 2026-11-09, answering the No Justice review): by the time of No Justice Harlan has

- DECIDED (lock): help or leave him is a deed the widow's lines follow ("the help-or-leave deed from
  the opening").

  him (the widow remembers).

## Where the sources disagree

- DECIDED (Sean, 2026-11-09, picked for `no-law`): his last words on day 2 depend on how Harlan treated
  him on day 1: well cared for, he's clear (his family, the town, maybe who shot him and why); badly
  cared for, confused and fading, a name or half a sentence. He deputizes Harlan either way.

- DECIDED (Sean, 2026-11-09): "Yes you are deputized": on day 2 the dying sheriff swears Harlan in as
  his deputy and gives him his revolver; a shake of the head gets "that's not the way this works" and
  the revolver all the same (`no-law`).
- OPEN (Sean, 2026-11-09): "Yeah I'm not sold on the whole evidence and trial stuff yet - that's a maybe". The ball, the forensics and the trial in DESIGN.md's spine
  (signature features 1–3) are a maybe.
- OPEN: **the ball as evidence.** DESIGN.md's spine (signature 2, decided 2026-10-01): "the ball in him
  is the evidence"; DESIGN.md "Story shape": "the ball the doctor digs out may match the stranger's
  gun". Sean, 2026-11-09, on No Law's saloon: "we aren't pulling bullets out to examine that idea isn't
  happening". Only the saloon's ball, or no ball examined anywhere?

- OPEN: whether he can live. DESIGN.md (2026-10-01 spine): "how well you treat him decides whether he
  lives"; §5: "be saved by the doctor". Lock (Sean, 2026-11-03, PR #621, not merged): "**He dies
  whatever you do**: you can get him to the doctor, but he doesn't survive".
- OPEN: dawn ride "into town" (DESIGN.md) / Harlan's dawn ride from his own homestead by the ford
  (valley-plan).
