# Joe on the Rope (`joe-on-the-rope`)

Other names: "the Joe hanging", "the rope shot" (#734, #738). Sources: #546 comments (Sean,
2026-11-04); #733 (Sean, 2026-11-09); #734 (2026-11-09); #738 and comments (2026-11-09);
`docs/briefs/valley-plan.md` §10.

- DECIDED (Sean, 2026-11-04, #546): "**If Harlan can't save Joe on the rope, Joe dies.** It's a real
  immersive-sim moment: no rescue by someone else, no fail-and-retry built in".
- DECIDED (Sean, 2026-11-04, #546): "Joe on the rope happens after Harlan has been given the rifle."

## How you hear of it

- PROPOSED (#546, 2026-11-04): "whoever warns Harlan says where they're taking Joe, and the route
  brings him to the overlook as the mob gathers. A nudge, not a marker."
- DECIDED (Sean, 2026-11-04, #546 "yes to the rifle plan"): "The overlook is suggested by the route,
  not a marker."
- OPEN: who warns him.

## The situation

- DECIDED (Sean, 2026-11-04, #546): "men decide to **hang Joe**, because he's been helping Harlan.
  Harlan has to **shoot the rope** while Joe hangs to save him."
- PROPOSED (#546, 2026-11-04), a reason tied to No Justice: "if Harlan shook his head and the killer
  was released, **that man leads the lynching**; if Harlan nodded and Joe killed him, **the killer's
  friends** come for Joe. Either way it's the consequence of that choice, and both branches meet in
  one scene."
- PROPOSED (#738 comment, 2026-11-09): "the bandits' kin or the town's anger at how that mission
  ended".
- PROPOSED (#546): "**Handle it seriously,** as No Justice is: a lynching of a Ute man is real
  history and racism, not spectacle. It's about Harlan's loyalty and the town showing what it is."

## The ways to do it

- DECIDED (Sean, 2026-11-04, #546): from the overlook, "About 100–150 m", Harlan shoots the rope.
- DECIDED (Sean, 2026-11-04, #546): "The other ways stay open but slower."
- PROPOSED (#546): the other ways: "shoot the man holding the rope's end, ride under Joe and lift
  him, cut it with a knife, or scatter the mob."
- PROPOSED (#546): "Joe's own physiology sets the clock: a short-drop strangulation, roughly tens of
  seconds until he's unconscious and minutes until he dies, so there's a real but short window."
- PROPOSED (#546): "The window must be fair: readable, achievable and the same every time for the
  same actions."
- PROPOSED (#546): "Afterwards, Joe is down and hurt and has to be helped and cut loose. The mob's
  men remember who did what."

## The big choice and its cost

- DECIDED: save him or lose him for good (above). The choice is in the doing: shoot the rope, shoot a
  man, ride in, or hesitate.
- OPEN: no source names a choice beyond saving him (e.g. whether killing mob members has a cost the
  player weighs against the clock). The lynch mob's men are townsfolk (PROPOSED reading of
  "the town showing what it is").

- PROPOSED (#546, 2026-11-04): "This is the one exception to 'Joe can't die in routine combat'."

  Harlan alone or someone else. Each Joe-dependent thread needs a without-Joe path."

- PROPOSED (#738, 2026-11-09): if he lives, "he's the man with plans" (see `MAP.md`, Joe).
- PROPOSED (#738 rule 2): "**no idea depends on him alone.**"
