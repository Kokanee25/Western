## 2. The middle: the missing reason, and the smallest fixes

What's missing is a reason: nothing explains why one man's plan produces the sheriff's murder, a gang, a
train robbery and soldiers on a train. #466 already holds the spine (the Johnson County War, a bought
officer "to make it look lawful"): **the Baron wins if Salt Creek looks lawless enough that soldiers are
sent to restore order; Rourke is the officer he pays to bring them.** Every crime becomes part of that
case, and Harlan's badge is what stands in its way. Three small changes carry it, with cards that exist:

1. **The red shirt is the Baron's hired gun, and the man who shot the sheriff.** "No law to save him
   now" becomes a boast; a well-tended sheriff's clear last words can name him, or name the water.
   Nothing in No Justice changes. In the arrest ending, the man on the platform who lets him go is
   Rourke or his officer: Rourke's first sighting, in Act I.
2. **The Baron at the sheriff's funeral**, quietly offering to buy the widow's claim (#546 P3, "met
   early"). He gets a face and a motive in Act I; the farmhouse reads as his answer to her refusal; and
   it settles #622 item 7 one way (the Colters on the Baron's pay).
3. **Payday Night is the hinge.** Keep only that part of `payday-inquest`. Gang B was paid through the
   Baron's foreman; the payroll's loss is the outrage that brings Rourke in on the train that payday,
   offering "order". The Shipment's ledgers show both payments. The Kid (`kid-wants-out`) is the
   informant who knows who pays the Colters, which carries it without an evidence system.

## 4. Contradictions Sean must settle

1. **Evidence is "a maybe", but Act IV runs on papers.** Without an evidence system, what are the papers
   for? Options: turning Rourke's soldiers (#546 P4), the newspaper, or a US Marshal.
2. **Where the game ends:** the inventory ends at the trestle ("must destroy the bridge"); #546
   (DECIDED 2026-11-04) puts the raid on the Baron's house after it; P4 offers turning the soldiers.
3. **Who works for whom:** who shot the sheriff, the red shirt's gang vs the Colters, who holds the
   farm, who runs Gangs A and B, who released the prisoner. Until settled, the middle can't be written.
4. **The Balcony Ambush can't be in Act I** as the showcase and the Mentor describe it (dynamite only
   from Buried Alive). Move it after the Mentor, or settle how posts come down without dynamite.
5. **Joe can die on the rope, but Act IV is written with him** (the Shipment is "Harlan and Joe plan").
   Each needs a path without Joe (#738 rule 2), or Sean narrows the rope's permadeath.
6. **The saloon's shooter and Joe's first meeting:** "a bold cowboy" (inventory) vs the red shirt (No
   Law); the bold cowboy also at the doctor's in No Justice; where Harlan meets Joe is flagged.
