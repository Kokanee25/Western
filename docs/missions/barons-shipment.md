## The ways to do it

- DECIDED (Sean, 2026-11-09, #736): "The player releases a big log from above. It rolls or swings
  down, knocks the iron-plated wagon over, and **dents its plates**."
- PROPOSED (#736 comment, session's spec): release it "by shooting the chain, knocking out the
  wedge, or using dynamite"; "Its line downhill must meet the road where the wagon is slowest: a
  bend, a grade, or a narrow cut where it can't swerve. This way the timing is the player's skill and
  not luck."
- PROPOSED (#736): "**A second way to do it** for the immersive sim: block the road, spook the team,
  or take it at the bend another way. The log is the showpiece, not the only way."
- PROPOSED (#546, 2026-11-04): "**The trap is suggested, not built by the player** (Sean: keep it
  simple)." "A short time skip or fade ... shows it **already rigged**: a huge timber hung by rope from
  a tree over the road." "The player does the moment: cut or shoot the rope as the coach passes."
  "Then the mission's hold-up or impersonation continues."
- PROPOSED (#736): "The documents sit in a strongbox inside, so the player has to open it (key off a
  guard, shoot the lock, or pry it) before the Baron's men arrive."
- PROPOSED (#736): "it's a heist, the guards fight back": "an outcrop opposite, cover at road level,
  and a way out (the rough rimrock trail, #736 A.2, suits it)."
