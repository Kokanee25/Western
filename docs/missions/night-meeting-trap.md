# The Night Meeting That's a Trap (`night-meeting-trap`)

Sources: `docs/briefs/showcase-moments.md` "Sean's favourites" (Sean, 2026-10-21).

- PROPOSED (showcase, Sean 2026-10-21): "**Fits:** a mission once time windows and messages exist
  (Phase 5)."

## How you hear of it

- PROPOSED (showcase): "'Be at the feed store between midnight and three.'" (a message).

## The situation

- PROPOSED (showcase): "You go in; it's an ambush. A sharpshooter across the street fires through the
  window, or someone throws lamp oil at the front and it goes up. The front is burning and there's no
  way out, except the dynamite in the back room: blow a hole in the back wall and get out."

## The ways to do it

- PROPOSED (showcase): "**Make 'no way out' true, not scripted:** a building chosen for it (a store room
  with barred windows and no back door, as the bank's `window_bars` already are), the front burning from
  real fire spread. Other ways out stay possible if the player finds them (an axe through the boards,
  shooting out a weak wall, the roof), and that's fine."
- OPEN: not going (or going early and watching) is not discussed.

## The big choice and its cost

- PROPOSED (showcase): "the fire reaching the dynamite before you light it, a blast in a small room
  hurting you (overpressure and the player's own wounds: find cover inside first), the sharpshooter
  waiting at the hole you make."
