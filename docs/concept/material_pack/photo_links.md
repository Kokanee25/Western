# Optional real-world construction photographs — physical structure only

These are real-world photo links from Wikimedia Commons. The images are NOT included in this ZIP because the source image files could not be reliably downloaded in this session. If Claude has internet access, have it open the pages and their original images; otherwise use them as a checklist for reference collection.

These photos establish real wood construction/grain, **not** Salt Creek art style or palette. The APPROVED style reference remains `01_APPROVED_STYLE/approved_saloon_exterior.png`.

1. **Exposed end face and side wood** (Pau Ferro lumber board): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pau_Ferro_Lumber.jpg — author Sondich, CC BY-SA 4.0. Note end-vs-long grain, exposed thickness; species is not a proposed frontier-building material.
2. **Weathered end grain** (wooden post close-up): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wooden_post.jpg — Elsa L Roberts, CC BY-SA 3.0. Useful for growth rings/checking, but more heavily weathered than most saloon boards.
3. **Boardwalk boards** (photo): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Boardwalk_Texture_-_HDR_(7688454132).jpg — Nicolas Raymond, CC BY 2.0. Useful for joint/seam and long-grain logic; HDR look is NOT the lighting target.
4. **Real rough-sawn timber frame**: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Beautiful_Post_and_Beam_Horse_Barn.JPG — Vermont Timber Works, see Commons page for licensing. Useful for posts, braces, joints and beam mass.
5. **Rough-sawn plank surface**: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kirschholzbrett_--_2021_--_7672.jpg — different species, physical saw texture only.

When integrating externally sourced material into a distributed game, verify the image's exact licence/attribution requirements on its source page; these are design research references only and should not be pasted into production textures.
