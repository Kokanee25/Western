# Salt Creek: the art judge's rubric

The yardstick is `10_authority_saloon_man.jpg` (it wins when references disagree), with the style
pictures beside it (`05`, `08`, `09`, `11`, `12`, `13`). See README.md for what each picture is for.

## The look

Realistic forms and proportions, simplified into broad, angular patches of painted colour that
follow the form: the grain of a board, the folds of cloth, the curve of a mug. A bit of square to
the paint is part of the look (the man's coat and the tin mugs in the authority). Warm key light
(lamps, low sun) against cool fill (blue night, sky), deep shadows you can still read into, a
little haze. Weathered, substantial timber; worn, used, dirty things.

**Wrong:** photographic texture or film grain; lens blur; smooth plastic surfaces with no paint
in them; watercolour washes and smears; squares that are random noise rather than following a
form (camouflage mottle), or coarse squares beside smooth work; cartoon proportions or outlines;
screen filters standing in for paint.

## The traits (each PASS or FAIL, with the region that decides it)

1. **Shapes:** forms and proportions credible, simplified into large readable masses; strong
   silhouettes; timber that looks thick enough to hold a building up.
2. **Painted surface:** angular painted patches that follow the form, a bit of square, no
   photographic texture, no noise.
3. **Light:** warm key and cool fill, readable darks, the light coming from somewhere you can name.
4. **People** (if any): faces modelled in paint like the authority's man (planes of the face,
   warm skin, painted moustache and hair), clothes painted in folds, figures lit, not silhouettes.
5. **One hand:** everything in the frame (people, props, buildings, ground, sky) looks painted by
   the same artist.

A trait that doesn't apply (no people in the frame) is N/A, not a pass.

## The verdict

PASS only if every trait that applies passes. Then the **three biggest problems**, biggest first,
each with: the region (a box, `x y w h` in the picture's pixels), what's wrong against the
authority, the likely cause (**paint**: the texture; **shape**: the model; **light**: the
lighting), and one fix to try. No score out of 100: the problems are the useful part.
