# The judge's reference set (Sean, 2026-10-18)

Sean's pictures for the art judge and the blind critic. The number is the order he sent them in;
the name says what each is for.

- **Authority** (wins when the others disagree): `10_authority_saloon_man.jpg`, the man at the card
  table in the saloon at night. The look: painted, realistic forms simplified into angular patches
  of colour that follow the form, with a bit of square to the paint (his coat, the mugs), warm lamp
  light and cool blue night outside.
- **Style** (the same hand): `05_style_table_lantern.jpg` (the boards' and furniture's paint up
  close), `08_style_street_a.jpg`, `09_style_street_b.jpg` (the street at sunset, first person),
  `11_style_saloon_bar.jpg`, `12_style_saloon_overhead.jpg`, `13_style_saloon_door.jpg` (the
  saloon at night: chandelier, turned balusters, rugs, stools, mirror bar, lamps everywhere).
- **Content only, not the look** (photoreal): `01_content_fire_dusk.jpg`, `02_content_fire_day.jpg`
  (how a building burns and falls in), `03_content_dynamite.jpg` (the blast's dust and clods),
  `04_content_floor_close.jpg` (worn boards and chairs close up). Repainted in the authority's hand
  as `made_*.jpg` (`tools/textures/references.json`, the `judge_*` entries; Nano Banana,
  `google/gemini-2.5-flash-image`).
- **Composition and mood** (painted, smoother than the authority): `06_comp_river.jpg`,
  `07_comp_triptych.jpg`.
