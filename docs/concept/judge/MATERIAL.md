# Salt Creek: the materials judge's rubric

Sean, 2026-11-10 (#701/#721: "we're grading the material, not the scene"). The cheap judge
(`CHEAP.md`) asks about a whole scene; on the material lab's sample panels its scene tells (empty,
edges of the world, too straight, atmosphere) fail whatever the wood is. This judge sees **only
crops of the material** and asks one question per swatch: **does this read as a real material of
this kind and age, made with care, in Red Dead's direction?**

It is a fresh, blind sub-agent. It sees the crops (named by swatch code and age, never by scan)
and, as a yardstick, official screenshots of shipped AAA games read where they are in the private
Character Studio (never copied into this repo, not even as a crop). It does not judge the scene,
the sky, the ground, the people or the light's colour (that's the light lane's), only the
material as the light shows it.

## What it checks (each PASS / FAIL, with a crop box as evidence)

1. **Real stuff:** the grain, figure and colour read as this kind of wood (pine, fir, walnut...),
   not a stock texture, laminate, plastic or a painting of wood.
2. **Age reads:** new / a few years / old are clearly different and each is believable for the
   desert (sun-greying, checking, splits, dirt splash, wear where hands and feet go).
3. **No tile tells:** no repeat, no seam, no square blocks, dots, weave or mush at 1 m.
4. **Board to board:** boards differ the way real ones do (tone, figure, width, set), without
   reading as random noise or stripes.
5. **Edges and joints:** joints, laps and edges read as shadowed gaps and worn arrises, not
   lines drawn on (bright or coloured outlines, dashes).
6. **Holds at distance:** at 5 m it keeps its character without shimmer, mush or a flat card.

## The grade (one per swatch and age)

| grade | what it means |
|---|---|
| **fake** | Reads as a texture: tells everywhere. |
| **game** | Reads as wood, but a game's: one or two clear tells. |
| **near the yardstick** | Reads as real wood; tells only if you hunt. |
| **at the yardstick** | As convincing as the AAA references' wood. |

Then, per swatch: the one change that would move it up most, and which of its ages is best.
