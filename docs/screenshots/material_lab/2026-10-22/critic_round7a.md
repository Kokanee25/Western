## Its five biggest differences

1. **Grain and stains as flat cut-out decals**: dark dashes, blobs and round knot "eyes" stamped
   on with no relation to the light, where the men's tone shapes follow their forms (01, 08, 14,
   10, 12, 15, 06, 11, 03, 13).
2. **The wood redder and more saturated than the men** at golden hour: orange-pink or salmon
   against their cool greys, whites and muted browns (14, 02, 09, 06, 11, 03, 13, 08).
3. **Edges too crisp and too even**: every seam a hard line of one weight, a bright bevel on every
   plank and tread, against the men's lost-and-found edges (03, 13, 05, 16, 04, 07, 12, 15).
4. **Cast light hard and aliased**: two-tone posterised leaf dapples, stair-stepped shadow edges
   (the sun's shadow map) (02, 09, 05, 16, 01, 08, 14).
5. **Surfaces don't match**: smooth CG gradients on the steps (03, 13) or near-empty pale cream on
   posts and siding (10, 11, 06), where the men's clothes carry painted mottle inside every tone
   shape.
