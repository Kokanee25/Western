## Its five biggest differences

1. **How light falls**: on the men in a few hard-edged flat values (sleeves, faces, vests); on the
   wood in smooth gradients (01, 03, 15, 09, 10, the post in 08). The banded light (round 6) barely
   changes a flat sunlit wall: what reads as smooth is the boards' own painted tone and the soft
   stains, which is what round 6b posterises.
2. **Grain scale and kind**: fine dense streak grain and sharp knots against the men's broad mottles
   (07, 04, 05, 16, 02).
3. **Colour**: either cool bleached grey-cream (04, 05, 12, 16) or hot orange blowing out (07, 03),
   neither the men's muted warm middle.
4. **Seams and edges**: hairline black seams and crisp machined bevels (02, 06, 07, 11, 14): the
   geometry's, not the texture's.
5. **Value pattern and shadows**: siding boards alternating light and dark in a regular stripe (11,
   14, 03); soft blurred leaf-like stain shadows against the men's hard stair-stepped cast shadows.
