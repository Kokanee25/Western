## The five biggest differences

1. **Pale, striped wood instead of one dark umber stain.** Boards alternate bleached grey or tan with dark, so walls read as weathered barn siding. In the reference every board is the same deep warm brown, and only the lamps lighten it. Most visible in 03, 11, 13, 15, 10, 04, 01 and 14.
2. **Photographic grain and raw knotty pine.** The beams, joists and boards carry sharp photo grain, knots and bright yellow new-lumber colour. That is the main thing that separates the wood from the smoothly painted men; the reference's wood is broad, soft, painted strokes. Most visible in 05, 09, 04, 10 and 13.
3. **No joinery: everything is a plain plank.** Walls are bare vertical boards with no framed or raised panelling. Posts are square sticks with no chamfers, caps or bases. There are no corbels or brackets under the gallery or beams. The bar top is a thin slab with no thick moulded nosing, and the bar front has no framed panels. Most visible in 01, 06, 14, 02, 04, 08 and 13.
4. **The balustrade is flat-sawn and crowded.** The balusters are flat cut-outs packed nearly shut, under a plain board handrail. The reference has round turned balusters with light between them, under a heavy moulded rail. Most visible in 05, 07 and 09; 17 and 12 come closest, with the vase shape and the ball newel.
5. **Cold floors and glare instead of warm lamplit highlights.** Floors are pale blonde or cold grey-blue, and in 18 and 12 they have glowing seam lines; the reference's floor is warm worn umber planks with pools of lamplight. The lamps blow out to white-orange glare across the stripes instead of picking out warm highlights on moulded edges (there are no edges for them to catch). Most visible in 18, 12, 13, 15, 04 and 10.

## Best and worst

- **Best:** 16 (warm stain, back-bar pediment, arch), 06 (one dark umber palette with the man), 08 (warm lamp pool on umber boards), 17 (turned-looking balusters and ball newel in umber). 02 and 07 come next.
- **Worst:** 13 and 15 (the whole room pale grey-lilac and blonde, the men pasted in), 09 and 05 (raw yellow knotty pine), 03 and 11 (bleached striped siding), 10 (glare over photo-grain stripes).
