## The five biggest differences

1. **What the detail is made of.** The men are broad soft areas of flat colour; the wood is fine
   continuous grain lines, crisp drawn knots and figure (03, 07, 13, 17, 18).
2. **Lightness.** Much of the wood bleached toward white with blown highlights; the men saturated
   mid-to-dark (01, 05, 06, 09, 14's fascia).
3. **Colour and stripes.** Plank-to-plank colour changes (orange, cream, pink, grey) make candy-stripe
   or patchwork walls; the men keep one muddy palette of browns (04, 16, 18, 03/17).
4. **Wear and dirt.** The men's grime is big soft stains; the wood is either spotless and varnished
   or "dirty" with specks and red spatter that read as noise (clean 13, 06; specks 05, 14, 20).
5. **Edges and resolution.** Hard stair-stepped shadow edges on the wood and blurry square pixels on
   some surfaces (09, 05; shadow staircases 01, 03, 04, 06, 11).

Best: the warm mid-tone softly grained walls and decks (11, 12, 20, then 02/10, 21). Worst: the
saturated striped walls (03, 04, 16, 17, 18), the varnished steps (13), the pixelated risers (09).
