# Blind critic: the bar and back bar against target 10 (2026-10-21)

## 1. The five biggest differences

1. **Colour: the woodwork is dark red cherry, the painting's is warm mid brown.** bar.jpg and bar_top.jpg, the whole bar; the painting's bar (right third, ref_back_bar.jpg) is orange-tan to umber, the same family as its walls. Fix: hue toward orange-umber, raise value two steps, less red.
2. **The arched mirrors read as black holes.** back_bar.jpg and bar.jpg, the two arches; the painting's mirror (upper right) is the brightest thing on the back wall, glowing with the lamps and a lit frame. Fix: a reflective, light-catching mirror pane with a bright edge; the bare shelves make the void worse.
3. **The back bar is slight and sunk into the wall.** interior.jpg and back_bar.jpg, columns and cornice; the painting's back bar has heavy square pilasters with capitals and a deep, multi-step cornice running up to the gallery. Fix: thicker pilasters, a deeper stepped cornice, bring it forward from the wall; drop or flatten the curved crest, which the painting doesn't have.
4. **The bar front's panels vanish.** bar.jpg, the front below the top; the painting's front has a frieze band of short panels under the top, then tall raised panels between stiles and a stepped plinth, each edge lit. Fix: deeper recesses, a lit top edge on every rail and stile, add the frieze band.
5. **Finish: smooth lacquer and fine photographic grain, not chunky painted squares.** bar_top.jpg, the long glossy streak and fine grain; the painting's bar top is matte, mottled in big blocks of tone with a warm lit nosing. Fix: cut the specular sheen, coarser blocky grain, keep the rings faint.

## 2. Verdicts

- **Bar front: DIFFERENT.** Right construction (panels, plinth, foot rail) but too dark and red for the panels to read; no frieze band.
- **Bar top: CLOSE.** The thick rounded overhang and plank seams match; the cherry gloss and red hue don't.
- **Back bar: DIFFERENT.** Arches, shelves and cornice are present, but it's thin, dark, mirrorless and reads as a hole, not the painting's lit, massive centrepiece.

## 3. What already works

The bar's proportions (height, thick overhanging top with a rounded nosing, panelled front on a plinth) and the back bar's layout of two arched bays between columns under a cornice are right in kind.
