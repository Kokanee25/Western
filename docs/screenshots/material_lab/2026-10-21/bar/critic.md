# Blind critic: the bar and back bar against the saloon painting (target 10)

## The five biggest differences

1. **The woodwork is far darker than the room; the painting's is as bright.** Render: bar.jpg and back_bar.jpg, the bar front and the back bar's base and columns sit near black-umber against glowing orange plank walls. Painting: the bar front and back bar (right third; ref_back_bar.jpg) are lit honey-brown, the same value as the walls. Fix: lift the stain's value or the lamps' reach onto the fixtures.
2. **The arched mirrors read as flat ochre plaster.** Render: back_bar.jpg and bar.jpg, both arches a dull, even olive-yellow fill. Painting: the back-bar mirror holds a bright, busy reflection of lamps and room in a gilt-lit frame. Fix: a reflective or painted-reflection mirror, brighter than its frame.
3. **The bar front's panels don't read.** Render: bar.jpg lower half (and bar_top.jpg bottom), a dark slab with faint vertical lines. Painting: a clear rhythm of framed raised panels between posts, over a plinth. Fix: lighter rails and stiles, a highlight on each panel's moulding.
4. **The finish is smooth photographic grain, not the painting's chunky squares.** Render: bar_top.jpg, fine streaky grain and soft gloss on the top. Painting: the bar top and front are a mottled mosaic of large warm squares. Fix: coarser texel size and painted mottle on the fixtures' woods.
5. **The back bar is thin and plain.** Render: back_bar.jpg, slim columns, two narrow shelves on small brackets, a shallow cornice band. Painting: heavy turned/carved columns, deep shelving and a tall layered cornice crowning the whole wall. Bare shelves make it worse: it reads as an empty wall cabinet. Fix: thicker columns and cornice, deeper shelf boards.

## Verdicts

- **Bar front: DIFFERENT.** Near black; the panelling the painting shows clearly is lost.
- **Bar top: CLOSE.** Right thick slab, overhang and lit rounded front edge, but too red-purple (cherry, not honey-brown) and too smooth.
- **Back bar: DIFFERENT.** Right layout, but dark, thin, with dead mirrors.

## What already works

The layout is right: two arched bays between columns under a cornice, a counter with a deep overhanging top in front, and the drink rings and worn edge on the top look believable.
