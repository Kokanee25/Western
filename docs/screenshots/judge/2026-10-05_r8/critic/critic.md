1. **The sky is pale lavender confetti, not a golden sunset** (street). A viewer reads the painting as sunset and the render as an overcast midday with a lens flare. Crop: `critic/1_sky.png`

Together with the orange-tinted right side it makes the whole render read as one orange colour where the painting's road is lighter and cooler than its buildings. Crop: `critic/5_road.png`
