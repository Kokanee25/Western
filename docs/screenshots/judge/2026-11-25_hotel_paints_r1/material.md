# Materials judge, hotel paints r1 (P7, P8, P9)

Grades are without the AAA yardstick (its directory was empty this round); they rest on my knowledge of painted clapboard and trim in RDR2, Hunt 1896, KCD2 and in life.
Known light fault (saw-toothed shadow edge along the laps, seen in P7 new/few near): noted once, not counted.
Crop boxes are in each crop's own pixels (near 1400x780, far 560x360).

- Best age: few (even film, believable chips). Change that moves it up most: keep the blue hue at 5 m (the far view loses all the colour) and rebuild chips as film edges at the lap foot rather than brown dabs.
