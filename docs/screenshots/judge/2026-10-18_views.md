   door views, man_saloon_night, inside_store_afternoon, town_bar, gun_smoke_saloon (a beige fog

   dynamite_store_blast, smoke_drift_street are bleached, with no warm or cool. The authority:
   warm pools from lamps, cool fill, deep but readable darks. *Art.*

3. **The player's hand in toy blocks** (9 of the first-person views). *Owner to agree:* how skin

   road_low_golden, road_low_noon, man_store_golden, dynamite_store_blast, texel_store_golden;

7. **Signs and windows** (5): coarse pixel lettering (SALOON, GENERAL STORE, MEALS), windows a few
