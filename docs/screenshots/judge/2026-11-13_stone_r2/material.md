# Materials judge: 2026-11-13_stone_r2 (stone and lime)

Blind judgment against the rubric (`docs/concept/judge/MATERIAL.md`, "Stone and lime"). Yardstick:
the AAA screenshots; the only stone and lime render in them that can be read closely is the gate tower
at the right of KCD2's street. Its ashlar has crisp, knocked arrises and joints that the sun shadows,
and the render above it is a soft coat with a sand texture, rain staining and flaked patches. The
Hunt and RDR2 shots show their surfaces' standard: grime and wear that follow use, with no drawn lines.

Crop boxes are `x y w h` in the named crop's pixels. Near crops are 1400x780, far crops 560x360.
I did not see the saw-toothed sun-shadow edge in any crop.

---

## S1: Coursed rubble (hammer-squared stone, thick lime joints, dressed quoins and coping)

The stone layout is the same in all three ages (the same wall aged), which is correct.

### S1_new

| # | Check | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Real stuff | FAIL | Stone faces carry a fibrous, hay-like grain, not a stone's grain or tooling (`S1_new_near` 345 300 300 190). The joints are flat, smooth grey bands with a raised lip round each stone, like grout round stone cladding, not thick lime struck back (`S1_new_near` 640 265 340 45). |
| 2 | Age reads | FAIL | New pointing should be pale and fresh. Here it is a mid grey, darker than several stones (`S1_new_near` 940 255 60 250). See the set note below. |
| 3 | No tile tells | FAIL | Nearly every stone has the same pinched diagonal crease running in from one corner, a "pillow with a pinched corner" form that repeats (`S1_new_near` 965 85 205 180, 1190 60 175 190, 670 290 240 190, 1130 515 170 150). Courses are level enough for coursed work. |
| 4 | Stone to stone | PASS | Size, length and tone vary believably (a red-brown stone at 345 305, a dark one at 1130 515, tans and greys), and it doesn't read as noise. |
| 5 | Edges and joints | FAIL | The bottom and right edge of every stone has a near-black line of even width, drawn rather than cast. The bed joints have dashed black lines (`S1_new_near` 660 470 300 30, 330 255 620 25). The faces swell like loaves, with no knocked arrises. |
| 6 | Holds at distance | FAIL | At 5 m, golden hour, the stones and mortar merge into one orange-tan. The stones read as rounded pebbles with thin outlines. The quoins are flat painted rectangles with no relief, draft or arris, butted with no mortar (`S1_new_far` 0 0 125 360). |

**Grade: game.** At 1 m it reads as a game's stone wall. The tells are the outlines, the loaves, the repeated crease and flat quoins.

### S1_few

### S1_old

| # | Check | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Real stuff | FAIL | Hay-like face grain. The mortar is a deep bump noise like stucco or popcorn texture, not eroded lime (`S1_old_near` 300 270 400 60). |
| 2 | Age reads | FAIL | Old differs from few mostly by a darker overall value and a rougher noise. There is no dark crust on the stones, and the joints aren't eroded back behind the arrises. None of the rubric's foot or coping tells (rising damp, streaks under the coping) appear in either crop (`S1_old_near` vs `S1_few_near` 300 270 400 60). |
| 3 | No tile tells | FAIL | The same repeated corner crease (`S1_old_near` 965 85 205 180, 670 290 240 190). |
| 4 | Stone to stone | PASS | The variation holds. |
| 5 | Edges and joints | FAIL | The heaviest outlines of the set: thick black lines on the bottom and right edges, often detached from the stone and running into the mortar, plus cream top lines (`S1_old_near` 670 420 240 70, 940 420 220 80, 1130 540 175 130). |
| 6 | Holds at distance | FAIL | At 5 m every stone is ringed in black, like a cartoon or a painting of stone. The quoins are flat (`S1_old_far` 140 20 420 330, 0 0 125 360). |

**Grade: fake.**

**S1 set: one change that would move it up most:** remove the drawn edge lines, dark and light, and make the joints real recesses (the mortar set back from the stone faces, with irregular, chipped arrises rather than pillow-rounded faces), so the joint shadows come from the sun. Give the quoins the same treatment, with a drafted margin and a raised face.
**Best age: new.**

## PL1: Lime plaster on stone, whitewashed (inside)

### PL1_few

| # | Check | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Real stuff | FAIL | A flat colour with soft cloud-noise blotches. It has no lime body: no trowel undulation, no sand grain and no brush marks of a wash. It reads as painted drywall or a sky texture (`PL1_few_near` 350 320 600 230). |
| 2 | Age reads | FAIL | "A few years" is shown only as grey clouds. There is no thinning of the wash at the arrises, no grimy band where men lean and no scuffs low. |
| 3 | No tile tells | FAIL | Fine horizontal streaks across the whole face, like brushed metal, with rows of dashed dark specks (`PL1_few_near` 0 30 400 30, 300 480 470 20, 950 255 300 20). |
| 4 | Variation (coat) | FAIL | The variation is random cloud noise and doesn't follow damp, hands or the stones beneath (`PL1_few_near` 350 320 600 230). |
| 5 | Edges and joints | FAIL | Dashed lines drawn across the plaster (`PL1_few_near` 300 480 470 20). No arris or corner is shown. |
| 6 | Holds at distance | FAIL | At 5 m it is a flat card with sepia cloud stains (`PL1_few_far` 0 0 560 360). |

Lamplight (`PL1_few_saloon_night`) shows the same: a flat orange card with darker clouds, and the horizontal streaks are clearer (0 30 700 80).

**Grade: fake.**

### PL1_old

| # | Check | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Real stuff | FAIL | The same cloud-noise card, with flakes cut out of it (`PL1_old_near` 350 320 600 230). |
| 2 | Age reads | FAIL | The only change from few is the flaked islands. Where the wash is gone, the hole shows the same tone as the coat, with no plaster, older wash or stone beneath (`PL1_old_near` 860 280 340 180, 585 140 90 150). There is still no grime band and no scuffs. |
| 3 | No tile tells | FAIL | The horizontal brushed streaks and specks remain (`PL1_old_near` 0 30 600 30). The scattered small flakes are of one size family and are spread evenly, like a stamp (`PL1_old_near` 880 530 420 100). |
| 4 | Variation (coat) | FAIL | Cloud noise, as in few. |
| 5 | Edges and joints | FAIL | The flake edges are the best thing in PL1: a thin shadow on one side reads as a lifted film's edge (`PL1_old_near` 1020 280 175 70). But they cut into a coat with no thickness, and the dashed lines are still drawn across the plaster (`PL1_old_near` 300 480 470 20). |
| 6 | Holds at distance | FAIL | At 5 m the flakes become pale specks on a cloudy card (`PL1_old_far` 160 0 240 120). |

**Grade: fake.**

**PL1: one change that would move it up most:** give the coat a lime plaster's body: a gentle trowel undulation and fine sand grain in its relief, and the brush strokes of the wash. Take out the cloud blotches and the horizontal streaks. Then age it by use (a grimy band at shoulder height, scuffs low, flakes showing buff plaster beneath) rather than by noise.
**Best age: few** (fewer tells, but still fake).

## PL2: Bare rubble, whitewashed (inside)

The stones are laid out exactly as S1's.

### PL2_few

| # | Check | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Real stuff | FAIL | It reads as embossed wallpaper or a painting of stone. The wash doesn't pool in the joints or thin over the stone, and the stones are soft pillow reliefs (`PL2_few_near` 680 280 240 210). |
| 2 | Age reads | FAIL | See old: the two ages differ only in warmth and in the roughness of the mortar. |
| 3 | No tile tells | FAIL | The same pinched corner crease on most stones (`PL2_few_near` 1190 60 175 190, 1130 515 170 150). There are dashed black lines in the bed joints (`PL2_few_near` 660 480 300 20, 960 255 300 20). |
| 4 | Stone to stone | PASS | Shapes and sizes vary. One tone under a wash is right. |
| 5 | Edges and joints | FAIL | Every stone is outlined in navy blue on its bottom and right edges and in cream on its top and left edges. These are coloured lines drawn on, the worst tell in the round (`PL2_few_near` 330 60 620 230, 0 290 340 210). |
| 6 | Holds at distance | FAIL | At 5 m it reads as a quilted cream panel with brown stains in each stone's centre and thin dark outlines (`PL2_few_far` 0 0 560 360). |

By lamplight (`PL2_few_saloon_night`), the cream lines become glowing gold strokes along every top edge (0 290 340 30, 330 65 620 40).

**Grade: fake.**

### PL2_old

| # | Check | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Real stuff | FAIL | The same as few (`PL2_old_near` 680 280 240 210). |
| 2 | Age reads | FAIL | Old is only browner and blotchier, with rougher mortar. The wash doesn't flake or thin to show stone, and there is no grimy band where men lean and no low scuffs (`PL2_old_near` 940 100 300 400 vs the same box in few). |
| 3 | No tile tells | FAIL | The same repeated crease, and the dashed joint lines (`PL2_old_near` 660 480 300 20). |
| 4 | Stone to stone | PASS | As in few. |
| 5 | Edges and joints | FAIL | Navy and cream outlines on every stone (`PL2_old_near` 330 60 620 230). |
| 6 | Holds at distance | FAIL | A padded panel with sepia stains and drawn outlines (`PL2_old_far` 0 0 560 360). |

**Grade: fake.**

**PL2: one change that would move it up most:** remove the navy and cream outlines. Let the wash lie thicker in the joints and hollows and thinner on the stones' high points, so that the rubble's own relief and the room's light show the stones.
**Best age: few.**
