# Blind critic, 2026-10-27_r1

1. **The street is a dark silhouette, not a sunlit town** (street). The painting is bright and airy: blue sky behind golden clouds, sun-struck facades in warm reds and bleached whites, a red saloon with huge red lettering, lit lanterns, a pale sunny road. The render is sunk in heavy orange-brown dusk: the fronts are near-black grey-brown timber in shadow, the signs are dim dark letters on dark boards, the sky has no blue at all, and the road is a dark rust brown with long black shadows. A viewer reads the painting as a cheerful golden afternoon and the render as a gloomy sunset in a canyon. Crop: `critic/1_street_dark_fronts.png`
2. **The man at the table is the wrong man in the wrong pose** (saloon). The painting's man sits low at the table in a hat and long brown coat, leaning in on his elbows, both hands round his cup, his warm-lit face square to the viewer. The render's man is hatless in a vest and red bow, half-standing so his head is cut off by the top of the frame, holding the mug up in the air with the other hand clawed open beside it and a leg stuck out stiffly. He reads as a mannequin posed mid-gesture rather than a man settled at cards. Crop: `critic/2_table_man.png`
3. **Every man has the same face and no hat** (saloon). In the painting the card players, the bartender and the men at the bar are different men, nearly all in wide hats, playing cards round a lamp. In the render the card players, the bartender and the main man all share one moustached face and slicked dark or grey hair, most are bareheaded in the same white shirt and burgundy vest, and they talk with open hands instead of holding cards. It looks like a room full of clones. Crop: `critic/3_same_faces.png`
4. **The tabletop is coarse orange noise** (saloon). The painting's table is smooth honey-coloured wood with long planks, soft grain and a warm sheen where the lamp falls. The render's top is a saturated red-orange covered in coarse, noisy squares that shimmer as speckle. Beside the smoother faces it reads as a low-resolution texture, and the pale, heavily knotted bar front behind it looks like a different, cold, bleached wood. Crop: `critic/4_table_top.png`
5. **The table lamp has no glass and no glow** (saloon). The painting's lamp is a tall glass chimney glowing near-white over the flame, on a brass base, with a gold halo of light round it and a bright pool on the table. The render's lamp is a squat dark bronze base with three prongs and a bare flame floating above it: no chimney, no halo, and little light thrown onto the table. A viewer loses the warm heart of the shot. Crop: `critic/5_lamp.png`
