# Blind critic, #873 (the eased edges and the handrail)

This merge's blind critic is the cheap judge's round in this folder (`cheap.md`): a fresh sub-agent that saw
only the renders, the rubric and the AAA references, not the work or its reasoning; the six views were
before/after pairs it was not told apart (key in `key_unblinded.json`). Its crops are the `*_*.jpg` here.

Top three (what a viewer notices first, across the views):
1. The rooms are empty: no people, no clutter on the gallery, the desk bare but for two ink bottles.
2. The sun lands on the floor with no air in it: no shaft, no dust, no haze.
3. Too clean: the floors glossy with no wear path, the desk's varnish spotless, every rail, baluster and
   cubby dead straight and regular.

On the change itself (its "Pairs"): the gallery rail clearly better after (rounded top catching a soft
highlight, a worn, handled rail, where before was a sharp-cornered box); the stairwell slightly better (the
rails' tops rounded and lit; the newel post still a sharp box in both); the doctor's desk the same but for a
softer seam (a kit prop, not a library board, so not eased).
