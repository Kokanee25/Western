# Materials judge: 2026-11-11_paint2_r1i (P5, P6; sets P and Q)

Blind judge. Rubric: `docs/concept/judge/MATERIAL.md` (painted wood). Yardstick: RDR2 street noon,
street golden, saloon night; Hunt 1896 street noon, saloon night; KCD2 street noon. Only the
wood and the paint on it were judged. The warm cast at night and the cream cast at golden hour
belong to the light. Crop boxes are `x y w h` in the named crop's own pixels.

No saw-toothed sun-shadow edge shows on these interior boards, so nothing was discounted for it.

How the sets differ: P and Q are very close (mean per-pixel difference 1-4 levels out of 255). Q is a little
less yellow at every age, and its grain figure is calmer, most visibly in the old ages. At night
P's figure reads as bare varnished wood more than Q's does. The fine detail is the same in both.
Every difference below is **within a grade**. None is a full grade.

---

## P5: pale cream interior paint on narrow planed pine (beaded tongue-and-groove)

### P5 few, set P
1. Real stuff: **PASS.** A cream oil film over beaded boards. The grain shows through as a soft
   figure. The short white dry-brush flecks in the figure come close to a streak texture
   (`P5_few_P_near.png` 450 250 450 300), but they don't fail it.
2. Age reads: **PASS.** A sound coat with a faint yellowing. That is believable for a few years
   indoors, and it differs from old (old has darker joints, more yellow and chips at 5 m).
3. No tile tells: **PASS.** No repeat, seam, blocks or weave at 1 m.
4. Board to board: **PASS.** The boards are almost identical in tone, which a single coat
   allows. The figure differs from board to board.
5. Edges and joints: **PASS.** At noon the beads and grooves read as shadowed hollows. At night they
   go to thin, even dark lines that look a little ruled (`P5_few_P_saloon_night.png`
   540 0 140 780), but not drawn on.
6. Holds at distance: **PASS.** At 5 m the lines are steady, with no shimmer or mush. It is plain, as a
   painted wall is.
- **Grade: near the yardstick.**

### P5 few, set Q
1. Real stuff **PASS** (the flecks are calmer than P's). 2. Age reads **PASS**. 3. No tile tells
   **PASS**. 4. Board to board **PASS**. 5. Edges and joints **PASS** (at night the lines are
   as ruled as P's). 6. Holds at distance **PASS**.
- **Grade: near the yardstick.** Slightly better than P (a calmer, more paint-like surface),
  within the grade.

### P5 old, set P
1. Real stuff: **FAIL.** Under the lamps the figure is strong and arched, so it reads as bare
   varnished pine and not as an old coat of paint (`P5_old_P_saloon_night.png` 0 0 520 780). At
   noon it reads as paint.
2. Age reads: **FAIL.** It is more yellow, with darker joints and a pale replaced board, but at 1 m
   nothing shows hand-rubbing, scuffing low, chipping to primer or crazing. It reads as
   "a few years, dirtier" and not as old (`P5_old_P_near.png` 0 480 1400 300). Chips show only at
   5 m (`P5_old_P_far.png` 250 318 200 25).
3. No tile tells: **FAIL.** The centres of the grooves carry a bright, regular stitched dash, like
   a zipper or seam stitching, stamped along the joint (`P5_old_P_near.png` 900 0 200 520).
4. Board to board: **PASS.** One board is distinctly paler (`P5_old_P_near.png` 1050 0 160 780),
   which reads as a later repair, and the others vary a little in yellowing.
5. Edges and joints: **FAIL.** The same stitched dashes read as lines drawn into the joint and
   not as a shadowed gap filled with grime (`P5_old_P_near.png` 900 0 200 520).
6. Holds at distance: **PASS.** It is steady at 5 m, and the chips on the rail read as flakes.
- **Grade: game.**

### P5 old, set Q
1. Real stuff: **PASS.** It reads as an aged paint film at noon and under the lamps (the figure is
   calmer than P's).
2. Age reads: **FAIL.** As P: no rubbing, scuffs or flaking at 1 m (`P5_old_Q_near.png` 0 480 1400 300).
3. No tile tells: **FAIL.** The stitched dashes are in the groove centres (`P5_old_Q_near.png` 900 0 200 520).
4. Board to board: **PASS.** The replaced board is less obvious than in P, but it is there.
5. Edges and joints: **FAIL.** The same dashes (`P5_old_Q_near.png` 900 0 200 520).
6. Holds at distance: **PASS.**
- **Grade: game.** Better than P (it reads as paint at night), within the grade.

**P5: the one change that would move it up most:** give the old age real indoor wear (rubbed
through to primer and wood at hand height and round the arrises, scuffs and chips low, fine
crazing along the grain) and take the stitched dash out of the groove centres, leaving grime
packed in a shadowed joint. Keep Q's calmer figure. **Best age: few.**

## P6: pale grey-green interior paint (a surgery's) on narrow planed pine

### P6 few, set P
1. Real stuff: **PASS.** A grey-green oil film over beaded boards. The grain telegraphs as a
   rippled, raised figure that edges towards embossed vinyl "woodgrain" siding
   (`P6_few_P_near.png` 820 260 120 260). You see it on a look, but it doesn't fail.
2. Age reads: **PASS.** It is a sound coat and clearly cleaner than old.
3. No tile tells: **PASS.**
4. Board to board: **PASS.** One coat with the figure varying, though the boards are very even in tone.
5. Edges and joints: **PASS.** Shadowed beads at noon. At night they are thin ruled lines
   (`P6_few_P_saloon_night.png` 540 0 140 780).
6. Holds at distance: **PASS.** At 5 m the golden light washes the green out, but that is the light's
   doing. The lines are steady and nothing shimmers.
- **Grade: near the yardstick.**

### P6 few, set Q
1. Real stuff **PASS** (the same ripples, a shade softer: `P6_few_Q_near.png` 830 270 110 240).
   2. Age reads **PASS**. 3. No tile tells **PASS**. 4. Board to board **PASS**. 5. Edges and
   joints **PASS**. 6. Holds at distance **PASS**.
- **Grade: near the yardstick.** Very slightly better than P, within the grade (the sets are
  nearly indistinguishable at this age).

### P6 old, set P
1. Real stuff: **FAIL.** At noon the raised, wavy grain is strong all over and reads as embossed
   vinyl or composite siding (`P6_old_P_near.png` 0 0 700 780). Under the lamps the figure reads
   as bare varnished hardwood and not as paint (`P6_old_P_saloon_night.png` 0 0 700 780).
2. Age reads: **FAIL.** It is more yellow, with grimy joints, but there is no hand-rubbing,
   scuffing or chipping at 1 m (`P6_old_P_near.png` 0 480 1400 300). Flakes show only at 5 m on the rail.
3. No tile tells: **FAIL.** The groove centres carry the stitched dash (`P6_old_P_near.png` 700 200 400 400).
4. Board to board: **PASS.** The yellowing and figure vary a little from board to board.
5. Edges and joints: **FAIL.** The stitched dash in the joints reads as drawn on
   (`P6_old_P_near.png` 700 200 400 400). Some grooves go to a bluish-black hard line
   (`P6_old_P_near.png` 420 0 140 780).
6. Holds at distance: **PASS.**
- **Grade: game.**

### P6 old, set Q
1. Real stuff: **PASS.** The figure is calmer, so it reads as an aged film over planed boards, at noon
   and at night.
2. Age reads: **FAIL.** As P (`P6_old_Q_near.png` 0 480 1400 300).
3. No tile tells: **FAIL.** The stitched dash is in the groove centres (`P6_old_Q_near.png` 700 200 400 400).
4. Board to board: **PASS.**
5. Edges and joints: **FAIL.** The same dashes and hard bluish-black lines (`P6_old_Q_near.png` 700 200 400 400).
6. Holds at distance: **PASS.**
- **Grade: game.** Better than P (no embossed-siding look, and it reads as paint at night), within
  the grade, and the clearest P/Q gap in this run.

**P6: the one change that would move it up most:** the same as P5: real indoor wear on the old age and no stitched
dash in the grooves. Also flatten the rippled raised grain, so the planed pine sits under the film
as a faint figure and not as embossed siding. **Best age: few.**
