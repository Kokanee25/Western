# Blind critic, 2026-10-27_r5

1. **The man at the table is the wrong man in the wrong pose** (saloon). The painting's man sits low and slumped at the table in a hat and brown coat, one hand round his cup on the wood, eyes on the viewer under the brim. The render's man is hatless in a waistcoat, half-standing over the table so he fills the frame from the top edge down to the floor. He holds a pale, blurry mug up at his chest while his other hand claws at nothing, and his legs are one dark shapeless mass. The scene's centre stops being a quiet, wary stranger and becomes an awkward mannequin, and his skin is a hot orange that reads as sunburn rather than lamplight. 2. **The table top is a noisy red-orange mosaic** (saloon). The painting's table is honey-gold planks with long smooth grain, a soft glossy sheen and clear plank seams. The render's top is a hot red-orange field of coarse, jittery squares with no planks or seams, and the stack of coins beside it is noisy too. It is the biggest surface in the foreground and it looks like static next to the smoothly painted men. Crop: `critic/2_table_top.png`
3. **The street's paint and signs are gone: everything is one weathered brown** (street). In the painting the saloon is barn-red with white trim, its sign a cream board with big red slab letters and scrollwork, and the store's and jail's boards are pale with crisp dark lettering. In the render every front is the same grey-brown wood, SALOON is a small dark stencil smudged into the board, and GENERAL STORE can't be read. The street loses its colour accents and its friendly storybook read, and you can't tell the buildings apart at a glance. Crop: `critic/4_saloon_front.png`
4. **The saloon's back has no depth or warmth: flat pale walls where the painting has a rich lit room** (saloon). Past the bar the painting is layered: carved oak pillars, a balcony gallery, a gilt mirror, many lamps, each with a glow. The render shows a blank beige-grey wall over the bottle shelves, a bleached grey-white bar front with oversized knots, a mostly dark upper left, and only a few wall lamps. The room feels like an empty set instead of a busy, glowing hall. Crop: `critic/3_back_wall.png`
5. **The street ends in a canyon under a brooding sky, not an open sunset** (street). The painting looks down the street to an open plain: distant houses, telegraph poles, a big pale sun disc low on the horizon, and bright pink-and-cream cumulus in a clear blue sky. The render closes the view with towering orange rock spires, hides the sun behind them, and fills the upper sky with dark, streaky purple-brown clouds. The mood moves from a warm, hopeful evening to something heavier and boxed-in. Crop: `critic/5_street_end.png`
