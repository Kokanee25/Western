# Blind critic, 2026-11-13_saloon_b_r2 (saloon B, the look)

## The five biggest differences a viewer would notice
1. **Night light: the building is a black silhouette, the rooms are red-orange caves** (pair_corner, pair_bar, pair_dance, pair_gallery; `crop_corner_night.jpg`, `crop_bar_ceiling.jpg`). The painting's night is carried by light: six or so wall lamps under the porch wash the siding, posts and boardwalk in warm gold, the big front windows blaze, and inside several hanging lamps give a soft, neutral-warm fill that reaches the ceiling joists and the floor. The render's exterior has no porch lamps at all, the windows glow weakly, and the walls, posts and porch deck fall to near black against a purple sky. Inside, a few small lamps light a patch of wall each and everything else drops to deep saturated red-brown; the floor and ceiling are almost unlit. Cause: light. Cheapest change: add the wall lamps under the porch (one per bay, as painted) and raise the range and energy of the interior lamps with a less orange colour, plus a low warm ambient fill indoors so shadows go to brown, not black.
2. **Wood colour and finish: fresh orange-red varnish instead of dry, grey-brown, dusty timber** (pair_door, pair_bar, pair_dance, pair_gallery; `crop_gallery_rail.jpg`, `crop_door_interior.jpg`). The painting's floors are pale, worn, matt grey-brown boards with dust, scuffs and sawdust; posts and joists are dark, rough and desaturated; only the bar is rich mahogany. The render's floor, posts, joists and railings are all one saturated orange-red wood with a bold, large-scale cathedral grain and a glossy sheen (the daytime floor reflects the window like a polished gym floor), so the bar no longer stands out and the place looks newly built. Cause: material. Cheapest change: one floor/timber material pass: cut saturation by about half, shift hue from red-orange toward grey-brown, raise roughness to kill the sheen, and shrink the grain pattern; keep the rich red only for the bar.
3. **Front: no corner entrance, no weathered cream trim, a flat dark box** (pair_front, pair_corner; `crop_front_facade.jpg`, `crop_front_painting_trim.jpg`). The painting's signature is the chamfered corner with glazed double doors, tall shopfront windows with panelled bases, chunky pale weathered posts with curved brackets, a turned-baluster balcony wrapping the corner, and a dark maroon sign board hung on the balcony. The render shows a flat front with three ordinary windows and a single narrow door at the far left, thin dark stick posts with no brackets, a thin picket balcony, and a blank white board high on a false-front parapet. In daylight the whole front is in flat blue-grey shade with trim barely lighter than the wall. Cause: geometry (corner door, shopfront, posts) and material (trim colour). Cheapest change: paint all porch posts, balcony rail, window and door casings a pale weathered cream-grey and thicken the posts with brackets; that restores the light-trim-on-dark-red read even before the corner is rebuilt. The sign board should be dark maroon and sit at balcony level.
4. **Siding: vertical boards in a uniform flat red, no belt course or corner boards** (pair_rear, pair_front; `crop_rear_wall.jpg`). The painting has horizontal clapboard, faded and uneven (sun-bleached pinkish on the lit side, deep red in shade), cut by a cream belt course between the storeys, cream corner boards, wide window casings with sills and a clear gable with a trimmed eave. The render's walls read as vertical boards in one even barn red with no fading, no belt course, no corner boards, small thinly cased windows and a low dark roof with almost no eave; the rear stair is a thin dark open-riser flight instead of a heavy pale timber stair with a landing. Cause: material (siding direction, weathering) and geometry (trim). Cheapest change: turn the siding texture horizontal with lap shadows and add tonal variation, then add the cream belt course and corner boards as plain boards.
5. **Interior walls: yellow striped boards with dark streaks instead of pale plaster over a dark wainscot; no haze** (pair_door, pair_dance, pair_bar; `crop_door_interior.jpg`, `crop_dance_slab.jpg`). The painting's rooms have off-white to tan plaster or whitewashed board walls, a dark wood wainscot to chair height, and heavy dark casings round windows and doors; lamp smoke hangs in the air and softens everything. The render's walls are a yellow-cream vertical board with a pattern of short dark vertical dashes that reads as stains or missing texels, a wainscot only in places, thin casings, and perfectly clear air. Cause: material, a little light. Cheapest change: swap the wall material for a plain warm off-white plaster/whitewash without the dash pattern and run the dark wainscot round every room; a faint interior haze would do the rest.

(Camera, once: the exterior renders are framed far back and from different sides than the paintings, with the building small in an empty plain, so they cannot be compared like for like. Emptiness of furniture, people and sign lettering is expected at this stage and not counted.)

## What already matches
- The overall massing: two storeys, a porch on posts with a railed balcony above, in dark red.
- The interior structure: a double-height room with a railed gallery, exposed ceiling joists on beams and posts with curved brackets, plank floors laid the right way.
- The dance hall's open trussed roof and wall-mounted lamps, and the back bar with its arched mirror bays.
- The rear's outside stair to an upper door, and the cream-cased sash windows on red siding.

## Any outright faults
- **Black slab filling the right third of the frame** (pair_dance, right side of the render; `crop_dance_slab.jpg`): a pure black, unlit rectangle from floor to above head height, probably a door leaf or wall end standing in front of the camera with no light on it; a smaller black slab also stands at the far right of pair_bar.
- **Light leaking through the structure** (pair_bar, upper left of the render, a row of five bright oval spots on the dark ceiling; pair_gallery, bright strips between the balusters at the bottom of the left railing; `crop_bar_ceiling.jpg`, `crop_gallery_rail.jpg`): lamps shining through floors or railings with no shadow.
- **Bright slots along the top of the far beam** (pair_bar, centre right; `crop_bar_ceiling.jpg`): a row of lit gaps between the joist ends above the beam, as if the gallery edge is open to a lit space behind.
- **Dark dashes on the cream walls** (pair_door, pair_dance, pair_bar): read as texture faults or pinholes rather than weathering.
- **Blank white sign board** (pair_front, top of the facade; `crop_front_facade.jpg`): far brighter than anything else in shade, and the dark panel at the far right of pair_bar is speckled with bright dots like pinholes.
- **Mirror-like floor in daylight** (pair_door, floor at centre and right): a sharp bright reflection of the window on plank flooring.
- The stair and porch supports look thread-thin for their span (pair_rear, pair_front), and the front porch deck appears to stand on stilts above the ground with no skirt.
