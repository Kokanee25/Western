## The five biggest differences

1. **Shop and counter (interior):** the target is a warm, packed grocer's: shelving walls, jars, a pot-bellied stove, lamps, cream clapboard with a green wainscot, sunlit floor. The render is a dark, empty hall of bare posts, grey-green striped walls and a uniform brown floor. Cause: dressing (props known to be missing), plus light (no warm window shafts, flat murky ambient) and material (walls read as grey-green pinstripe, not cream boards).
2. **Cellar:** the target has shafts of light from the cellar doors, a stair up to the double doors, barrels, hanging hams and a lit lantern over a sandy floor. The render is almost black, with a cobbled grey-blue floor, red-tinted posts and no stair or light. Cause: light and geometry (no stair or cellar-door openings visible), material (floor is stone, not packed earth).
3. **Rear and side:** the target is a gabled, shingled, white clapboard wing with a hoist door, loading balcony, chimney, fences and a woodpile, in warm low sun. The render is a flat-topped, near-featureless box with a shallow roof edge, a large flat yellow-tan wall, and bare yard, with a hard shadow cast across it. In the side view the render also seats the building far away behind grey boxes. Cause: geometry (roof form, missing wing, hoist) and camera, dressing.
4. **Front:** the target is pale cream clapboard with white-trimmed shopfront windows, double doors, a wide steps-and-cellar-flap boardwalk, and a plain painted sign band. The render is dull olive-yellow, with a flat dark "GENERAL STORE" panel at the parapet instead of the lower signboard, a much weaker porch and shopfront (small windows, a railing and tiny door), and a dark ground in cool dusk light. Cause: material (colour too yellow-green and dark), geometry (shopfront glazing, door and sign position), light (cool, low-key versus golden hour).
5. **Dock (under the porch):** the target has big weathered posts, a tilted cellar flap over a stair-down hole, a barrel and a wagon wheel in warm backlight. The render has a railing, blue-tinted steps and a very black ground, with no cellar hole or flap. Cause: geometry (no cellar entrance), light (ground nearly black), dressing.
