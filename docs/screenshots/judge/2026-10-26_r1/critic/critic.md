# Blind critic, 2026-10-26_r1

1. **The saloon is a black cave, not a lamplit room** (saloon). In the painting every wall, post and beam glows honey-amber, and each sconce lights a warm pool on the planks around it, so the whole room reads warm and busy. In the render the side wall, the posts, the ceiling and the back of the room fall to near-black with only one or two hot spots, and the wall lamps there blow out to white blobs instead of small flames with halos. A viewer sees a dark, empty room at night, not the cosy saloon. Crop: `critic/1_saloon_dark.png`
2. **The street is in murky brown shade instead of sunlit** (street). The painting's road is bright orange-tan with sun on it, pale porches and steps, and soft grey-green sage in the light. In the render the ground, the porches and the boardwalk are deep chocolate brown, the grass is dark spiky clumps, and the street's lower half is close to black, so the golden hour is only in the sky. Crop: `critic/3_street_ground.png`
3. **The man at the table has no hat, dark clothes in hard blotches, and a stiff pose** (saloon). The painting's man wears a broad hat and a tan duster lit warm, slouches over his cup, and his coat is painted in soft, smooth strokes. The render's man is bareheaded, in near-black clothes broken into hard-edged flat patches of dark and cream (beside a smoothly shaded face), and holds the mug and his other hand up stiffly at chest height. He reads as a cut-out figure, not the hero of the picture. Crop: `critic/8_saloon_man2.png`
4. **Every front on the street is the same dull grey-brown wood** (street). The painting has a red-painted saloon with big red lettering on a cream sign board, a pale grey general store with clear dark lettering, and sunlit cream timber trim. The render's buildings are all one dark, weathered brown, and the signs are small, dark lettering on dark boards that you can hardly read. The town loses its colour and its landmarks. Crop: `critic/4_street_fronts.png`
5. **The bar front is bleached pale wood with giant knots** (saloon). The painting's bar is warm dark-amber panelling with mouldings and a brass foot rail, and it sits back in the room's glow. The render's bar is flat horizontal boards in a washed-out grey-cream with oversized knots and coarse streaky grain, the brightest thing in the room after the lamp. It looks like raw fencing beside the dark room and pulls the eye from the table. Crop: `critic/5_saloon_bar.png`
