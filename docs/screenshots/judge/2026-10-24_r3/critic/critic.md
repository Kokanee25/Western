1. **One orange cast over everything; no blue sky, no cool shade** (street). The painting is golden hour with contrast: a clear blue sky overhead, cream and pink clouds, a white sun low over the distance, and shadows on the ground and under the porches with some cool in them. The render's sky is orange-to-yellow edge to edge with no blue anywhere, the dirt is the same orange, and the shade is a deeper brown-orange of the same hue, so the whole frame reads as one sepia filter, hot and smoky rather than warm and clear. 2. **Buildings dark brown and backlit instead of sunlit weathered wood** (street). In the painting the fronts take the low sun: silver-grey and tan boards, a red-painted saloon, crisp cream signboards with dark lettering ("DRY GOODS", "GENERAL STORE") that read clearly. In the render the false fronts are a uniform dark brown in their own shadow, the general store's lettering is barely legible, the signs further down are mush, and the right edge of the frame is filled by a big near-black brown wall with no detail. The street feels gloomy and enclosed where the painting feels bright and open. Crop: `critic/2_buildings.png`

3. **The horses: small dark clumps in the road instead of one handsome horse at the rail** (street). The painting gives one glossy chestnut horse, side-on in full sun at a hitching rail: saddle and bridle readable, a dark mane and tail, white socks, light catching his flank. The render has a crowd of four or five horses milling in the middle of the street with men among them, all a dark red-brown in shadow, with stiff blocky legs and heavy black tails; they read as silhouettes, and the one near the jail is half hidden behind a man. Give at least one horse a tied pose at a rail, lit side-on, with tack and a warm, varied coat. Crop: `critic/3_horses.png`

4. **The street is bare: no props on the porches, coarse grass instead of sage** (street). The painting's boardwalks are crowded: barrels, stacked crates, a red Navajo blanket on the rail, benches with men on them, sage and rocks along the road, and a water tower, windmill and trees beyond. The render's porches are mostly empty planks, the roadside dressing is a few clumps of tan grass drawn in coarse, noisy squares that sit badly beside the smooth sky and ground, and the far end is a closed canyon wall instead of a lived-in town running into the distance. Crop: `critic/4_dressing.png`

5. **People are dim, unlit figures; the lamps glow in banded rings** (street). The painting's people are lit and characterful: the man on the saloon porch with his red waistcoat, white shirt, long coat and moustache, others in vests and hats, all reading as people at a glance. The render's townsfolk are dark brown and black shapes standing in shade, their faces and clothes lost, and the porch lamps throw stepped, concentric rings of light on the wall that look like a graphics fault rather than lamplight.
