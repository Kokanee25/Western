1. **The room behind the man is a dim, empty brown box** (saloon). The painting's back half is busy and lit: a gallery balcony with turned balusters and a man leaning on the rail against a blue night window, a staircase, a mounted deer head, framed pictures, a piano player, and a dozen lamps each pooling warm light on the posts, with pipe smoke hanging in the air. In the render that same area is mostly flat, plank-dark wall in one orange-brown, with a thin rail, two wall lamps and a few figures (one a long-coated back blocking the middle). The painting reads as a crowded, lived-in saloon; the render reads as an empty set lit by one colour. Crop: `critic/1_back_room.png`
2. **The low sun gives no golden light** (street). The painting's sun is a large, saturated yellow-orange disc in a gap of the red cliffs, with rays fanning out, warm haze filling the far street, and a sky going from deep blue to gold and peach. Its light rakes the false fronts and throws long shadows toward you. The render's sky is a pale lavender-pink, with orange smears for clouds and a small white sun mostly hidden behind the rock; the cliffs are a flat tan and the building faces sit in even, dull shade. The painting feels like late, hot sunset; the render feels like an overcast evening. Crop: `critic/4_sky_sun.png`
3. **The doorway shows a navy wedge and a blank wall, not the town** (saloon). Through the painting's door you see a moonlit two-storey building with warm lit windows, a horse, trees, swinging batwing doors and a soft blue night sky. The render's doorway is mostly a plain brown wall cutting across at a slant, with a hard-edged triangle of navy and grey-white streaks above it and a pale grey moon. The render loses the cool-blue-against-warm contrast that sets the painting's mood. Crop: `critic/2_doorway.png`
4. **The man's face is pale, hot and staring** (saloon). The painting's face is weathered tan with darker shadow under the brim, eyes narrowed and looking at you sideways, cheeks and jaw modelled in a few large, calm squares, and a patterned hat band. The render's face is washed with near-white highlights on the nose, cheeks and forehead, the eyes are wide open with bright whites, and the squares break up into a noisy patchwork; the hat is blotchy light-and-dark camouflage. He looks startled and overlit rather than tired and dangerous. Crop: `critic/3_face.png`
5. **The street is sparse: weeds and loose horses, few people on the porches** (street). The painting's boardwalks are dressed and peopled: men sitting and standing on the porches, barrels, crates, a red Navajo rug over the rail, glowing lanterns, a horse tied at a hitching rail, a wagon, a water tower, a windmill and trees down the street. The render has two dark horses standing loose in the open road, rows of identical yellow grass clumps along both edges, a couple of dim figures in the shadow of the porch and an unbroken run of grey fronts with small, unreadable signs. It reads as an emptier, less finished town. Crop: `critic/5_street_dressing.png`
