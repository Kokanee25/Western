# Materials judge: painted wood, round 2 (2026-11-11_paint_r2)

Blind judge. Rubric: `docs/concept/judge/MATERIAL.md` (painted wood). Yardstick: the six AAA
screenshots named in the brief, read for their wood only (RDR2's porch boards and post, its saloon's
balusters and rails; Hunt 1896's grey weathered planks and boat ribs; KCD2's shingles and painted
fronts). Crops seen: all 32 (near and far, sets P and Q, ages few and old) at full size, with
zoomed sub-crops of the near views. Crop boxes are `x y w h` in the named file's own pixels.

Noted once, not counted: the saw-toothed edge of the sun's shadow along laps and battens (P1 and P4
laps, P2_old_Q joints, P3 left battens) is the light's known fault.

Common to every set (both P and Q): the near crops have no repeat, seam or square blocks; boards are
recognisably boards under a film of paint; the faults are in where and how the paint fails, and in
what survives at 5 m.

## P1: white lead in linseed oil, on planed pine lap siding

### Set P

| check | result | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Real stuff | FAIL | Few reads as white oil paint with grain under it; old reads as a stamped "peeling paint" texture: dark flecks spread evenly like a leopard print, a grey lace net over the film, cracks as thin black squiggles. `P1_old_P_near.png 0 380 700 240`, cracks `P1_old_P_near.png 980 20 260 30` |
| 2 Age reads | FAIL | The ages differ, but not as paint fails. Few already has flecks out in the middle of the boards (`P1_few_P_near.png 450 270 500 200`), not at lap edges or board ends. Old is the same flecks, denser, still a sound white film: no strips failed to primer or grey wood. |
| 3 No tile tells | FAIL | Evenly scattered dark dots plus faint pale "ghost" dots over every board (`P1_few_P_near.png 1100 0 300 250`); a lace crazing net (`P1_old_P_near.png 0 0 500 110`); the nail rust marks read as orange pins (`P1_old_P_near.png 530 50 60 300`). |
| 4 Board to board | FAIL | Every board is the same white and has the same density of flecks; no board has chalked or weathered more than its neighbour. `P1_few_P_far.png 0 0 560 360` |
| 5 Edges and joints | PASS | The laps read as shadowed overlaps, not drawn lines (sawtooth noted above). |
| 6 Holds at distance | FAIL | Few at 5 m is a flat peach card whose laps are hairlines (`P1_few_P_far.png 0 0 560 360`); old at 5 m is speckle noise (`P1_old_P_far.png 0 100 560 200`). |

Grades: **few: game**. **old: fake.**

### Set Q

| check | result | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Real stuff | PASS | White oil paint with the pine's figure under it (`P1_old_Q_near.png 950 250 450 120`, cathedral grain through the film). The crazing and squiggle cracks are tells, not a failure of what the material is. |
| 2 Age reads | FAIL | Old puts the failure where water sits, along the lower edges of the laps (`P1_old_Q_near.png 0 230 1400 30`): right. But few still has flecks mid-board (`P1_few_Q_near.png 900 100 400 300`), and old is still a mostly sound white coat, with no runs of bare grey wood or primer. |
| 3 No tile tells | FAIL | Scattered flecks and pale ghost dots (`P1_few_Q_near.png 900 0 500 380`); a crazing net drawn as wandering contour lines (`P1_few_Q_near.png 0 0 700 100`); cracks as black squiggles (`P1_old_Q_near.png 300 700 340 40`). |
| 4 Board to board | FAIL | Every board has the same tone and the same wear. `P1_few_Q_far.png 0 0 560 360` |
| 5 Edges and joints | PASS | The laps read as overlaps, and the peel along their edges reads as a film letting go. |
| 6 Holds at distance | FAIL (few) / PASS (old) | Old keeps its character at 5 m: the peel lines up along the laps (`P1_old_Q_far.png`). Few is a flat card (`P1_few_Q_far.png 0 0 560 360`). |

Grades: **few: game**. **old: game.**

**P vs Q:** Q is better. At old the difference is a full grade (fake to game); at few it is within a grade.
**Best age:** few.
**One change to move it up most:** make the coat fail where water works it, in strips along each lap's lower edge and at board ends, down to primer and grey bare wood, and take out the even mid-board flecks, the ghost dots and the lace crazing net.

## P2: chrome green trim paint, on planed pine boards (casings, corner boards)

### Set P

| check | result | evidence |
|---|---|---|

| 2 Age reads | FAIL | The fade and chalk to grey-green at old reads right. But the failure is evenly spread over the faces, not at the board ends, edges or foot, and old has not failed in strips to bare wood (`P2_old_P_near.png 0 0 1400 780`). |
| 3 No tile tells | FAIL | The flecks are uniform confetti (`P2_few_P_near.png 380 380 400 350`); the nail heads are bright orange dots in ruler rows (`P2_old_P_near.png 0 260 1400 30`); the cracks are dark squiggles wandering across the grain (`P2_old_P_near.png 410 520 90 260`). |
| 4 Board to board | PASS | Tone and figure vary a little from board to board; it isn't noise. |
| 5 Edges and joints | FAIL (old) | Old's joints are even, pure-black slots of one width, with no worn arris on either side (`P2_old_P_near.png 520 0 40 780`). Few's are acceptable. |
| 6 Holds at distance | PASS | Both ages keep their boards, flecks and cracks at 5 m without shimmer. The cracks read as worms there (`P2_old_P_far.png 100 20 300 160`), but they are not mush. |

Grades: **few: game**. **old: game.**

### Set Q

| check | result | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Real stuff | PASS | Green paint over planed pine with brushy grain under it. The chips are fewer, and their halos show only up close (`P2_old_Q_near.png 1040 390 60 60`). |
| 2 Age reads | FAIL | The same as P: the ages are clearly different in fade, but the failure is scattered and old has no strips to bare wood (`P2_old_Q_near.png 0 0 1400 780`). |
| 3 No tile tells | FAIL | Confetti flecks (`P2_few_Q_near.png 780 200 450 400`); orange nail dots in rows (`P2_old_Q_near.png 0 50 1400 30`); squiggle cracks (`P2_old_Q_near.png 590 260 40 360`). |
| 4 Board to board | PASS | The boards vary in tone and figure. |
| 5 Edges and joints | FAIL (old) | Uniform black slots (`P2_old_Q_near.png 480 0 30 780`); the sawtooth along others is the light's fault. Few passes. |
| 6 Holds at distance | PASS | `P2_old_Q_far.png` and `P2_few_Q_far.png` keep their character. |

Grades: **few: game**. **old: game.**

**P vs Q:** Q is better, within a grade (fewer and quieter chips at old, the grain more alive).
**Best age:** few.
**One change to move it up most:** move the failure from evenly scattered flecks to the board ends, edges and foot, in strips down to grey wood; drop the pale halo round each chip; and make the cracks fine checks that follow the grain rather than squiggles across it.

## P3: ox-blood red (iron oxide in linseed oil), on rough-sawn board and batten

### Set P

| check | result | evidence |
|---|---|---|

A smooth, smeared patch at near (`P3_few_P_near.png 1000 100 250 250`). |
| 2 Age reads | FAIL | Old's chalking reads right. Its failure is small flecks only, with no runs of grey bare wood at the batten feet or board ends (`P3_old_P_near.png 0 0 1400 780`). |
| 3 No tile tells | FAIL | Old's chips are teal-grey blobs with dark outlines, spread evenly like dots (`P3_old_P_near.png 900 150 500 350`). |

| 6 Holds at distance | FAIL | The battens shrink to hairlines and the wall goes to a flat orange card with figure (`P3_old_P_far.png 0 0 560 360`). |

Grades: **few: game**. **old: game.**

### Set Q

| check | result | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Real stuff | FAIL | Near, it is convincing: saw arcs and growth rings under the film (`P3_few_Q_near.png 400 100 500 400`). At 5 m it is again orange plywood with a strong cathedral figure (`P3_few_Q_far.png 230 20 80 330`). |

**Best age:** few.
**One change to move it up most:** let the paint fill the grain. Keep the rough-sawn relief but kill the cathedral figure showing through the film, which turns it into stained plywood at 5 m. Give the battens real relief and shadow instead of a red stripe.

## P4: buff (ochre and white lead), on planed pine drop siding

### Set P

| check | result | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Real stuff | FAIL | Few reads as buff oil paint over pine. Old reads as a stamped peeling texture: blue-grey leopard spots over every board (`P4_old_P_near.png 0 120 700 200`), with squiggle cracks (`P4_old_P_near.png 1000 490 400 60`). |
| 2 Age reads | FAIL | Few's flecks are mid-board (`P4_few_P_near.png 800 150 500 300`). Old is evenly spotted, not failed in strips to grey wood. |
| 3 No tile tells | FAIL | Scattered flecks and faint round tan ghost dots (`P4_few_P_near.png 0 300 700 300`); a crazing net (`P4_old_P_near.png 0 0 700 110`). |
| 4 Board to board | FAIL | Every board has one tone and the same wear (`P4_few_P_far.png 0 0 560 360`). |
| 5 Edges and joints | FAIL | Drop siding's cove and rabbet read as a single drawn dark line, not a profile in shadow (`P4_few_P_near.png 0 300 1400 30`). |
| 6 Holds at distance | FAIL | Few at 5 m is a flat card (`P4_few_P_far.png`). At old, the nail streaks make a regular grid of thin vertical lines (`P4_old_P_far.png 120 20 10 340`) over speckle. |

Grades: **few: game**. **old: fake.**

### Set Q

| check | result | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Real stuff | PASS | Buff paint over pine, with a clear figure under the film (`P4_old_Q_near.png 700 330 700 130`). |
| 2 Age reads | FAIL | Old's peel sits along the lower edges of the boards (`P4_old_Q_near.png 0 100 1400 40`), which is right, but the coat is still mostly sound. Few has flecks mid-board. |
| 3 No tile tells | FAIL | Ghost dots and flecks (`P4_few_Q_near.png 0 300 500 400`); a lace crazing net (`P4_old_Q_near.png 500 0 900 110`); nail streaks as rows of orange pins (`P4_old_Q_near.png 580 480 60 280`). |

Grades: **few: game**. **old: game.**

**P vs Q:** Q is better. At old the difference is a full grade (fake to game); at few it is within a grade.
**Best age:** few.
**One change to move it up most:** the same as P1. The coat should fail in strips along the boards' lower edges and ends, down to grey wood, with no evenly scattered flecks, ghost dots or crazing net. Model drop siding's cove so the joint is a shadowed profile, not a line.
