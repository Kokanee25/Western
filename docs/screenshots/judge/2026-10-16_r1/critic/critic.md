# Blind critic, 2026-10-16_r1

1. **The road is flat, with ruts painted on rather than dug in** (road). The road painting's ruts
   are real troughs: each has a raised, sunlit crest of churned earth on one side and a deep
   shadow in the groove, and ridges and clods run alongside, so the low sun picks out the shape
   of the whole street. In the render the ruts are thin dark stripes on a smooth sheet of dirt.
   They throw no shadows and catch no rim light, and up close the surface melts into a blurred,
   photo-like grain instead of crisp squares. Low and looking down the road, it reads as a
   decal on a floor, not dirt that wagons have chewed up. Crop: `critic/1_road_relief.png`
2. **The stones are flat dark lozenges, scattered evenly** (road). The painting's stones are
   pale, rounded, sunlit pebbles and clods, each with a bright top and a small cast shadow.
   They sit in clusters along the rut edges and in a gravelly band near the boardwalks. The
   render's stones are low-poly flattened ovals sprinkled evenly over the whole road. In the
   golden-hour view many of them are near-black discs that look like holes or puddles. At noon
   they turn bright white and look like scattered eggs. Crop: `critic/2_road_stones.png`
3. **The saloon's people are stiff mannequins standing in a knot** (saloon). The painting's room
   is full of life spread through its depth: card players hunched over the felt, a man at the
   piano, a drinker leaning on the bar, a man on the balcony, a bartender, all with textured
   clothes and faces drawn in the same squares. The render puts four men in the middle of the
   floor with their backs turned, arms hanging straight, smooth flat shading and pale cut-out
   collars. The red-vest man's shirt has a jagged hole of colour. They look like placed props,
   not patrons. Crop: `critic/3_saloon_crowd.png`
4. **The golden-hour sky is mauve and grey, cut into hard-edged cloud shapes** (street). The
   painting has a clear blue sky above with soft, warm, sunlit clouds broken into chunky
   squares. Low down, a white-hot sun throws rays through a glowing haze over the buttes. The
   render fills the whole sky with flat lilac-grey and peach shapes with sharp vector edges, a
   bit like a poster. The sun is a pale, washed-out disc with no glare or rays, so the scene
   feels dusky and bruised instead of blazing. Crop: `critic/4_street_sky.png`
5. **The card table is a checkerboard of big orange tiles** (saloon). In the painting the table
   is fine wood grain running in curved planks, drawn in small squares, with the lamp's glow
   pooling around its base. In the render the tabletop is a grid of large flat squares in
   alternating oranges, like a tiled floor. The lamp base, mugs and ashtray are smooth faceted
   low-poly shapes with clean shading, not the painting's squared-off, textured metal. This
   foreground takes up a third of the frame, so it is what the viewer sees first. Crop:
   `critic/5_saloon_table.png`
