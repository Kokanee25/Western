# Materials judge, round 2026-11-11_paint_r3i (blind)

Rubric: `docs/concept/judge/MATERIAL.md` (painted wood). Yardstick: RDR2 street noon, street golden,
saloon night; Hunt 1896 street noon, saloon night; KCD2 street noon. I looked only at their wood:
RDR2's saloon woodwork and Hunt's grey boards have a body to them. Every surface carries uneven wear,
dirt that collects where it would, soft sheen on the edges and real depth in the joints. Nothing in
them looks clean or printed.

Crop boxes are `x y w h` in the named crop's own pixels.

**The known light fault, noted once:** a dashed light line runs inside the bead joints (worst in
P6_old_P_near `700 150 60 250` and P6_old_Q_near `690 150 80 260`, faint in P5_few_Q, P5_old_P,
P5_old_Q and P6_few_Q). It looks like the saw-toothed edge of the sun's shadow along the laps, so it
did not fail a check or lower a grade.

What is common to all eight: the boards are narrow, beaded V-jointed pine, and the joints and bead
relief read well. At noon the coat is a matte, even film with the grain raised through it, and up
close it looks like wood-grain-embossed vinyl or fibre-cement more than oil paint on planed pine.
The paint has no sheen, no brush or edge build-up, and no film edge anywhere. Under lamplight every
swatch turns into what looks like stained or varnished pine, because the grain figure shows through
as colour, not only as relief. In the "old" crops this is strong.

**P vs Q (P5):** Q is better. At few years they are equal for practical purposes, and Q is
marginally livelier. At old, Q is the only set whose age reads. The difference is within a grade,
since all four are "game".

**Best age:** few years (both sets).

**The one change that would move P5 up most:** make the coat opaque. Take the grain figure's
colour out from under the paint, keep only a faint raised-grain relief, and give the film a low
sheen with slight build-up at the arrises. That alone stops the noon vinyl look, the night
varnished-pine look and the bare-pine look at 5 m. After that, old needs its wear indoors: a rub
band at hand height, scuffs low and a few real lifted film edges in place of the orange dots.
