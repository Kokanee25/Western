# Blind verdict: the moment of firing (own_smoke_r1)

## 50 ms (firing_005)
- **Colt day:** a tiny white-pink star at the muzzle, about the size of the front sight. No fireball, no smoke. Reads as a spark, not a black-powder discharge.
- **Colt night:** a thin red-orange horizontal streak with a white core, plus a small yellow lick by the cylinder. Thin and flat, like a lens flare line. Doesn't light the hand or ground.
- **Winchester day:** almost nothing: a faint pinkish sliver behind the front sight. Hidden by the gun.
- **Winchester night:** a short red diagonal streak by the sight. It reads as a stray line, not flame.
- No smoke in any frame.

## 150 ms (firing_015)
- **Colt day:** a faint blue-grey ring of blocky squares round the muzzle, mostly see-through. It's there, but it's a halo the size of the gun, not a cloud. Bluish rather than dirty white.
- **Colt night:** a tan/peach blob of hard-edged squares around the gun, uniformly lit, flat, with a sharp outer edge. It reads as a pixel sprite or glow disc, not smoke (it looks like it's lit from inside, or by the flash staying on).
- **Winchester day:** faint grey squares above the receiver, barely visible.
- **Winchester night:** same peach square blob as the Colt, smaller.
- Shape is a ring centred on the gun; nothing drifts forward down the line of fire, no volume or billowing.

## Grade: cheap indie (bordering on placeholder)

## Top 3 problems
1. **Smoke far too small and too faint.** Black powder should throw out a large dirty-white cloud in front of the muzzle, a metre or more, hiding the target briefly. Here it's a see-through halo the size of the gun.
2. **Night smoke is a flat, glowing peach blob of hard squares.** Same brightness throughout, sharp edge, no shading: it reads as a sprite, not lit smoke. It should be dark grey, lit only where the flash catches it, and fade out.
3. **The flash is a thin streak or spark.** It needs a short, bright orange-yellow cone of flame and sparks out of the barrel, big enough to light the hand, gun and ground for a frame (most of all at night). On the Winchester it's hidden behind the sight.
