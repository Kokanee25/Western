## Grade
**Cheap indie.** The smoke timing exists, but the flash is near-invisible and the night smoke is glitchy.

## Top 3 problems
1. **Night smoke glows like a sun disc:** it's cream-yellow, overbright and has square-stepped edges. Smoke should take lamp light dimly (dark grey-brown with a warm side) and shouldn't look emissive.
2. **The flash is far too small for black powder:** at 50 ms there should be a big orange-white fireball and sparks with a cone forward, plus a light pulse on the hands and ground. Right now it's a dot hidden behind the sight, and on the rifle it's essentially absent.
3. **The smoke is a flat, screen-facing circle centred on the muzzle:** it needs volume and a forward push (downrange, drifting up and off-axis), several overlapping puffs of uneven density, and a neutral grey-white colour by day instead of blue-grey or soot.
