The painting's man sits slumped at the table in a wide-brimmed hat and long brown coat, both hands low round his cup, looking up at us, weary and wary. The picture's quiet, tired stranger reads instead as someone mid-toast or stumbling, and the frame loses its focus. Crop: `critic/1_table_man.png`

The painting's back bar is warm dark wood: pillars, cubbies, a gilt-framed mirror, lamps along it, the bottles glowing in gold light. The room's richest corner looks like a stockroom, and the whole right side goes pale and empty where the painting is darkest and warmest. Crop: `critic/2_back_bar.png`

The painting has a barn-red saloon with a big cream sign in red lettering, grey-white store fronts, green sage, a blue sky and a chestnut horse, all held together by the golden light. The town loses its landmark and its sense of place. Crop: `critic/3_saloon_front.png`
4. **The end of the street is a canyon under heavy clouds, not a low sun over the town** (street). The painting looks down the road to a big white sun disc sitting low over hazy rooftops, with a water tower and bright, sunlit cumulus piled up above. The painting's warm, open sunset becomes a darker, more closed-in and dramatic mood, and the light has no visible source. Crop: `critic/4_street_end.png`

It is the nearest surface to the eye, so the noise stands out against the smoother men and the lamp. Crop: `critic/5_table_top.png`
