# Materials judge: q3_F2 (black walnut, narrow tongue and groove, varnished)

Blind judgment of the crops in this folder against the yardstick's wood (RDR2 street and saloon,
Hunt 1896, KCD2). Crop boxes are `x y w h` in the named crop's own pixels.

Noted once, not graded: the sun's shadow of the rail in every `noon_rail` crop has a saw-toothed
edge (e.g. `F2_few_P_noon_rail.png` `660 270 1060 40`). A fault of the light, not the material.

Common to all four (said once): nothing in any crop reads as *varnished*. There is no sheen, no
highlight rolling over the boards by lamplight or at noon, so the surface reads as a flat printed
stain. The colour is a red-brown by day and a bright orange at golden hour, closer to cherry or
stained pine than black walnut (the yardstick's saloon woodwork is browner and carries a soft
gloss). And the panel is made of two visibly different kinds of board: coarse, open cathedral
grain, and very smooth boards with soft wavy lines that read as a digital painting of wood.

## P against Q

**P is better, within a grade** (both are "game"). P has no horizontal band at either distance and
its ages can be told apart at 1 m; Q carries a straight band across the boards in both ages at
5 m (and faintly at 1 m when old) and its two ages are nearly the same. Q's one advantage is at
1 m when a few years old: more of its boards have the coarse cathedral grain, which reads more
like wood than P's many smooth boards.

## The one change that would move each up most

- **F2 (both ages, both sets):** make the varnish show. A real gloss over the grain (highlights
  by lamplight and at noon, dulled where worn) and a browner, less orange walnut would do more
  than anything else; then replace the smooth wavy-line boards with boards of real figure.
- **F2_few:** in Q, remove the horizontal band. Best age of the swatch: **few, set P**.
- **F2_old:** age it by the varnish, not by a crackle net: worn-through and dulled patches,
  dirt low and in the grooves, tread wear on the floor; in Q, remove the band and line.
