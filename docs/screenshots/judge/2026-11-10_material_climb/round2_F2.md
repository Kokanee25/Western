# Materials judge: F2 (Black walnut, narrow tongue and groove, varnished)

Blind judgment of the crops in `build/judge/q2_F2` against the yardstick (RDR2, Hunt 1896, KCD2
official screenshots; their wood only). Crop boxes are `x y w h` in the named crop.

Seen in every swatch and set (said once):

- The sun's shadow on the floor in every `noon_rail` crop has a saw-toothed edge (e.g.
  `F2_new_P_noon_rail.png` 660 270 1080 110). A known fault of the light; it fails nothing here.
- The two sets differ in one thing a viewer sees: how strongly the grooves between the narrow
  boards are cut and shadowed. One set has clear dark V-grooves, the other has them nearly gone.
  Which letter is which changes per swatch (new: Q grooved; few: P grooved; old: P grooved).
- No set shows a varnish: no sheen, no highlight rolling over the boards at noon or by lamplight.
  The yardstick's saloon woodwork (RDR2 `saloon_night_1`) carries its finish in the highlights.
- By lamplight every set goes to one deep red with the figure only as a darker red; that colour is
  the light's and is not graded, but the wood gives the light nothing to catch.

**Better set: Q**, within a grade (it reads as boards on a wall; P reads as a sheet).

**The one change that would move F2_new up most:** give each narrow board its own piece of grain
(offset or re-pick the figure per board so nothing runs through a groove), keeping Q's grooves.
Next after that: a varnish highlight and a browner, less orange walnut.

## Ages against each other

New and few cannot be told apart in either set. Old is clearly different at 1 m and is the best
age: its colour and figure are the nearest to walnut and its grooved set is the best crop here.
None reaches the yardstick: the references' wood shows its finish in the highlights, has worn
arrises and dirt where it is used, and no two boards share a figure.
