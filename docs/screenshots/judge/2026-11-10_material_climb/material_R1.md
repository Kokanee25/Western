# Materials judge: qf_R1 (R1, rough-sawn pine, board and batten)

Blind judgment against the yardstick (RDR2 street/saloon, Hunt 1896 street/saloon, KCD2 street; wood
only). Crops judged: near (1 m, noon) and far (5 m, golden hour) for each age and set. Crop boxes
are `x y w h` in the named crop.

Noted once, not counted: the saw-toothed edge of the sun's shadow along the batten gaps (e.g.
`R1_new_P_near.png` 95 0 130 780 and 450 0 40 780; `R1_few_Q_near.png` 0 0 150 780). A fault of the
light, not the material.

One thing applies to every Q crop and is not a rubric check on its own: the battens hardly read as
battens. In Q the wall looks like plain butted planks with dark gaps; in P the battens read as
separate strips (too plain, but there). It is counted below only where it shows as an edge fault.

| check | result | evidence |

and the grain has to come back before anything else matters.

### Set Q — grade: **near the yardstick**

| check | result | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Real stuff | PASS | Weathered softwood with real checks, lifted fibre, short cross-cuts and nail heads; close to Hunt's grey boards. |
| 2 Age reads | FAIL | `R1_few_Q_near.png` 400 0 700 780: deep checking and full dark greying is decades of weather, not a few years; at 1 m it cannot be told from `R1_old_Q_near.png`. The far crop's unweathered top band (`R1_few_Q_far.png` 0 15 560 90) is the only difference. |
| 3 No tile tells | PASS | None found. |

**P vs Q (few): Q is better by two full grades.**
**The one change that would move it up most:** Q: make it actually a few years old — keep a good
share of the warm pine colour, greying only started and only where the sun and splash reach, with
shallow surface checks instead of deep splits. P: as for new, remove the weave and the orange edge
lines and restore real grain.

### Set Q — grade: **near the yardstick** (high end; the best crop of the twelve)

| check | result | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Real stuff | PASS | Convincing old sun-beaten softwood: long checks, torn fibre, a paler less-weathered board (`R1_old_Q_near.png` 930 0 150 780), rusty nail heads. |

| 3 No tile tells | PASS | None found. |
| 4 Board to board | PASS | The warm board among the grey ones is how real walls look. |

**P vs Q (old): Q is better by two full grades.**
**The one change that would move it up most:** Q: make the battens read — a proud strip with its
own grain, a cast shadow on one side and a worn arris — and lift the grey toward a paler, drier
silver. P: remove the weave and the orange edge lines and restore real grain.

## Across ages

- **Set Q:** new is clearly different from few and old; few and old are the same material at 1 m.

  **new**. All three carry the weave.
- **Q is better than P at every age**: by one full grade at new, by two full grades at few and old.
