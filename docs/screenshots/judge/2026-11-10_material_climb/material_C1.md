# Materials judge: qf_C1 (C1, planed pine lap siding; sets P and Q)

Blind judgment against the yardstick (RDR2 street/saloon, Hunt 1896 street/saloon, KCD2 street:
their wood only). Crop boxes are `x y w h` in the named crop. Only the near (1 m, noon) and far
(5 m, golden hour) crops were given; no saloon_night or noon_rail crops for this swatch.

Noted once: the saw-toothed edge of the sun's shadow along the laps shows in every crop (strongest
in set Q, e.g. `C1_new_Q_near.png` 0 235 1400 30). It is the light's fault and has failed no check
and lowered no grade.

Also noted: the far crops of the "few" age carry a band of fresh orange wood across the top
(`C1_few_P_far.png` 0 0 560 60, `C1_few_Q_far.png` 0 0 560 90). I have read it as a neighbour in
the frame and not graded it; if it belongs to the swatch it is a hard tone break.

## C1_new

### Set P: grade **game**

| check | result | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Real stuff | FAIL | `C1_new_P_near.png` 0 250 900 240: the cathedral figure is too bold, too wide and too high in contrast for planed pine; it reads as a stock ash/oak veneer or a wood-grain laminate. `C1_new_P_far.png` 0 150 560 120: at 5 m the swirls read as a printed plywood face. |
| 2 Age reads | PASS | Clearly new (clean, yellow, no checks), clearly different from few and old. Too clean even for new: no dust, no handling marks. |
| 3 No tile tells | PASS | No repeat or seam found. The nails sit on a rigid grid of grey-blue dots (`C1_new_P_near.png` 400 30 560 720) but that is fixing, not tiling. |
| 4 Board to board | PASS | Every board has its own figure and a slightly different tone. |
| 5 Edges and joints | PASS | Laps are dark shadowed gaps; shallow (hairlines at y=100, 360, 590) but not drawn on. |
| 6 Holds at distance | PASS | Keeps its character, no shimmer or mush; too busy, but not a card. |

### Set Q: grade **fake**

| check | result | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Real stuff | FAIL | `C1_new_Q_near.png` 0 110 1400 380: flat salmon-peach with almost no grain; reads as painted card or MDF, not pine. |
| 2 Age reads | PASS | Reads as "new" only in the sense of unmarked; it does differ from Q's other ages. |
| 3 No tile tells | FAIL | `C1_new_Q_near.png` 560 110 320 120: a fine woven/gridded mush in place of grain at 1 m; also 980 490 300 80. |
| 4 Board to board | FAIL | `C1_new_Q_near.png` 0 0 1400 590: five boards the same flat tone with no figure; only the sixth (0 600 1400 90) shows grain, so it looks like a different material. |
| 5 Edges and joints | PASS | Laps are soft shadows (the saw-tooth aside). |
| 6 Holds at distance | FAIL | `C1_new_Q_far.png` 0 0 560 360: a flat orange card with dotted lines. |

**Better set: P, by a full grade.**
**One change that would move it up most (P):** calm the figure: finer, lower-contrast, mostly
straight pine grain with a few small knots, so it stops reading as a veneer print.

## C1_few

### Set P: grade **near the yardstick**

| check | result | evidence |

| 1 Real stuff | FAIL | `C1_few_Q_near.png` 0 100 1400 120: flat grey with smudges; reads as grey paint on canvas, no grain. |
| 2 Age reads | FAIL | `C1_few_Q_near.png` 0 100 1400 600 beside `C1_old_Q_near.png` 0 100 1400 600: few and old are the same surface but for rusty nail heads. |

unweathered wood under each lap and less grey overall, and clean out the blue/magenta specks.

## Best age and set

Set P is better at every age. Its best age is **few** (near the yardstick); new and old are
game. Set Q is fake at all three ages: no grain, weave/mush at 1 m, and a coloured band along
every board edge in few and old.
