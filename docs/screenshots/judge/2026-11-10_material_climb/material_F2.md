# Materials judge: F2 (black walnut, narrow tongue and groove, varnished), sets P and Q

Blind judgment against the yardstick's wood (RDR2 saloon rails and floor, RDR2 street boards,
Hunt's hull planks, KCD2's stalls and shingles). Crop boxes are `x y w h` in the named crop.

Noted once: the sun's shadow edge is saw-toothed in every `noon_rail` crop (e.g. 660 270 1080 40).
It is the light's fault and has not failed any check or lowered any grade.

Across both sets: nothing here is at the yardstick. The yardstick's varnished wood (RDR2 saloon
balustrade and floor) has fine pore-level grain, worn pale arrises, scuffs and dust in the grooves,
and a broken, uneven sheen. Set P has crisp but drawn-looking figure and the wrong colour at
distance; set Q has the right dark varnished tone but its grain is smeared away at 1 m.

sharp grain, and replace P's wobbly contour-line figure with fine pore-level walnut grain.

**Better set: Q, within a grade.** Q reads older and its joints and floor are convincing. P's

lamplight, so it does not gain a full grade.

**The one change that would move it up most:** remove P's uniform crackle web, or break it into
patches that follow the grain. Keep Q's dark grimed tone and joints, with enough grain left to
read as wood at 1 m and by lamplight.
