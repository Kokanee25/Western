# Materials judge: q2_R1 (rough-sawn pine, board and batten)

Blind judgment against the yardstick (RDR2 street/saloon, Hunt 1896 street/saloon, KCD2 street; wood only).
Crops seen: near (1 m, noon) and far (5 m, golden hour) for each age and set. No saloon_night or
noon_rail crops were in the folder, so lamplight and rail/floor were not judged.
Crop boxes are `x y w h` in the named crop's own pixels.

Noted once, not counted: the sun's shadow along the open joints is saw-toothed (e.g.
`R1_few_P_near.png` 0 0 160 780; `R1_new_P_near.png` 440 0 60 780). A fault of the light.

P against Q in general: the two sets are almost the same picture. The mean difference is about 3
levels of 255: Q is ~3 lighter on new and few and ~2 darker on old, with board-to-board tone steps
a little stronger in Q and the dark joint lines a little crisper in P. Nothing a player would
notice without flipping between them; no check changes between P and Q on any age.

## R1_new

### Set P
1. Real stuff: PASS. Clearly pine: cathedral figure, early/late wood bands, small pin knots. But the
   face is brushed/planed, with no saw marks; it is not "rough-sawn".
2. Age reads: PASS. Unmistakably new against few and old; clean, yellow, no greying.
3. No tile tells: PASS. No repeat, seam, blocks or weave at 1 m. The nail heads are identical grey
   dots in two ruled rows (hunted tell: 0 255 1400 40).
4. Board to board: PASS. Figure differs board to board (straight grain, cathedral, ring burl);
   tone and width vary a little.
5. Edges and joints: FAIL. Only two joints open as shadowed gaps; the rest butt dead flat with
   no gap, no arris wear and no batten standing proud: `R1_new_P_near.png` 740 0 120 780,
   1000 0 120 780, 1240 0 160 780. For "board and batten" no batten reads as a raised strip.
6. Holds at distance: PASS (narrowly). Keeps its figure with no shimmer, but the figure is so
   loud and the colour so even that it leans toward printed pine panelling: `R1_new_P_far.png`
   400 20 160 340.

Grade: **game**

### Set Q
1. Real stuff: PASS (as P).
2. Age reads: PASS.
3. No tile tells: PASS (same nail-dot rows).
4. Board to board: PASS.
5. Edges and joints: FAIL. Same flat butt joints: `R1_new_Q_near.png` 740 0 120 780,
   1000 0 120 780, 1240 0 160 780. At 5 m two of the joint lines are fainter than P's:
   `R1_new_Q_far.png` 235 20 20 340, 320 20 20 340.
6. Holds at distance: PASS (narrowly, as P).

Grade: **game**

Better set: **P, within a grade** (hair-crisper joint lines at 5 m; otherwise indistinguishable).

The one change that would move it up most: give every joint real depth and put the battens on as
raised strips with a cast shadow and a softened arris; then knock the figure's contrast down and add
saw marks, so it reads as sawn boards and not as panelling.

## R1_few

### Set P
1. Real stuff: PASS. Reads as real weathered softwood: checks, raised grain, torn fibres, pale
   scuffs. Species is no longer legible, as with real grey wood.
2. Age reads: FAIL. At 1 m this is not "a few years": it is deeply checked, grey-black, decades
   old, and almost the same material as R1_old at 1 m (`R1_few_P_near.png` 400 0 600 780 against
   `R1_old_P_near.png` 400 0 600 780). Only the 5 m crop shows a different age, by an orange
   unweathered band under the top edge (`R1_few_P_far.png` 0 15 560 110), and that band is as
   saturated as new wood sitting directly over heavily checked wood.
3. No tile tells: PASS. No repeat, blocks or weave. Some softness (slightly smeared detail) at
   1 m in the right half, 900 0 500 780, short of mush.
4. Board to board: FAIL. The right half is one continuous sheet about four boards wide with no
   board identity: `R1_few_P_near.png` 760 0 640 780. The left boards differ only by their gaps.
5. Edges and joints: PASS. The three joints that exist are true shadowed gaps with ragged edges
   (360 0 60 780; 700 0 70 780). No drawn lines.
6. Holds at distance: PASS. At 5 m it keeps checks, knots and scuffs with no shimmer or card.

Grade: **game**

### Set Q
1. Real stuff: PASS.
2. Age reads: FAIL. Same: `R1_few_Q_near.png` 400 0 600 780 reads as old; the orange band
   `R1_few_Q_far.png` 0 15 560 110.
3. No tile tells: PASS.
4. Board to board: FAIL. `R1_few_Q_near.png` 760 0 640 780: still one sheet. Q's tone steps are
   a touch stronger but not enough to make boards.
5. Edges and joints: PASS.
6. Holds at distance: PASS.

Grade: **game**

Better set: **no visible difference** (Q about 2 levels lighter; within a grade, a coin toss).

The one change that would move it up most: make it actually a few years old at 1 m (warm brown
still showing through a thin silver-grey, shallow checks only, dirt splash low), so it sits clearly
between new and old; today it is "old" twice.

## R1_old

### Set P
1. Real stuff: PASS. The most convincing surface of the three: deep checks, eroded soft grain,
   lichen-pale scuffs, rusted nail heads.
2. Age reads: PASS. Clearly old and desert-believable; rust on the nails is a good touch. (It does
   not separate from R1_few, but that is few's fault.) One bright orange unweathered strip at 5 m
   reads as a new batten on an old wall: `R1_old_P_far.png` 48 20 22 340; believable as a repair,
   but it is the first thing the eye lands on.
3. No tile tells: PASS. No repeat or blocks. Slight softness at 1 m (1000 0 400 780).
4. Board to board: FAIL. At 1 m the whole crop is one continuous grey sheet; the boards cannot be
   counted: `R1_old_P_near.png` 380 0 1020 780.
5. Edges and joints: FAIL. Almost no joints at 1 m: one hairline at the left
   (`R1_old_P_near.png` 0 150 120 630) and faint lines at 350 0 60 780 and 690 0 60 780; no
   battens standing proud. At 5 m the joints are thin dark lines with no depth
   (`R1_old_P_far.png` 210 20 20 340).
6. Holds at distance: PASS. Keeps knots, checks and blotches at 5 m without shimmer.

Grade: **game**

### Set Q
1. Real stuff: PASS.
2. Age reads: PASS. The fresh strip is brighter still: `R1_old_Q_far.png` 48 20 22 340, and a
   second at 395 20 20 340.
3. No tile tells: PASS.
4. Board to board: PASS (narrowly). Tone now separates boards: a paler board at
   `R1_old_Q_near.png` 900 0 160 780 and darker ones at 150 0 200 780 and 1050 0 200 780. They
   read as boards more than stripes, though the pale one is close to a tint band.
5. Edges and joints: FAIL. The boards Q separates by tone still meet with no gap or arris:
   `R1_old_Q_near.png` 880 0 60 780, 1040 0 60 780.
6. Holds at distance: PASS.

Grade: **game** (the top of it)

Better set: **Q, within a grade** (boards can be told apart at 1 m; one check better, same grade).

The one change that would move it up most: open the joints. Old shrunken boards should show
dark gaps of uneven width, with battens proud of the face, split and lifting; with that, Q's old
would be near the yardstick.

## Best age

R1_old (set Q) is the best of the swatch; R1_few is the weakest because it has no age of its own.
