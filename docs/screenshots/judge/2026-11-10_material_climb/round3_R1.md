# Materials judge: q3_R1 (rough-sawn pine, board and batten)

Blind judgment of eight crops against the yardstick's wood (RDR2 street noon / saloon night,
Hunt 1896 street noon / saloon night, KCD2 street noon; RDR2 street golden shows no wood to
speak of). Boxes are `x y w h` in the named crop. Only near (1 m, noon) and far (5 m, golden
hour) crops were given; no saloon_night or noon_rail crops, so lamplight and rail/floor are unjudged.

Noted once, not graded: the saw-toothed edge of the sun's shadow down the left batten in the
near crops (e.g. R1_few_P_near `20 60 130 720`, R1_old_Q_near `10 60 140 720`).

## The one change that would move each up most

- **R1_few:** make it actually a few years old at 1 m: keep warm pine in the body of the board with
  greying only on the sun side and edges, shallow checks instead of deep splits. That alone
  would lift P a grade. (Q also needs the pale knot-patch broken up.)
- **R1_old:** P: give the joints depth at 1 m (the batten's shadowed step); Q: stop the pale knot
  patch recurring.

Best age: **old** in both sets.
