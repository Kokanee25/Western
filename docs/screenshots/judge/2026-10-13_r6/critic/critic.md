1. **The saloon is drowned in one orange haze** (saloon). In the painting the room has real darks: deep brown shadows between beams, warm pools only around each lamp, and a cool blue night outside the door with a moonlit building and a horse. The render is one even sepia fog from floor to rafters: walls, ceiling and people all sit in the same mid-orange, the shadows are lifted, and the doorway shows a flat grey wall slab under a streaky blue sky instead of a town at night. It reads as smoky daylight, not a lamplit room at night. Crop: `critic/1_haze.png`
2. **The road is loud static, not dusty ground** (street). The painting's road is a soft, low-contrast tan with gentle wheel ruts, scattered darker pebbles and long porch shadows across it. The render's road is a high-contrast speckle of bright orange and near-black squares at full strength right to the horizon, with no ruts and no shadow shapes; it pulls the eye to the bottom half of the frame and looks like TV noise or a leopard-print carpet. He reads as surprised and waxy, not world-weary. Crop: `critic/3_face.png`
