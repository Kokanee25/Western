# Materials judge: paint round 1, P6 (pale grey-green interior paint on narrow planed pine)

Blind judge, 2026-11-11. Rubric `docs/concept/judge/MATERIAL.md` (painted wood). Yardstick: RDR2
(street noon, golden, saloon night), Hunt 1896 (street noon, saloon night), KCD2 (street noon),
wood only. Crop boxes are `x y w h` in the named crop's own pixels.

The sun's saw-toothed shadow edge along battens: not seen in these crops (a ceiling of beadboard).
The paint's hue at 5 m golden hour and at lamplight is the light's colour, not judged.

What the yardstick's wood has that P6 lacks: RDR2's saloon woodwork and Hunt's walls carry a
coat that is uneven in a believable way: soiled and darker where hands, smoke and lamp heat go,
dulled flat in the field and polished on arrises, with joints that sit in real shadow of varying
depth. Every P6 crop has the same even coat over the whole field.

| 5. Edges and joints | FAIL | Each bead ends in a crisp hairline, the same width and darkness on every joint: drawn, not a shadowed groove. Worst at night: uniform black lines (night 540 0 30 780, 640 0 30 780). |

**Grade: game.** Reads as a painted beadboard ceiling; the grit, the ruled joint lines and the
flat card at 5 m give it away.
**The one change:** take the sandy grit out of the planed boards (a smooth film with only the
faint raised grain under it), which removes the most visible tell at 1 m.

lifted film edges, rubbed through where hands go. Cut the flecks to a few, and only where a nail
would be.

## The set (new, a few years, old)

**Age reads: FAIL.** New and a few years are nearly the same at 1 m and identical at 5 m. Old
differs, but by flecks and grain, not by the paint ageing. **Best age: new.**
