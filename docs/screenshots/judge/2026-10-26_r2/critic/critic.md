# Blind critic, 2026-10-26_r2

1. **The saloon is near-black with blown-out lamp blobs** (saloon). The painting's room is lit all over in warm honey-gold: every post, rail, ceiling beam and back wall reads, and each wall lamp is a small bright flame inside clear glass with a soft halo. In the render most of the frame is black or deep maroon, the two wall lamps top right turn into big overexposed white-orange smears that wash out the wall, and the balcony, stair and ceiling are only shapes against dark. A viewer gets "dark cave with a floodlight", not the snug, glowing, busy saloon. Crop: `critic/1_room_black.png`
2. **The street's buildings are dark silhouettes, not sunlit painted fronts** (street). The painting has the light on the facades: a red-painted saloon with a cream sign board and big red lettering, a pale grey general store and livery with crisp lettered signs, blue sky between the clouds. The render's fronts are all the same dark brown weathered wood in shadow, the SALOON sign is dark letters on dark board and GENERAL STORE is barely legible, and the sky is all orange with no blue. The street feels heavy and gloomy instead of bright, colourful and welcoming. Crop: `critic/2_facades_dark.png`
3. **The man at the table is the wrong man and reads broken** (saloon). The painting's man sits low at the table under a big brown hat, in a tan coat, looking at us, hand round his cup, the clear centre of the picture. The render's man is hatless, oversized and too close, his head cut off by the top of the frame, half standing in a black coat, and both his sleeves show hard white patches (the shirt pushing through the coat) while his hands are dark with orange fingers like gloves torn open. The eye goes to the faults, not to him. Crop: `critic/6_card_man.png`
4. **The bar front is pale bleached planks with giant knots** (saloon). In the painting the bar is dark warm panelled wood with mouldings, lit gold by the table lamp. The render's bar front is grey-white rough boards with oversized dark knots, much lighter than everything round it, so it reads like a fence or crate pasted into a dark room; the bottle beside it is a flat black shape and the lamp's glass is a milky white tube with no flame detail or halo. Crop: `critic/4_bar_front.png`
5. **The street ends in a wall of canyon cliffs, not open country under a low sun** (street). The painting looks out past the town to low hills, trees, a small house, a wagon and a big pale sun disc on the horizon, with the water tower and windmill on the skyline: open, airy, golden haze. The render closes the street with tall rust-coloured rock spires and rays bursting from behind them, and the far buildings and people are murky brown in a haze. It feels boxed-in and dramatic rather than the painting's calm open evening. Crop: `critic/5_street_end.png`

**What's closest:** the street's golden-hour sky with its painted pink and orange clouds, and the long raking shadows down a rutted dirt road with horses at the rails, already carry the painting's time of day; in the saloon the table lamp on the round table and the green card table with men at the left are placed as in the painting.
