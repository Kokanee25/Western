- **Face:** A and B differ in 4 pixels, on the panel's bottom edge.
- **Collar:** A and B differ in about 400 of 300,000 pixels (0.14 %). All of them are in the

I checked this by subtracting the two panels and enlarging that strip 4×. At any normal size the
difference can't be seen, so the two panels get the same scores. Where one panel is "best" and the
other "worst" below, the note describes the same picture both ways.

cream points either side of the dark knot. Their edges step in clean saw-teeth, and the shadow
under them is a clear band. In A and B the cream at the lower left and lower middle is a jumble of
pale polygons of different sizes. There are no points, and no edge to show where collar ends and
shirt begins.

**Worst (A = B), biggest flaw:**

- The coat on the right is drawn as long slanted streaks, not the master's tweed of even squares.
- The dark neckerchief at the bottom left breaks into scattered grey-brown blocks, not one solid
  mass.

The neck's skin squares in the upper half are fairly even. They follow the jaw's curve as a
believable underside, with a dark contact line under the jaw. That part is closest to the master.

**Best (A = B) against the master: the eyes.** The master's eyes are drawn finer than the squares
around them: an almond with a lid line, some white either side of a brown iris, and a soft catch
light. In A and B each eye is a dark slot under the brow with a hard white star-shaped glint. Very
little white shows, and there is no lid shape. They read as glints in a shadow, not as drawn eyes.

**Worst (A = B), biggest flaw: the lamp-side face is washed out.** On the cheek and forehead (the
centre and left of the panel) the squares go pale peach with low contrast. The master's skin is a
warm mid-tone mosaic. Other faults:

- The whole square grid is tilted with the head, so the rows run on a slant.
- The brim's edge at the top is stretched into horizontal streaks.
- The ear on the right is a plain orange lit wedge, with no rim or hollow.

What reads well:

- The moustache is two dark masses with a clear notch of skin at the middle of the upper lip.
- The moustache's ends hang down past the mouth.
- The chin and jaw read as a solid form.

The moustache's edges are blockier than the master's, which steps in finer squares. The lower lip
is a bright pink band, where the master's is muted.

## Final ranking

1. **A (tied):** at any normal size it is the same picture as B. Good: the moustache with its
   middle gap, and the jaw's form. Behind the master: the eyes are glint-slots, the lit cheek is
   washed out, the collar has no points and the coat is in streaks.
2. **B (tied):** the same reasons as A. Its only differences are a few pixels in the neckerchief
   on the collar panel's bottom edge, which don't change any score.
