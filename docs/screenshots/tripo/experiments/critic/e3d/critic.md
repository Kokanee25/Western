# Blind critic: e3d

Scores 1 (far from the master) to 5 (as the master). Columns: Sq = square regularity, Cr =
crispness, 3D = 3D form, Rd = semantic readability, St = resemblance to the master's style.

The three panels are identical, or very nearly, everywhere except the moustache. That's in the
nose_moustache, face and coat crops (the "coat" crop shows the chin, moustache and collar more
than the coat). The eyes, ear, collar and hat differ only by a few pixels, so they get the same
scores, with small differences noted where I could see them.

Best (B) against the master: B's moustache reads as bold squares like the master's, but it's a
flat, near-black cut-out. The master's moustache is a dark brown mass mottled with 2-3 tones that
droops past the mouth corners, so it sits on the face, not on top of it. B's also stops short and
square at the mouth corners. On the rest of B's face the squares are skewed and fragmented across
the cheeks (the left cheek and the forehead under the brim), where the master's are a steady,
nearly upright grid.

Worst (A): the moustache (centre, under the nose) is a soft, blurred brown smear with no square
structure and a fuzzy lower edge. It reads as a smudge, not a drawn mass. The cheeks are soft
patches of skewed squares.

Best (B) against the master:
- B is the only panel with real moustache masses: two near-black blocks with stepped outlines and
  a clear light gap of skin under the nose (the philtrum), plus dark nostril pockets above it.
- But B's gap is a tall, bright slot running through the whole moustache to the lip. It splits
  the moustache into two separate slabs, where the master's gap is a small notch at the top
  centre and the two halves stay joined below it.
- B's moustache is one flat black tone. The master's has mottled brown squares with darker edges,
  which make it a soft 3D mass. B's reads as a sticker; that's why its 3D score is low.

Middle (C): a dark brown moustache made of visible squares, with a little tonal variety (closer
to the master's colour than B). Its philtrum gap is only a slightly lighter column, barely
readable. Its outline steps in squares but is softer than the master's.

Worst (A):
- The moustache is a soft, blurry brown blob with a ragged, noisy top edge.
- A few stray light skin squares float inside it, left of centre, where the gap should be: a
  broken patch rather than a drawn gap.
- The nostrils are a smeared dark streak along the bottom of the nose.
- The lower edge melts into the lip.
- No square drawing at all on the main feature.

## Final ranking over all features

1. **B**: the only one whose moustache reads in the master's language (bold stepped masses, a
   philtrum gap, nostrils). But it's flat black, the gap is far too tall and splits it in two,
   and the eye whites are a little more fragmented.
2. **C**: a moustache in visible squares with a tone near the master's and some 3D form, but its
   gap and edges are too soft to read.
3. **A**: a soft, blurred moustache smear with stray skin squares in it and smeared nostrils. On
   the feature that sets these panels apart, it's the furthest from the master's crisp, authored
   squares.

Common to all three, and bigger than any difference between them:
- the skewed, fragmented squares on the cheeks;
- the shapeless collar;
- the soft hat band with an aliased brim;
- the ear as a lit wedge;
- eyes drawn as dark slots with white chips.
