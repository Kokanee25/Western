# Blind critic, e7

Panels A and B match closely everywhere except the eyes (and so the face crop's eye area). Where
they don't differ to the eye, they get the same scores.

## eyes.png

| Panel | Square regularity | Crispness | 3D form | Semantic readability | Master's style |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 3 | 4 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| B | 2 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 |

- **Best (B) against the master:** the master draws each eye as a closed almond: a dark upper lid
  line, then white on both sides of a brown iris, with a glint. B's eyes are dark almond slots under
  the lids, with only a 1–2 px glint and stray off-white specks at the corners. On the viewer's-right
  eye, a cross-shaped speck at the left corner and a starburst at the lower right sit outside the
  slot. The white of the eye never reads as a band either side of the iris.
- **Worst (A), its biggest flaw:** the eyes are drawn as big, block-sized white squares dropped onto
  a black mass. On each eye there's a large pure-white block in the middle of the iris (a glint as big
  as an iris), plus pale blocks at the lower-right corner, below the slot. They read as confetti or
  "googly" highlights, not as the white of the eye. Nothing outlines an almond, so you can't tell lid
  from iris from white. The block-scale drawing matches the face's square grid, but it loses the
  master's finer drawing of the eye.

## Final ranking over all features

1. **B:** its eyes are the more readable of the two: dark almond slots with a small glint, nearer
   the master's finer drawing of the eye. But the whites are still only specks, and stray light
   specks float outside the slot on the right eye. Everything else is the same as A.
2. **A:** its block-scale eyes fit the face's square grid but read as big white confetti glints, not
   eyes: the white of the eye, iris and lid can't be told apart. Everything else is the same as B.

Shared by both, and bigger than the difference between them: the ear has no rim or hollow; the
collar has no pointed wings; the hat's brim edge is streaked, not in squares; the moustache is a flat
stencil with notches, not a rounded mass. None of the eyes has the master's band of white either
side of a brown iris.
