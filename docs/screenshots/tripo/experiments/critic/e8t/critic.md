# Blind critic: e8t (one feature, the ear)

## Ear

| Panel | Square regularity | Crispness | 3D form | Semantic readability | Master's style |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 2 | 3 | 3 | 2 | 2 |
| B | 2 | 2 | 3 | 1 | 2 |
| C | 2 | 3 | 3 | 2 | 2 |

What the master does: the ear is a lit outer rim curling round the top and down the left. Inside the
rim there is a dark hollow, and dark stroke-clusters mark the inner ridge. The hair is a dark mass
behind it. The squares are an even size on a steady grid, so every edge (the rim's outline, the
hollow's opening) steps in square units.

All three renders draw the ear as a narrow lit wedge pointing up. They are built from slanted,
triangular and trapezoidal patches rather than squares. There is a dark-red triangle at mid-height,
a pale diagonal sliver to its left and a large pale round blob near the bottom. None shows a rim
curling round a hollow. A and C are nearly the same image; B differs from both in the top half.

**Best (C) against the master:** the master draws a continuous lit rim round a dark hollow. C has
only a short dark slot near the top centre and a near-white diagonal sliver at the left of mid-height.
The sliver reads as a glint, not as the rim. Below that, the slanted, wedge-shaped patches don't
describe an ear's parts at all. C edges out A because its top-left edge is a slightly lighter rim
against the dark slot, and its highlight is crisper.

**Worst (B):** at the top of the wedge, where A and C have a dark slot, B has none. The top half is a
few large, soft, slanted orange blocks with only the dark-red triangle at mid-height in them. You
cannot find a rim or a hollow, and the blocks read as averaged mud rather than drawn squares.

## Final ranking (all features)

1. **C**: the crispest of the three, with the clearest (though still small) dark hollow at the top
   and a lit edge beside it. Its squares are still slanted triangles, not a grid, and it has no
   readable rim.
2. **A**: almost identical to C. Its top is a little more broken and darker, so the hollow and rim
   separate a shade less clearly, and its highlight is duller.
3. **B**: larger, softer, muddier blocks. It has lost the top hollow, so it is the hardest of the
   three to read as an ear.
