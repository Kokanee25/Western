# Blind critic: two renders against the master's style

Scores run from 1 (far from the master) to 5 (as the master). Columns: Sq = square regularity, Cr = crispness, 3D = 3D form, Read = semantic readability, Style = resemblance to the master's style.

## Face

| Panel | Sq | Cr | 3D | Read | Style |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 2 | 3 | 4 | 3 | 3 |
| B | 3 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 2 |

**Best (A) vs master:** the master's squares are steady and the same size everywhere, and its moustache is outlined in hard dark steps. A's squares lean and stretch with the surface: you can see it in the long slanted blocks on the right cheek and temple. A's moustache is also a soft dark smear with no outline and no gap of skin at the middle of the lip.

**Worst (B), biggest flaw:** the lower half goes to mush. The moustache and mouth (bottom centre-left) are blurred dark and red blocks with no edge. The cheek squares are uneven patches of mixed sizes, so the face reads flatter.

## Eyes

| Panel | Sq | Cr | 3D | Read | Style |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 2 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 2 |
| B | 2 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 2 |

**Best (A) vs master:** the master draws each eye as one clean white wedge on either side of a brown iris, with a soft lid line above it. In A the white is broken into scattered star-shaped specks, with a cross-shaped glint on the iris and lone white pixels at each corner. They look like sparkles, not a white of the eye. The brows are bold blocks, but their edges are slanted and frayed.

**Worst (B), biggest flaw:** both eyes are narrow black slits with sparkle specks at the corners (centre and left of the panel). The brow above the right-hand eye is a heavy horizontal smear that merges with the hat's shadow. The forehead between the eyes is a patchwork of blurry orange blocks.

**Worst (B), biggest flaw:** the moustache and mouth (bottom left) are a blurred dark rectangle smeared into the lip. You can't tell moustache from mouth, and the nose above has no nostril or tip drawn. It is a field of pale blocks.

**Best (A) vs master:** the master's ear has a stepped rim around a darker hollow and inner fold, and it sits in mid skin tone. **Worst (B), biggest flaw:** the ear is a thin, overexposed orange strip on the panel's left edge with one red blob. It is cut off by a hard vertical black edge (the hair or background, centre), so nothing reads as an ear.

**Worst (B), biggest flaw:** the collar along the bottom is an even softer white blotch. Its black gaps (lower left and centre) look like noise, not shadow, so the collar, the shirt and the neck can't be told apart.

**Best (B) vs master:** the master's band is a crisp row of small light and dark checker squares along a dark, cleanly stepped brim. B's band (top) has the checker idea, but its blocks are larger and blurred together. Its brim is a pile of horizontal streaks, cut by a hard black horizontal seam (lower centre) and a vertical seam at the right, so it doesn't read as a curved brim.

**Worst (A), biggest flaw:** the brim (left half, diagonal) is drawn as long thin slanted stripes, like wood grain, not squares. The band's blocks above it are soft, blurry blobs, so the checkered band is lost.

**Best (A) vs master:** the master's coat lapel is a bold dark band with a hard stair-stepped edge, running diagonally against the collar and the skin. The eye goes to the chin above, which is in good regular squares but bounded by a smooth, thin, curved outline that isn't in the master's language.

**Worst (B), biggest flaw:** the coat (left and lower right) dissolves into dark smudges and the white collar spray. Nowhere does the cloth show the master's crisp authored squares; only the chin (top) is clearly blocked.

## Final ranking over all features

1. **A**: slightly better overall. Sharper eyes and brows, and a stronger sense of 3D form (cheek planes, the brim's curve). But its squares slant and stretch with the surfaces, and the moustache, collar and ear are soft smears. Its sparkle-speck eye whites and the striped brim are not the master's language.
2. **B**: squares closer to a steady, screen-square grid, and the most master-like hat band. But it is blurrier everywhere that matters (moustache and mouth, ear, collar). It has hard seams across the brim, and the eyes are slits with specks. Overall it reads flatter and less legible.

**Common to both:** neither draws the important features finer and crisper than the squares around them, as the master does. The moustache has no outline or philtrum gap, the eye whites are specks, the ear has no rim or hollow, and the collar points have no cast shadow. The skin and cloth squares are softer and more blended than the master's flat, hard-edged clusters.
