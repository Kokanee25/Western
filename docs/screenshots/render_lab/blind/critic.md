# Blind critic, render lab (2026-10-14)

A fresh sub-agent with only the paintings' description and the three boards in this folder; X and
Y shuffled per board. Key: saloon X = paint light, Y = production; street X = production,
Y = paint light; lab X = paint light, Y = production.

**Saloon board:** Y is a little closer, but it was hard to tell. Y is slightly warmer than X: more
red and amber, less blue. You see it in the face, the hat and the lamplit tabletop, which edge
toward the painting's honey-amber. Everything else is the same: composition, value range (both
have murky dark-brown backgrounds), the faces, how readable the materials are and the square
size. I only found the difference by comparing them closely.

**Street board:** X is a little closer, again hard to tell. X is a touch warmer and more golden on
the road, the facades and the foreground scrub. Y leans slightly cooler and greyer in the dirt.
The painting's road is warm sand, so X wins by a hair. Sky, signage, lighting and square
structure are the same in both.

**Lab board:** Y is a hair warmer in every lamplit and golden-hour shot, most visibly in the near
man's face (more red-orange). The noon facade shots are effectively identical. With no painting to
compare against, warmer leans toward the target. Very hard to tell apart.

Overall, X and Y differ by one small warm/cool shift (a few levels of red against blue). Neither
render differs in structure, values or detail.

**The five biggest differences from the paintings (both renders):**

1. **Saloon, full frame: the room is blocked and dead.** A wall of standing men (the red-vest
   man, dark coats) cuts across the middle of the frame. They stand stiffly, like mannequins. The
   painting opens into depth: the moonlit window, the stairs and balcony, the bar with lamps
   receding, a lively warm haze. In the renders the back of the room is a murky brown with few
   lit mid-values.
2. **Saloon crops: surfaces don't read as the painting's crisp squares.** The wall behind the lamp
   is huge, soft, blurred squares (around 4–5 times the painting's scale). The tabletop is streaky
   horizontal smears, not planks with grain. The lamp and bottle are smooth, low-poly and glossy
   with a plastic hotspot, and the bottle has no label. In the painting, every surface has small,
   crisp, evenly sized squares that shift in shade from one to the next.
3. **Saloon and lab: the faces are blotchy and harsh.** The seated man's face is hard-edged orange
   and black patches. The eyes look wet and glassy, there is a bright white chin and stubble band,
   and the hat is camouflage-like blotches. The painting's face is calm, readable planes: light
   side, shadow side, a clear brow and moustache. In the lab lamp shots the near man's face goes
   red-orange on near-black. His shirt sleeves are flat cream wedges that look blown out and
   untextured.
4. **Street, road: loud, high-contrast static.** The road is a dark/light mottle that stays just as
   noisy all the way to the horizon, and the wheel ruts don't read. The painting's road is soft
   sand with gentle ruts and quiet values. The foreground is also crowded with spiky dry-grass
   clumps, and a large dark plank wall fills the right edge. In the painting that space holds the
   horse at the hitching rail, the livery and people on the boardwalk.
5. **Street, sky and overall colour.** The render's sky is a stormy purple-grey with orange
   streaks, and the sun behind the mesas glows weakly. The whole frame is close to one
   brown-orange colour. The painting has blue sky against a gold sunset and cool, readable shadows
   on the facades, and that warm/cool contrast makes the materials (painted signs, weathered grey
   boards, red brick) stand apart. In the render, the brick and boards under the saloon porch sink
   into one dark red-brown.

(Also: in the lab, the lamplit room shots are nearly black with little warm bounce light. The
painting's interior is full of warm mid-tones.)

**Would a player notice X against Y without a side-by-side?** No. The difference is a slight
warm/cool shift that only shows when the two are compared directly.
